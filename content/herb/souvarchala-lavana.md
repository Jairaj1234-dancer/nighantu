## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Sodium chloride with sodium sulfide
- Sodium sulfate (Na2SO4)
- Iron sulfide (FeS)
- Hydrogen sulfide (H2S - volatile, gives egg-like odor)
- Sodium bisulfate and sodium bisulfite
- Iron compounds (Fe2+, Fe3+)
- [Magnesium](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#magnesium) (trace)
- [Potassium](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#potassium) (trace)
- [Manganese](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#manganese) (trace)

## How does it work?

- Digestive stimulant: Sulfur compounds stimulate bile production in liver and gastric acid secretion, enhancing overall digestive capacity and appetite
- Anti-flatulent: traditionally used to reduce gas accumulation and abdominal heaviness
- Mild anti-anemic: contains iron sulfide; traditionally used as a dietary supplement in Pandu (anemia)
- Srotoshodhana (channel clearing): Light, sharp, and hot qualities clear Kapha-obstructed channels; aromatic sulfur compounds have mild [expectorant](/reference/expectorant/) and decongestant properties

## Recent safety updates

- Maximum daily consumption should not exceed 6 grams; excessive intake may cause sulfide-related GI distress
- Commercial 'black salt' products often use artificial coloring and sulfur additives rather than traditional kiln-firing process; authenticity verification important
- Contraindicated in Raktapitta (bleeding disorders) and hyperacidity when consumed in excess due to heating potency
- Individuals with sulfite allergies should use with caution; hydrogen sulfide content varies by preparation method

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Crystal/powder

**Standard Dosage:** 0.5–2 g as digestive; in formulations: as directed

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals as Deepana-Pachana; with Hingvastak Churna

**Storage:** Airtight container (sulfur compounds are volatile)

**Marker Compounds:** Sodium chloride, Sodium sulfate, Iron sulfide, Hydrogen sulfide (responsible for smell), Trace minerals

**Used in formulations:** Part of Pachana (digestive) formulations; Hingvastak Churna, Lavana Bhaskar Churna
