## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Nano-diamond particles (carbon nanostructures)
- Carbon in various allotropic forms post-calcination
- Trace [silica](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#silica) and alumina from processing
- Organic compounds from rose water and [aloe vera](/nighantu/herb/aloe-vera/) media
- Iron and other trace elements from crucible materials

## How does it work?

- Anti-cancer activity through nanodiamond-mediated immunostimulation: activates natural killer cells, promotes tumor cell apoptosis, reduces angiogenesis, and inhibits metastatic spread
- [Immunomodulatory](/nighantu/reference/immunomodulatory/) action through enhancement of both innate and adaptive immune responses, increasing phagocytic activity and lymphocyte proliferation
- [Rasayana](/nighantu/reference/rasayana/) (rejuvenation) effect through carbon nanostructure-mediated cellular repair and [antioxidant](/nighantu/reference/antioxidant/) activity, supporting tissue regeneration and longevity
- Tridosha balancing through multi-system regulatory effects on Vata (nervous), Pitta (metabolic), and Kapha (structural) functions

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Cancer and tumors (Arbuda): Animal studies showed significant tumor volume reduction, metastasis inhibition, and increased survival in tumor-bearing animals
- Immune deficiency: Immunomodulatory activity confirmed through enhanced NK cell activity and lymphocyte proliferation in preclinical models
- Tuberculosis (Rajayakshma): Anti-inflammatory and immune-enhancing properties provide mechanistic basis, though specific clinical validation for TB is limited

## Recent safety updates

- HIGH SAFETY MARGIN: Murine toxicological assessments show no hepatotoxicity, nephrotoxicity, or genotoxicity at therapeutic equivalent doses when prepared through validated methods
- STABLE HEMATOLOGY: Hematological profiles remained stable in animal studies; histopathological examination showed no cellular inflammation or necrosis
- LIMITED HUMAN DATA: Clinical trials in humans are still limited; most safety data from animal models and pilot studies
- PREPARATION-DEPENDENT: Adverse reactions usually due to improperly prepared or contaminated formulations; may include gastric irritation, metallic taste, or rare hypersensitivity
- EXTREME COST: Very expensive preparation, limiting widespread use and also increasing adulteration/substitution risk in commercial products

**Dosage Forms:** Bhasma

**Standard Dosage:** 15–30 mg (rare and premium)

**Bioavailability:** Carbon nanostructure form post-calcination; unique properties

**Optimal Timing:** With honey or specific formulation vehicle

**Standardized Extract:** Calcined diamond bhasma — rarest bhasma in Rasashastra

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite

**Storage:** Airtight container; premium storage

**Marker Compounds:** Carbon nanostructures, Trace mineral matrix from Bhavana

**Quality Parameters:** Standard bhasma tests; due to extreme cost, authentication is critical

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Honey, ghee, or gold bhasma combination
