## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Calcium carbonate (CaCO3) as predominant constituent
- Calcium oxide (CaO) formed during calcination
- Trace [strontium](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#strontium), [magnesium](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#magnesium), sodium
- Marine-derived trace minerals
- Organic matrix residues from shell protein (conchiolin)

## How does it work?

- Antacid action through direct acid neutralization by calcium carbonate and calcium oxide
- Calcium supplementation via marine-derived bioavailable calcium supporting bone metabolism, muscle contraction, and nerve impulse transmission
- Pitta-shamana (Pitta-pacifying) action through alkaline pH correction and reduction of Pitta-predominant digestive fire excess

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Hyperacidity and GERD (Amlapitta): traditionally used for these conditions. A clinical study reported a reduction in GERD symptoms with lemon-purified [Shankha Bhasma](/herb/shankha-bhasma/).
- Digestive disorders (Agnimandya, Ajirna): traditionally used as a digestive aid and antacid. A high acid neutralization capacity has been reported.
- Calcium deficiency: traditionally used for bone health and Asthi Dhatu nourishment. It is described as containing marine-derived calcium.

## Recent safety updates

- SAFETY: Properly prepared Shankha Bhasma is reported to contain negligible heavy metals and to pass classical safety tests (Varitara, Rekhapurna)
- MILD SIDE EFFECTS: Possible mild constipation or alkaline reflux
- CAUTION: Hypercalcemia is possible with overdosage
- METAL CONTENT: Marine shell-based preparation without intentional toxic metal content; described as carrying lower contamination risk than metallic Bhasmas.
- PURIFICATION METHOD MATTERS: Lemon-purified Shankha Bhasma showed superior clinical efficacy vs sour gruel-purified, indicating preparation quality directly affects outcomes

### Mineral/Elemental Profile

- **Calcium carbonate (CaCO3):** Major component (conch shell)

- **Magnesium (Mg):** Trace
- **Strontium (Sr):** Trace

**Analytical Methods:** XRD, FTIR, ICP-OES

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Bhasma

**Standard Dosage:** 250–500 mg twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Good; calcium carbonate from conch shell with natural shell (conchiolin) matrix

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals for hyperacidity; with meals for calcium supplementation

**Standardized Extract:** Calcined conch shell bhasma

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite

**Storage:** Airtight, dry container

**Marker Compounds:** Calcium carbonate (95%), Calcium phosphate, Trace strontium and magnesium

**Quality Parameters:** Calcium >35%, standard bhasma tests, no marine contaminants

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Honey, warm water, or lemon juice
