## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Capsule, Tablet, Swarasa (fresh juice), [Arka](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/arka/) (distillate), Essential oil, Tea/infusion, Tincture, Soft gel

**Standard Dosage:** 300-600mg standardized extract twice daily; 3-5g dried leaf powder daily; 5-10ml fresh juice; 2-3 cups tea infusion daily

**Bioavailability:** Ocimum tenuiflorum (O. sanctum) contains [eugenol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#eugenol), rosmarinic acid, ursolic acid, and ocimumosides. Eugenol is rapidly absorbed (Tmax ~1 hour) with moderate bioavailability. Rosmarinic acid shows ~1-2% bioavailability but significant first-pass metabolism. Ursolic acid has poor water solubility (~0.4% bioavailability); nanoformulations and phospholipid complexes can enhance absorption 5-10x. SNEDDS formulations improve terpenoid bioavailability.

**Optimal Timing:** Morning on empty stomach for [adaptogenic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/adaptogenic/) effect; with meals for digestive support; as tea throughout the day

**Standardized Extract:** Ethanolic extract standardized to 2.5% ursolic acid; hydroalcoholic extract standardized to 1% eugenol and 2% rosmarinic acid; essential oil (eugenol chemotype) with >60% eugenol

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (dried powder); 3 years (standardized extract capsule/tablet); 2 years (essential oil); 1 year (fresh juice preparation)

**Storage:** Cool, dry place below 25°C, protected from light. Essential oil in amber glass, sealed tightly. Dried herb in airtight containers to preserve volatile content.

**Marker Compounds:** Eugenol, Rosmarinic acid, Ursolic acid, Oleanolic acid, Ocimumosides A and B, [Apigenin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#apigenin), [Luteolin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#luteolin), Beta-caryophyllene

### Extraction Methods

- Hydroalcoholic extraction (60:40 to 80:20 ethanol:water)
- Steam distillation (essential oil, eugenol-rich)
- Aqueous extraction (rosmarinic acid-rich)
- Supercritical CO2 extraction (terpenoid-enriched fraction)
- Standardized ethanolic extract (ursolic acid-enriched)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Ashwagandha](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ashwagandha/) for adaptogenic and anti-stress synergy
- With [Brahmi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/brahmi/) for cognitive enhancement
- With [Guduchi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/guduchi/) for immune modulation
- With Honey as traditional Anupana
- With [Pippali](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/pippali/) for respiratory support
