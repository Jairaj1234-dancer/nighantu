## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (fruit/seed powder), Swarasa (fresh fruit juice), Avaleha (fruit confection), Capsule, Tablet, Phalasava (fruit wine/fermented), Majja (fruit pulp preparation)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g fruit powder twice daily; 20-50ml fresh juice; 5-10g Avaleha daily; 500mg-1g extract capsule

**Bioavailability:** Good oral bioavailability for water-soluble constituents. Ziziphus mauritiana (Indian jujube/Ber) fruit contains vitamin C (well absorbed), [flavonoids](/nighantu/reference/flavonoids/) (moderate absorption), and cyclopeptide [alkaloids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) (variable absorption). Betulinic acid has limited oral bioavailability (~5%) but is enhanced by lipid co-administration. [Polysaccharides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polysaccharides) (jujuban A, B) act as immunomodulators via Peyer's patches. Fruit [pectin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#pectin) aids sustained delivery of co-administered compounds.

**Optimal Timing:** Fruit/juice as snack or with meals for nutritional support. Seed kernel powder at bedtime with warm milk for sleep. Avaleha anytime for general health.

**Standardized Extract:** Fruit extract (10:1) standardized to minimum 2% total flavonoids (as [rutin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#rutin) equivalent) and minimum 1% betulinic acid. Seed extract standardized to minimum 3% jujubosides (for sedative/[anxiolytic](/nighantu/reference/anxiolytic/) indication). Vitamin C content minimum 200mg/100g in fresh fruit preparation.

**Shelf Life:** 2 days (fresh fruit); 2 years (dried fruit powder); 3 years (capsules/tablets); 1 year (preserved juice); 5 years (Phalasava)

**Storage:** Fresh fruit refrigerated (2-8°C). Dried fruit and powder in airtight containers, cool and dry. Phalasava in amber glass at room temperature.

**Marker Compounds:** Betulinic acid, Oleanolic acid, Ursolic acid, Jujuboside A, Jujuboside B, Rutin, [Quercetin](/nighantu/reference/quercetinquercetin/), Ziziphin, Vitamin C, Ceanothic acid

### Extraction Methods

- Fresh juice extraction (cold press)
- Aqueous decoction of dried fruit
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (70:30) for flavonoid enrichment
- Methanol extraction for alkaloid profiling
- Spray-drying of juice for instant powder
- Fermentation for Phalasava

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Ashwagandha](/nighantu/herb/ashwagandha/) for anxiolytic and sleep-promoting synergy
- With [Brahmi](/nighantu/herb/brahmi/) for cognitive support and stress relief
- With [Yashtimadhu](/nighantu/herb/yashtimadhu/) for sore throat and cough
- With [Draksha](/nighantu/herb/draksha/) (raisins) for blood nourishment and general debility
- With [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/) (ghee) for Vata pacification and nourishment
