## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Iron oxide as major constituent (from iron tourmaline)
- [Silicon](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#silicon) dioxide (SiO2) as major constituent
- Aluminium oxide (Al2O3)
- Boron compounds (tourmaline contains boron)
- Sodium, [potassium](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#potassium), [magnesium](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#magnesium) as trace elements
- Sulfur compounds from processing with [Gandhaka](/glossary/concepts-a-m/#gandhaka)

## How does it work?

- [Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/) (rejuvenation): classically regarded as a rejuvenative
- [Vajikarana](/glossary/pharmacology/#vajikarana) ([aphrodisiac](/glossary/pharmacology/#aphrodisiac)) effect through mineral cofactor provision supporting reproductive hormone synthesis and gonadal function
- Classically described as pramehaghna (used in prameha); no mechanism has been established
- Tridosha balancing through complex multi-mineral composition addressing Vata (nervous), Pitta (metabolic), and Kapha (structural) imbalances simultaneously

## Traditional uses

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Diabetes (Prameha): traditionally used for diabetes.
- Anemia: traditionally used for blood deficiency conditions. Physicochemical analysis has reported iron content in the preparation.
- Respiratory disorders (Shwasa, Kasa): traditionally used for asthma and chronic cough.

## Recent safety updates

- PRELIMINARY SAFETY: Single-dose toxicity study in rats showed no adverse effects, but data is limited
- STRICT MEDICAL SUPERVISION: Must be taken only under medical supervision. Overdosage may cause severe gastritis.
- SHORT DURATION USE: Recommended for prescribed dosage for short duration only
- SOURCE STANDARDIZATION: variations between tourmaline sources affect composition and possibly safety. Only black tourmaline (schorl) is traditionally used for medicinal preparation.

## What is it made of?

### Mineral/Elemental Profile

- **Primary component:** Calcined mineral/metal oxide (after Shodhana and Marana)
- **Particle size:** Sub-micron to nanoparticle range
- **Note:** Exact composition depends on source material and preparation method
