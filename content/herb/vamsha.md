## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Vamshalochana (bamboo manna) Churna ([Sitopaladi Churna](/formulation/sitopaladi-churna/)), Capsule, Tablet, Avaleha, Kwatha (decoction of leaves), Svarasa (stem juice)

**Standard Dosage:** Vamshalochana: 250 mg-1 g twice daily; Bamboo shoot/leaf preparation: 3-6 g twice daily; as part of Sitopaladi Churna: 1-3 g with honey

**Optimal Timing:** Traditionally taken after meals with honey in respiratory complaints, or in the morning with warm milk as a general tonic

**Storage:** Vamshalochana in airtight containers, protected from moisture (silica is hygroscopic). Leaf products in airtight, light-resistant containers at 15-25 deg C.

**Marker Compounds:** Silicon dioxide (amorphous), Orientin, Vitexin, Isoorientin, [Isovitexin](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#isovitexin), Choline, p-Coumaric acid, Ferulic acid, [Tricin](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#tricin)

### Synergistic Combinations

- Sitopaladi Churna components: Vamshalochana + [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) + Ela + Twak + Mishri - classical respiratory formula
- [Pravala Pishti](/herb/pravala-pishti/) (coral calcium) - complementary mineral supplementation
- Amalaki - vitamin C enhances silicon absorption
- [Yashtimadhu](/herb/yashtimadhu/) - demulcent synergy for respiratory conditions
