## How does it work?

*The mechanisms below are reported from research on indigo naturalis and its components.*

- Indirubin anti-proliferative CDK/STAT3 inhibition: Studies report that indirubin inhibits cyclin-dependent kinases (CDKs) and STAT3 signaling and reduces keratinocyte proliferation
- Tryptanthrin IL-17 and Th17 suppression: Studies report that tryptanthrin decreased IL-17 production by Th17 cells
- Claudin-1 skin barrier restoration: Indigo naturalis components were reported to upregulate claudin-1 expression in tight junctions, supporting skin barrier function
- Synergistic neutrophil inflammation reduction: Indigo, indirubin, and tryptanthrin together were reported to suppress neutrophil infiltration and activation

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Psoriasis: a review and transdermal patch studies have reported combined anti-psoriatic activity of indigo, indirubin, and tryptanthrin.
- Hair and scalp health: traditionally used as a hair dye and conditioner. An EU safety assessment has reported on its safety for hair coloring applications.
- Eczema: a pilot clinical study reported symptom improvement with a Nili extract gel.
- Wound healing: animal studies have reported faster wound closure, increased collagen synthesis, and reduced inflammatory markers.

## Recent safety updates

- Indigofera tinctoria is considered non-toxic in oral administration based on 13-week rat toxicity study with no deaths at doses up to 1000 mg/kg/day. Topical indigo naturalis preparations are generally well-tolerated
- EU Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety (SCCS) assessed Indigofera tinctoria for hair dye use; classified as safe for topical cosmetic application at appropriate concentrations. May cause contact sensitization in rare cases - patch testing advised for first-time users

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Taila (oil), Lepa (paste), Capsule, Tablet, Kwatha (decoction), Hair oil formulation

**Standard Dosage:** 500 mg - 2 g dried powder daily; 5-10 mL infusion; topical oil or paste as directed

**Optimal Timing:** Oral use: after meals. Topical hair oil: apply at night, wash in morning. Skin applications: as directed, typically twice daily.

**Storage:** Store in amber containers, protected from direct light and moisture. Temperature below 25 deg C. Oil formulations in amber glass bottles, cool dark storage. Bulk indigo extract in nitrogen-flushed containers.

**Marker Compounds:** Indirubin, Indigo (indigotin), Isatin, Kaempferol, [Quercetin](/reference/quercetinquercetin/), Gallic acid, Rotenoids
