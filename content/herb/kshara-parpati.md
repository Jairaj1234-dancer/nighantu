## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Siddha Yoga Sangraha, Ashmari Mutrakrichra Adhikara (Urinary Stones and Dysuria Chapter)

## How does it work?

- Surya Kshara ([potassium](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#potassium) nitrate) acts as a potent alkalizing agent, increasing urinary pH to dissolve uric acid and calcium oxalate stones
- [Diuretic](/nighantu/reference/diuretic/) action of potassium nitrate increases urine volume, flushing out small calculi and preventing new stone formation
- Sphatika (alum) has astringent and antiseptic properties, reducing urinary tract inflammation and infection
- Navasadara (ammonium chloride) has urine-acidifying properties at certain concentrations but in Parpati form acts synergistically as a balanced urinary modifier
- Antacid-like action in the GI tract alleviates dyspepsia and acid-related digestive disorders
- Parpati Kalpana (flake preparation) method ensures rapid dissolution and absorption in the GI tract

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Mutrashmari (urinary calculi/kidney stones) - traditional use supported by comparative clinical study showing litholytic and diuretic efficacy
- Mutrakrichra (dysuria/painful urination) - traditional primary indication validated through pharmaceutical review and clinical practice observations

## Recent safety updates

- This is a purely chemical/mineral formulation without iron - no iron overload risk
- Potassium nitrate (Surya Kshara) in high doses may cause methemoglobinemia; strict dose adherence essential (125-500 mg)
- Ammonium chloride may cause metabolic acidosis at high doses or in patients with renal impairment
- Heavy metal contamination is not a primary concern for this mineral-salt formulation, but quality testing should verify absence of contaminants
- Contraindicated in patients with hyperkalemia, severe renal impairment, and cardiac conditions sensitive to potassium levels

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Parpati (flake form)

**Standard Dosage:** 125–250 mg twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Enhanced; Parpati form (thin flake) has increased surface area for absorption

**Optimal Timing:** With buttermilk ([Takra](/nighantu/herb/takra/)) or rice water (Tandulodaka)

**Standardized Extract:** Kajjali melted with Kshara (alkaline substance) and poured on banana leaf

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite

**Storage:** Airtight, moisture-proof container

**Marker Compounds:** Mercury sulfide, Potassium/sodium carbonates (from Kshara), Sulfur

**Quality Parameters:** Thin flake formation, uniform thickness, standard Parpati tests, free mercury <1 ppm

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Buttermilk ([Takra](/nighantu/herb/takra/)) — classical combination
