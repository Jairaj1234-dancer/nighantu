## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Bhaishajya Ratnavali, Stri Rogadhikara (Gynecological Disorders Chapter), Verses 75-79

## How does it work?

- [Loha Bhasma](/herb/loha-bhasma/) is the iron-bearing ingredient, traditionally used for anemia secondary to excessive menstrual blood loss
- [Tamra Bhasma](/herb/tamra-bhasma/) (copper calx) supports copper-dependent [enzymes](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#enzymes) essential for iron metabolism and ceruloplasmin synthesis
- [Abhraka Bhasma](/herb/abhraka-bhasma/) (mica calx) provides trace minerals and is classically regarded as a [Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/) (rejuvenative) for uterine tissue
- [Trikatu](/herb/trikatu/) (Shunthi + Maricha + [Pippali](/herb/pippali/)) enhances absorption of mineral Bhasmas and stimulates Agni (digestive fire)
- [Triphala](/herb/triphala/) components are traditionally described as having [hemostatic](/glossary/pharmacology/#hemostatic), anti-inflammatory, and [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) effects
- [Varatika Bhasma](/herb/varatika-bhasma/) (cowrie shell calx) provides calcium and is traditionally described as a uterine tonic for excessive bleeding

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Menorrhagia and metrorrhagia (Pradara/excessive menstrual bleeding): traditionally used for excessive menstrual bleeding. Clinical observations have reported reduced menstrual blood loss and improved hemoglobin.
- Leucorrhea (Shweta Pradara): used in classical practice as a gynecological application for leucorrhea. Reports from clinical practice have described improvement in vaginal discharge and associated symptoms.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains Shuddha Haratala (purified arsenic trisulfide) - requires strict medical supervision and limited treatment duration
- Heavy metal content (arsenic, copper, tin) necessitates periodic monitoring of liver and kidney function during treatment
- Iron overload risk with prolonged unsupervised use; serum ferritin monitoring essential beyond 4-6 weeks
- Contraindicated in pregnancy, hemochromatosis, Wilson's disease, and hepatic/renal impairment
- Must be prepared according to classical Shodhana (purification) protocols to ensure toxic metal conversion to safer forms; quality-tested products from licensed manufacturers only

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Vati

**Standard Dosage:** 250 mg twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Good; formulated specifically for gynecological iron delivery

**Optimal Timing:** After meals with [Ashoka](/herb/ashoka/) Kwatha or honey

**Standardized Extract:** [Loha Bhasma](/herb/loha-bhasma/) with gynecological herbs

**Shelf Life:** 3–5 years

**Storage:** Airtight container

**Marker Compounds:** Iron oxide

**Quality Parameters:** Iron content as labeled, standard tablet tests

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Ashoka Kwatha, [Lodhra](/herb/lodhra/) Kwatha, or rice water
