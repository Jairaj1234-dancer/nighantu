- [Piperine](/reference/piperine/) (from both peppers)
- Gingerols/Shogaols (from [ginger](/herb/ginger/))

## Therapeutic Actions (Karma)

- Bioavailability enhancer
- Digestive stimulant
- Thermogenic
- [Expectorant](/reference/expectorant/)
- Metabolism booster

## How is it used traditionally?

Synergistic combination, piperine enhances absorption of other herbs and foods. Used for Agnimandya (weak digestion), Ama (metabolic toxins), Kasa (cough), and Sthaulya (obesity). Often added to other formulations as bioenhancer.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

- Charaka Samhita
- Sharangadhara Samhita

## Recommended Dosage

Powder: at the dose given under Standard Dosage, before meals with honey

## Safety, contraindications and cautions

- Pitta disorders
- Gastritis/ulcers
- Pregnancy
- Bleeding disorders

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Vati (tablet), Capsule, [Guggulu](/formulation/guggulu/) combination, Kwatha additive

**Standard Dosage:** 0.5-1 g powder twice daily with honey or warm water; 250-500 mg tablet/capsule twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals (15-30 minutes) for digestive stimulation and bioenhancer effect; avoid at bedtime due to thermogenic activity

**Storage:** Airtight containers at 15-25 deg C, protected from moisture and light. Piperine is photosensitive; use opaque packaging. Powder tends to absorb moisture; include desiccant sachets.

**Marker Compounds:** Piperine, [Piperlongumine](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#piperlongumine), [Piperanine](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#piperanine), 6-[Gingerol](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#gingerol), 8-Gingerol, 10-Gingerol, 6-Shogaol, [Zingerone](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#zingerone)

### Synergistic Combinations

- Acts as universal bioenhancer (Yogavahi) for virtually all Ayurvedic formulations
- [Triphala](/herb/triphala/) + Trikatu - digestive and metabolic combination (Trifala Trikatu)
- [Guggulu](/formulation/guggulu/) - enhanced anti-inflammatory and lipid-lowering formulations
- [Sitopaladi Churna](/formulation/sitopaladi-churna/) - respiratory formula containing Trikatu components
- [Chitrak](/herb/chitrak/) (Plumbago zeylanica) - enhanced thermogenic action
