## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Vati (tablet), Capsule, [Arka](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/arka/) (distillate)

**Standard Dosage:** 2-4 g powder per day; 50-100 mL kwatha twice daily; 500 mg capsule twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Alkaloid constituents show moderate oral bioavailability (30-45%). Flavonoid [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) undergo intestinal hydrolysis. First-pass metabolism is moderate. Absorption is enhanced by warm water vehicle as traditionally prescribed.

**Optimal Timing:** Morning on empty stomach for Medhya (cognitive) indications; with honey as Anupana (vehicle)

**Standardized Extract:** Aqueous-ethanolic extract (6:1), characterized by HPTLC fingerprint profile; standardized to NLT 1% total [alkaloids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) by titrimetric method

**Shelf Life:** 24 months for churna; 30 months for capsules and tablets

**Storage:** Store below 25 deg C in airtight containers, protected from light and moisture.

**Marker Compounds:** Alkaloids (total by gravimetric method), Beta-[sitosterol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), [Flavonoids](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/flavonoids/) (total), [Tannins](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/tannins/)

### Extraction Methods

- Aqueous decoction
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50-60% ethanol)
- Cold maceration
- Percolation with 60% ethanol
