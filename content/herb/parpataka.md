## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (whole herb powder), Kwatha (decoction), Capsule, Tablet, Swarasa (fresh juice), Hima (cold infusion)

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3 g dried powder twice daily; 15-30 mL decoction twice daily; 10-20 mL cold infusion; 500-750 mg standardized extract daily

**Bioavailability:** [Swertiamarin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#swertiamarin) and other secoiridoid [glycosides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) from Parpataka (Fumaria indica/Oldenlandia corymbosa) show moderate oral bioavailability (25-40%). Bitter principles stimulate digestive secretions providing local GI effects. [Hepatoprotective](/nighantu/reference/hepatoprotective/) activity is partly mediated through portal circulation, with efficient first-pass hepatic uptake being therapeutically beneficial. Fumaric acid derivatives are well absorbed (>60%).

**Optimal Timing:** Cold infusion (Hima): morning on empty stomach for liver and fever conditions. Decoction/powder: before meals for digestive and hepatic benefits.

**Standardized Extract:** Parpataka whole herb extract (8:1) standardized to swertiamarin >1.5% (if Fumaria species) or total [alkaloids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) >0.5% (fumarine for Fumaria indica). HPTLC fingerprint mandatory for species confirmation.

**Shelf Life:** Dried herb: 12-18 months; Powder: 12-18 months; Extract: 24 months; Capsules: 24 months; Cold infusion: prepare fresh daily

**Storage:** Dried herb and powder: airtight containers, below 25 deg C, RH <55%, protected from light. Extracts: amber containers, below 25 deg C. Fresh juice and cold infusion: refrigerated, consume within 24 hours.

**Marker Compounds:** Swertiamarin (species-dependent), Fumarine, Fumaric acid, [Protopine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#protopine), [Cryptopine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-c-d/#cryptopine), Oleanolic acid, Ursolic acid, Caffeic acid

### Extraction Methods
