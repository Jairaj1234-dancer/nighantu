## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Capsule, Tablet, Taila (medicated oil), Lepa (paste)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g powder twice daily; 50-100 mL decoction twice daily; external oil application as needed

**Bioavailability:** Argyrein ([coumarin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-c-d/#coumarin) glycoside) has moderate oral bioavailability (~25-35%) with hepatic hydrolysis releasing the aglycone. [Friedelin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#friedelin) and other [triterpenoids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-t-z/#triterpenoids) have low oral bioavailability (<10%) improved by lipid co-administration. [Flavonoids](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/flavonoids/) ([kaempferol](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/kaempferol/), [quercetin](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/quercetinquercetin/) [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides)) undergo typical Phase II conjugation with moderate bioavailability (~15-20%). External application (Taila) allows local absorption through skin for musculoskeletal conditions.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals for internal use; external oil application at night before sleep for musculoskeletal conditions

**Standardized Extract:** Root extract standardized to >2% total flavonoids (as quercetin equivalent by UV); >0.5% argyrein by HPLC; ergot [alkaloids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids): absent (mandatory safety test)

**Shelf Life:** Root powder: 18-24 months; Capsules/Tablets: 24 months; Medicated oil: 18-24 months; Decoction: prepare fresh

**Storage:** Airtight, moisture-proof containers at 15-25 deg C. Medicated oil in amber glass. Protect from light and humidity.

**Marker Compounds:** Argyrein, Friedelin, Quercetin, Kaempferol, beta-[Sitosterol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), [Scopoletin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#scopoletin), Caffeic acid

### Extraction Methods

- Methanol extraction for coumarin-rich fraction
