## How does it work?

- [Antidiabetic](/reference/antidiabetic/) activity via mangiferin-mediated AMPK activation, enhanced GLUT4 translocation and inhibition of alpha-glucosidase
- Anti-inflammatory action through inhibition of NF-kB, COX-2, and iNOS by mangiferin and gallic acid derivatives
- [Antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) activity via xanthone-mediated free radical scavenging, chelation of Fe2+ ions and upregulation of Nrf2/ARE pathway
- [Anticancer](/reference/anticancer/) mechanisms through induction of apoptosis (caspase-3/9 activation), cell cycle arrest and inhibition of angiogenesis

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Bark and leaf decoctions are traditionally used for diabetes. Preclinical studies and preliminary clinical observations have reported antidiabetic activity.
- Traditionally used for diarrhea and dysentery. Animal studies have reported [antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) activity and astringent effects of tannins.
- Bark decoction is traditionally used as a mouthwash for oral health. Studies have reported antimicrobial activity against oral pathogens.
- Traditionally used for anemia. The leaves and fruits are reported to contain iron. Studies in deficient models have reported hematopoietic effects.

## Recent safety updates

- Mangiferin and mango [polyphenols](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polyphenols) are generally safe at dietary and supplemental doses; GRAS (Generally Recognized as Safe) status for mango fruit and derived products
- Mango latex and sap contain urushiol-like allergens causing contact dermatitis in sensitive individuals; mango allergy (cross-reactivity with cashew and pistachio) documented in clinical reports

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (dried fruit/seed kernel powder), Amchur (dried unripe fruit powder), Aam Panna (beverage), Capsule, Tablet, Avaleha (preserve), Majja (pulp-based preparations)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 5-10g Amchur as culinary-medicinal ingredient; 50-100ml Aam Panna

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals for appetite stimulation (Amchur); with meals for digestive support; Aam Panna traditionally taken in summer afternoons

**Storage:** Amchur in airtight containers at room temperature. Extracts below 25°C in moisture-proof packaging. Fresh pulp preparations require refrigeration.

**Marker Compounds:** Mangiferin, Gallic acid, Ellagic acid, Beta-carotene, Isomangiferin, [Quercetin](/reference/quercetinquercetin/), Catechol tannins

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Ela (cardamom) for digestive and cooling properties
- With [Jeeraka](/herb/jeeraka/) and Saindhava in Aam Panna, traditionally taken as a summer cooling drink
- With Amalaki for antioxidant synergy
- With [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) for enhanced mangiferin absorption
