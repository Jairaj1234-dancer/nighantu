## How does it work?

- [Antidiabetic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antidiabetic/) activity via mangiferin-mediated AMPK activation, enhanced GLUT4 translocation and inhibition of alpha-glucosidase
- Anti-inflammatory action through inhibition of NF-kB, COX-2, and iNOS by mangiferin and gallic acid derivatives
- [Antioxidant](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antioxidant/) activity via xanthone-mediated free radical scavenging, chelation of Fe2+ ions and upregulation of Nrf2/ARE pathway
- [Anticancer](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/anticancer/) mechanisms through induction of apoptosis (caspase-3/9 activation), cell cycle arrest and inhibition of angiogenesis

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Antidiabetic use of bark and leaf decoctions validated through multiple preclinical studies and preliminary clinical observations
- Anti-diarrheal and dysentery treatment validated through [antimicrobial](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antimicrobial/) activity and astringent tannin effects in animal models
- Oral health use (bark decoction as mouthwash) validated through antimicrobial activity against oral pathogens
- Anemia management supported by iron content in leaves and fruits and confirmed hematopoietic effects in deficient models

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **A Review on Ethnopharmacological Applications, Pharmacological Activities, and Bioactive Compounds of Mangifera indica (Mango)** (2023): Documented anticancer, anti-inflammatory, antidiabetic, antioxidant, antibacterial, gastroprotective, [hepatoprotective](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/hepatoprotective/) and [immunomodulatory](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/immunomodulatory/) activities attributed primarily to mangiferin and gallotannins.
- **Nutritional and medicinal values of Mangifera indica L. fruit** (2024): Mangiferin demonstrated potent antidiabetic activity through AMPK activation, improved [glucose](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#glucose) uptake and insulin sensitization in adipocytes and hepatocytes.
- **Pharmacological Activities of Mango (Mangifera indica): A Comprehensive Review** (2024): Leaf and bark extracts showed significant anticancer activity in MCF-7 breast cancer and HeLa cervical cancer cell lines through apoptosis induction via caspase-3 activation.

## Recent safety updates

- Mangiferin and mango [polyphenols](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polyphenols) are generally safe at dietary and supplemental doses; GRAS (Generally Recognized as Safe) status for mango fruit and derived products
- Mango latex and sap contain urushiol-like allergens causing contact dermatitis in sensitive individuals; mango allergy (cross-reactivity with cashew and pistachio) documented in clinical reports

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (dried fruit/seed kernel powder), Amchur (dried unripe fruit powder), Aam Panna (beverage), Capsule, Tablet, Avaleha (preserve), Majja (pulp-based preparations)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 5-10g Amchur as culinary-medicinal ingredient; 50-100ml Aam Panna

**Bioavailability:** Good bioavailability for mangiferin (major xanthone), with oral bioavailability of approximately 5-8% for pure mangiferin but significantly enhanced (2-3x) by mango fruit matrix. Lipid co-administration increases mangiferin absorption by 40-60%. Gallic acid has high oral bioavailability (>70%). [Carotenoids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-a-c/#carotenoids) (beta-carotene) require fat for absorption.

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals for appetite stimulation (Amchur); with meals for digestive support; Aam Panna in summer afternoons for heat protection

**Standardized Extract:** Leaf extract standardized to minimum 5% mangiferin. Bark extract standardized to minimum 8% mangiferin. Seed kernel extract: minimum 15% total [tannins](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/tannins/) (as gallic acid equivalent).

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (powder/Amchur); 3 years (capsules/tablets); 1 year (pulp preserves); 18 months (Aam Panna concentrate)

**Storage:** Amchur in airtight containers at room temperature. Extracts below 25°C in moisture-proof packaging. Fresh pulp preparations require refrigeration.

**Marker Compounds:** Mangiferin, Gallic acid, Ellagic acid, Beta-carotene, Isomangiferin, [Quercetin](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/quercetinquercetin/), Catechol tannins

### Extraction Methods

- Hydroalcoholic extraction (70:30) for mangiferin enrichment
- Aqueous extraction for polyphenol fraction
- Supercritical CO2 for carotenoid enrichment
- Ethyl acetate fractionation for mangiferin isolation
- Spray-drying of pulp concentrate

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Ela (cardamom) for digestive and cooling properties
- With [Jeeraka](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/jeeraka/) and Saindhava in Aam Panna for heat stroke prevention
- With Amalaki for antioxidant synergy
- With [Pippali](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/pippali/) for enhanced mangiferin absorption
