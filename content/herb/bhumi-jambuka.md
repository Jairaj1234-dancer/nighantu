## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Swarasa (fresh juice), Capsule, Lepa (paste)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 10-20ml fresh juice; 50-100ml decoction. Limited modern dosage standardization—follow traditional Vaidya guidance.

**Bioavailability:** Limited modern pharmacokinetic data available. Traditional preparations use honey or warm water as vehicles to enhance absorption. Mucilaginous content may provide sustained-release characteristics. Phenolic compounds likely undergo glucuronidation and sulfation in hepatic first-pass. Note: this is a less-studied herb requiring further pharmacokinetic investigation.

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals with warm water or honey; fresh juice early morning on empty stomach

**Standardized Extract:** Whole plant aqueous extract 10:1 ratio. Standardization parameters require further development due to limited phytochemical characterization.

**Shelf Life:** 18 months (powder); 2 years (capsule/tablet)

**Storage:** Cool, dry place in airtight containers. Protect from moisture and light.

**Marker Compounds:** Beta-[sitosterol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), [Lupeol](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/lupeol/), Gallic acid, Flavonoid [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides), Phenolic acids

### Extraction Methods
