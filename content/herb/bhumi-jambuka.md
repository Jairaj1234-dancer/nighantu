## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Swarasa (fresh juice), Capsule, Lepa (paste)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 10-20ml fresh juice; 50-100ml decoction. Limited modern dosage standardization, follow traditional Vaidya guidance.

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals with warm water or honey; fresh juice early morning on empty stomach

**Storage:** Cool, dry place in airtight containers. Protect from moisture and light.

**Marker Compounds:** Beta-[sitosterol](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), [Lupeol](/reference/lupeol/), Gallic acid, Flavonoid [glycosides](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides), Phenolic acids
