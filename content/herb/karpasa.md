## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Beeja Churna (seed powder), Taila (cottonseed oil), Kashayam (decoction of root bark), Capsule, Pattika (cotton wick for Varti/suppository)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-5 g seed powder twice daily; 5-10 mL cottonseed oil; 50-100 mL root bark decoction

**Bioavailability:** [Gossypol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#gossypol) (polyphenolic aldehyde) has variable bioavailability (30-50%) influenced by dietary protein and mineral content. Gossypol chelates iron and calcium, reducing both mineral and gossypol absorption. Protein-rich meals reduce gossypol absorption. Refined cottonseed oil (gossypol-free) has standard lipid bioavailability. Cyclodextrin complexation can modulate gossypol release profile.

**Optimal Timing:** Root bark decoction: after meals; seed preparations: with meals to moderate gossypol absorption

**Standardized Extract:** Root bark extract standardized to minimum 3% total [flavonoids](/nighantu/reference/flavonoids/); seed extract: gossypol content must be quantified and controlled (<0.05% for food-grade; specific higher levels for pharmaceutical research)

**Shelf Life:** 18 months for root bark powder; 24 months for refined cottonseed oil; 24 months for capsules

**Storage:** Cottonseed oil in cool, dark place (15-25°C) in amber glass or food-grade HDPE. Root bark powder in airtight containers protected from moisture. Gossypol-containing preparations require restricted storage.

**Marker Compounds:** Gossypol, Gossypin (flavonoid), [Gossypetin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#gossypetin), [Herbacetin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#herbacetin), Beta-[sitosterol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), Linoleic acid, Oleic acid

### Extraction Methods

- Cold pressing of seeds for oil
- Solvent extraction (hexane) for commercial oil production
- Hydroalcoholic extraction of root bark
- Selective extraction for gossypol using acetic acid-diethyl ether
