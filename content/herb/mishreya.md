## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Phenolic acids (rosmarinic acid, chlorogenic acid)

## How does it work?

- [Antispasmodic](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#antispasmodic) and carminative mechanism through trans-anethole blocking calcium channels in GI smooth muscle, reducing intestinal spasm and gas-related pain
- Estrogenic activity mediated by trans-anethole and its polymers (dianethole, photoanethole) acting as weak estrogen receptor agonists, supporting [galactagogue](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#galactagogue) and menstrual regulation properties
- [Hepatoprotective](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/hepatoprotective/) mechanism through [antioxidant](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antioxidant/)-mediated reduction of hepatic lipid peroxidation and enhancement of glutathione and catalase enzyme levels

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Carminative and digestive properties extensively validated through clinical and pharmacological studies, confirming premier Ayurvedic use as Deepana-Pachana (appetite and digestion promoter)
- Galactagogue (breast milk promoting) activity supported by clinical evidence showing increased milk production in breastfeeding women, confirming traditional Stanyajanana use
- Antispasmodic activity in infantile colic validated through randomized clinical trials, confirming traditional use for pediatric GI complaints

## Recent safety updates

- GRAS (Generally Recognized as Safe) by FDA; however, estragole component has raised genotoxicity concerns - EMA recommends limiting estragole exposure; [fennel](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/fennel/) products should be standardized for estragole content
- Phytoestrogenic effects warrant caution in hormone-sensitive conditions (breast cancer, endometriosis); potential interactions with anticoagulants and ciprofloxacin; allergic cross-reactivity possible in patients with celery/carrot/mugwort allergy (celery-birch-mugwort-spice syndrome)

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (seed powder), Essential oil, Svarasa/Ark (distillate - Saunf water), Capsules, Tablets, Gripe water component, Syrups, Infusion/Tea

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g seed powder per day; 0.1-0.5 mL essential oil; 10-30 mL Saunf Ark; 250-500 mg extract capsules after meals

**Bioavailability:** Trans-anethole has excellent oral bioavailability (~60-70%) due to high lipophilicity. Rapidly absorbed in upper GI tract. Metabolized hepatically via O-demethylation and omega-oxidation. Fenchone has moderate bioavailability. Essential oil components readily absorbed via inhalation and buccal mucosa. Estragole undergoes extensive first-pass metabolism.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals for digestive and carminative effects; between meals for bloating relief; Saunf Ark can be consumed throughout the day; nursing mothers: between feedings for galactagogue effect

**Standardized Extract:** Essential oil (BP/EP grade) with minimum 80% trans-anethole and maximum 5% estragole. Aqueous distillate (Saunf Ark) with 0.01-0.05% dissolved volatiles. Standardized seed extract (5:1) with minimum 3% anethole. De-estragole processed oil for safety-conscious markets.

**Shelf Life:** Whole seeds: 24 months. Ground powder: 12 months (volatile loss). Essential oil: 36 months sealed. Saunf Ark: 12 months. Capsules: 24 months.

**Storage:** Whole seeds in airtight containers at room temperature. Ground powder nitrogen-flushed in foil pouches. Essential oil in amber glass, tightly sealed, below 25 deg C. Saunf Ark refrigerate after opening.

**Marker Compounds:** Trans-anethole, Fenchone, Estragole (controlled), Alpha-pinene, Limonene, Methyl chavicol, Anisaldehyde

### Extraction Methods

- Steam distillation for essential oil (yield 2-6%)
- Hydrodistillation for Saunf Ark
- Aqueous infusion
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50% ethanol)
- Supercritical CO2 extraction
- Cold pressing of seeds
