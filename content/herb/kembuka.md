## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Kalka (paste), Capsule, Phanta (hot infusion)

**Standard Dosage:** 2-4 g powder twice daily; 40-80 mL decoction twice daily; paste for external application as needed

**Bioavailability:** [Alkaloids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) and flavonoid [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) show moderate oral bioavailability. Traditional preparation with [Trikatu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/trikatu/) enhances absorption of lipophilic constituents. Decoction form provides hydrolyzed glycosides with improved absorption. Mucilaginous constituents slow but sustain absorption.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals with warm water; for anti-inflammatory applications, twice daily at consistent intervals

**Standardized Extract:** Standardized to minimum 2% [diosgenin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#diosgenin) by HPLC and 3% total [saponins](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/saponins/) (as diosgenin equivalents)

**Shelf Life:** 18 months for rhizome powder; 24 months for capsules; decoction consumed within 24 hours

**Storage:** Store dried rhizome material in airtight containers at 15-30°C. High moisture content of raw rhizome (>70%) demands thorough drying to <10% moisture. Protect from humidity and insect infestation.

**Marker Compounds:** Diosgenin, [Costunolide](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-c-d/#costunolide), Eremanthin, [Stigmasterol](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/stigmasterol/), Beta-[sitosterol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), Gracillin, Prosapogenin

### Extraction Methods

- Water decoction (traditional)
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50-60% ethanol)
- Cold maceration for [mucilage](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#mucilage)-rich preparations
- Infusion for volatile-rich fractions

### Synergistic Combinations

- Shunthi ([Ginger](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ginger/)) for digestive and carminative synergy
- [Haridra](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/haridra/) for anti-inflammatory combinations
- Musta for digestive disorders
- [Eranda](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/eranda/) for anti-arthritic formulations
