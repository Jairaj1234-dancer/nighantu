## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Swarasa (fresh juice), Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Tablet, Capsule

**Standard Dosage:** 5-10ml fresh juice twice daily; 3-6g powder twice daily; 50-100ml decoction twice daily; 500mg extract capsule twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals with warm water; fresh juice on empty stomach

**Storage:** Powder and extracts: airtight containers below 25 deg C, protected from moisture and light. Fresh material: refrigerate at 2-8 deg C.

**Marker Compounds:** Cucurbitacin B, Cucurbitacin E, Colocynthin, Saponins, Luffin (ribosome-inactivating protein)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Katuki](/herb/katuki/) (Picrorhiza) for [hepatoprotective](/reference/hepatoprotective/) synergy
- With [Haritaki](/herb/haritaki/), traditionally used as a purgative
- With Bhringaraj, traditionally used for jaundice
- With [Punarnava](/herb/punarnava/), traditionally combined for hepatorenal support
