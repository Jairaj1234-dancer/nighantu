## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Capsule, Essential oil, Tablet, Spice extract

**Standard Dosage:** 250-500mg powder with meals; 125-250mg extract; 1-2 drops essential oil externally

**Bioavailability:** Myristica fragrans mace (aril) has similar but distinct phytochemistry compared to nutmeg seed. Mace essential oil is richer in [myristicin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#myristicin) and macelignan. Myristicin absorption and metabolism profile similar to nutmeg. Macelignan shows good oral absorption with [hepatoprotective](/nighantu/reference/hepatoprotective/) activity. Mace oleoresin is lipophilic with good GI absorption when taken with food.

**Optimal Timing:** With meals for digestive support; in small doses as flavoring agent in formulations

**Standardized Extract:** Essential oil standardized to myristicin (10-18%) with [safrole](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#safrole) <1%; oleoresin (15-20% volatile oil content); powder with HPTLC fingerprint

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (whole mace); 1 year (ground powder); 3 years (essential oil, sealed); 3 years (capsule)

**Storage:** Cool, dry place below 25°C, protected from light. Whole mace more stable than ground. Amber containers for essential oil. Nitrogen-flushed packaging for ground mace.

**Marker Compounds:** Myristicin, Macelignan, [Elemicin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-d-g/#elemicin), Safrole (monitor and limit), [Sabinene](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sabinene), Alpha-pinene, Myristic acid

### Extraction Methods

- Steam distillation (essential oil, 4-17% yield from mace)
- Supercritical CO2 extraction
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (80:20)
- Oleoresin extraction (acetone or ethanol)
- Mechanical grinding for Churna

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Jatiphala](/nighantu/herb/jatiphala/) (nutmeg) for comprehensive Myristica-based formulation
- With Ela (cardamom) and [Lavanga](/nighantu/herb/lavanga/) (clove) for digestive blends
- With [Dalchini](/nighantu/herb/twak-cinnamon/) (cinnamon) for metabolic support
