## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Capsule, Essential oil, Tablet, Spice extract

**Standard Dosage:** 250-500mg powder with meals; 125-250mg extract; 1-2 drops essential oil externally

**Optimal Timing:** With meals for digestive support; in small doses as flavoring agent in formulations

**Storage:** Cool, dry place below 25°C, protected from light. Whole mace more stable than ground. Amber containers for essential oil. Nitrogen-flushed packaging for ground mace.

**Marker Compounds:** Myristicin, Macelignan, [Elemicin](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#elemicin), Safrole (monitor and limit), [Sabinene](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sabinene), Alpha-pinene, Myristic acid

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Jatiphala](/herb/jatiphala/) (nutmeg) for comprehensive Myristica-based formulation
- With Ela (cardamom) and [Lavanga](/herb/lavanga/) (clove) for digestive blends
- With [Dalchini](/herb/twak-cinnamon/) (cinnamon) for metabolic support
