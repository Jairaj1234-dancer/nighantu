## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Swarasa (fresh leaf juice), Churna (dried leaf powder), Lepa (paste), Kashayam (decoction), Culinary herb (fresh)

**Standard Dosage:** 10-20ml fresh juice; 2-4g dried leaf powder; external paste as needed; liberally used as fresh culinary herb. Cilantro/[coriander](/herb/coriander/) leaves used as fresh herb primarily.

**Optimal Timing:** Fresh juice traditionally taken early morning; as culinary herb with meals; external paste as needed for skin conditions

**Storage:** Fresh herb refrigerated at 2-5°C with damp paper towel in sealed bag. Dried leaves in airtight, light-protected containers. Freeze-dried product in nitrogen-flushed, moisture-proof packaging.

**Marker Compounds:** Decanal, Dodecanal, 2-Decenal, Linalool (trace in leaves), [Quercetin](/reference/quercetinquercetin/), [Kaempferol](/reference/kaempferol/), [Apigenin](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#apigenin), Chlorogenic acid, Beta-carotene, [Lutein](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#lutein)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Mint ([Pudina](/herb/pudina/)) for digestive and cooling formulations
- With [Neem](/herb/neem/) for detoxification and skin health
- With Amalaki for Pitta-pacifying combinations
- With [Haridra](/herb/haridra/) for anti-inflammatory leaf preparations
