## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Swarasa (fresh juice), Vati (tablet), Capsule, Nasya (nasal drops)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-5 g powder per day; 50-100 mL kwatha twice daily; 5-10 mL swarasa twice daily; 2-4 drops nasya oil per nostril for headache

**Bioavailability:** Flavonoid [glycosides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) and phenolic acids show moderate oral bioavailability (25-35%). Nasya (nasal) administration provides rapid absorption of volatile and lipophilic constituents via nasal mucosa — onset of action within 15-30 minutes for headache relief. Essential oil components are well-absorbed transdermally.

**Optimal Timing:** Morning before sunrise for preventive headache management (per traditional rationale); nasya in morning after facial steaming; acute use as needed

**Standardized Extract:** Whole plant extract (6:1 in 50% ethanol), characterized by HPTLC fingerprint; nasya oil processed per Sneha Kalpana with standardized herb content

**Shelf Life:** 7 days for swarasa; 24 months for churna and capsules; 24 months for nasya oil

**Storage:** Swarasa: refrigerate 2-8 deg C. Churna and capsules: below 25 deg C, airtight, protect from light. Nasya oil: amber glass dropper bottles at room temperature.

**Marker Compounds:** [Acteoside](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-1-a/#acteoside) (if Clerodendrum species), Beta-[sitosterol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), Ursolic acid, [Flavonoids](/nighantu/reference/flavonoids/) (total), Essential oil constituents

### Extraction Methods

- Aqueous decoction
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50-60% ethanol)
- Cold pressing for swarasa
- Maceration in [sesame oil](/nighantu/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila) for nasya preparation
- Steam distillation for volatile fraction
