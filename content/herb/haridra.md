## How does it work?

- NF-kB pathway suppression: [Curcumin](/herb/curcumin/) blocks NF-kB activation by inhibiting phosphorylase kinase, suppressing 200+ genes related to inflammatory cell proliferation, cell migration, and epidermal hyperproliferation in psoriasis and dermatitis
- Tyrosinase inhibition and MITF regulation: [Curcumin](/herb/curcumin/) suppresses melanogenesis through dual strategies - competitive tyrosinase binding to block catalytic activity AND disrupting MITF-TYR/TRP transcriptional axis for melanin synthesis inhibition
- MMP-1/MMP-9 collagen protection via MAPK pathway: [Curcumin](/herb/curcumin/) reduces MMP-1 expression by blocking JNK and p38 phosphorylation in the MAPK signaling pathway, preventing type I collagen degradation and protecting dermal extracellular matrix
- Wound healing fibroblast stimulation: [Curcumin](/herb/curcumin/) promotes fibroblast proliferation, enhances collagen synthesis and deposition, increases vascular density at wound sites, and modulates TGF-beta signaling for accelerated tissue repair

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Vranaropana (wound care): traditionally used for wounds. A scoping review of clinical trials reported faster wound closure, greater collagen deposition and increased vascular density.
- Ekakushtha (psoriasis): traditionally used for Ekakushtha. A randomized controlled trial reported that a [curcumin](/herb/curcumin/) microemulgel improved psoriasis severity scores and quality of life.
- Varnya (skin complexion): traditionally used for complexion. Research has reported tyrosinase inhibition and MITF regulation, with reduced melanin synthesis.
- Anti-aging and photoprotection: research has reported reduced UV-induced photoaging, linked to MMP suppression, [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) protection and anti-inflammatory activity.

## How is it used traditionally?

Used in many Ayurvedic formulations. Sacred in Hindu rituals. Classified as Varnya (complexion-enhancing), Kushthagna (anti-dermatosis), and Vishaghna (antitoxic). Golden milk (Haldi Doodh) is a traditional preparation.

## Recent safety updates

- Topical curcumin is generally well-tolerated with minimal adverse effects; may cause temporary yellow staining of skin. The 0.5% microemulgel formulation showed excellent tolerability in clinical trials with no significant side effects
- Major limitation is low oral bioavailability; enhanced formulations (nano-curcumin, liposomal curcumin, [piperine](/reference/piperine/) combinations) improve absorption. Oral curcumin at high doses (>8g/day) may cause GI disturbance. GRAS status by FDA for food use

## Recommended Dosage

Powder and curcumin extract: at the doses given under Standard Dosage (with piperine for bioavailability).

## Safety, contraindications and cautions

- Gallstones (stimulates bile)
- Blood thinners (anticoagulant synergy)
- Pre-surgery
- Iron-deficiency anemia (may reduce iron absorption)

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Tablet, Capsule, Kashayam (decoction), Lepa (paste for external use), Taila (oil), Soft gel capsule, Nano-formulation

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder daily; 400-600mg curcuminoid extract twice daily; 15-30ml decoction

**Optimal Timing:** With meals containing fat for improved absorption; traditionally taken with warm milk or ghee at bedtime

**Storage:** Cool, dry place protected from light (curcumin degrades under UV). Store below 25°C in amber containers. Avoid alkaline environments.

**Marker Compounds:** Curcumin (diferuloylmethane), Demethoxycurcumin, Bisdemethoxycurcumin, ar-Turmerone, Turmerone, Zingiberene

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) (Piper longum), which contains piperine
- With Maricha ([black pepper](/herb/black-pepper/)) for curcumin absorption
- With [Guduchi](/herb/guduchi/) for immune modulation
- With [Neem](/herb/neem/) for skin disorders
- With Ghee or oil for lipid-mediated absorption
- With Amalaki for antioxidant synergy
