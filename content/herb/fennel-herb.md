## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Fresh herb (culinary), Dried herb powder, Infusion/tea, Capsule, Tincture (1:5 in 45% ethanol)

**Standard Dosage:** Fresh herb: 5-15g in salads/cooking; Dried herb: 2-4g as infusion 2-3 times daily; 500mg dried extract capsule twice daily; 2-5ml tincture three times daily

**Optimal Timing:** Infusion: after meals for digestive comfort; fresh herb consumed with meals for culinary and digestive benefit

**Storage:** Fresh herb: refrigerate in perforated bags. Dried herb: airtight, light-resistant containers below 25 deg C. Protect from moisture and UV light to preserve green color and volatile content.

**Marker Compounds:** Quercetin, Kaempferol, Chlorogenic acid, trans-Anethole (lower levels than seed), Fenchone, [Rutin](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#rutin)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Dill herb for carminative synergy
- With Mint for digestive tea blends
- With Chamomile for calming digestive combination
- With [Ajwain](/herb/ajwain/) for enhanced carminative and anti-flatulent action
