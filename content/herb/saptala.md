## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- [Terpenoids](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#terpenoids) and [sterols](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sterols)
- Latex compounds (diterpene esters)

## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory activity through COX-2 inhibition by ingenol derivatives and flavonoid-mediated reduction of inflammation mediators
- [Antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) mechanism via flavonoid glycoside-mediated scavenging of reactive oxygen species and hydroxyl radicals
- [Analgesic](/reference/analgesic/) effect through both peripheral (prostaglandin inhibition) and central (opioid pathway modulation) mechanisms
- Purgative action through irritant diterpene ester stimulation of intestinal smooth muscle

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Purgative (Virechana): traditionally used as a purgative. Studies have reported stimulant [laxative](/reference/laxative/) action of diterpene esters on intestinal smooth muscle.
- Skin diseases (Kushtha): traditionally used for psoriasis, ringworm, and warts. Studies have reported [antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) and anti-inflammatory activity.
- Anti-rheumatic (Amavata): traditionally used for Amavata. Animal studies have reported anti-inflammatory and analgesic activity.
- [Diuretic](/reference/diuretic/) (Mutrala): traditionally used as a diuretic, as described in Ayurvedic texts.

## Recent safety updates

- Latex is irritant and can cause severe skin and mucosal irritation; internal use requires careful dose calibration; traditionally classified as a purgative (Virechana dravya) requiring practitioner supervision
- Formal large-scale toxicological studies lacking; debate persists among pharmacologists about safety profile for internal use; ingenol esters may promote tumor growth at certain doses (tumor promoter concern in Euphorbia genus)

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Vati (tablet), Capsule

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3 g churna per day; 30-50 mL kwatha twice daily. Start at low dose and titrate upward due to purgative activity.

**Optimal Timing:** Early morning on empty stomach for purgative action; take with warm water

**Storage:** Store below 25 deg C in airtight containers, protected from moisture and light. RH <55%.

**Marker Compounds:** Not established.

### Synergistic Combinations

- [Trivrit](/herb/trivrit/) (synergistic purgative action for Virechana)
- [Haritaki](/herb/haritaki/) (mild laxative formulations)
