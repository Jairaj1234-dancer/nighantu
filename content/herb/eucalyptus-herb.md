## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Dried leaf infusion/tea, Churna (leaf powder), Capsule (leaf extract), Tablet, Tincture (1:5 in 45% ethanol), Poultice (crushed leaves)

**Standard Dosage:** 2-4g dried leaf as infusion 2-3 times daily; 500-1000mg standardized leaf extract capsule twice daily; 2-5ml tincture three times daily; poultice applied externally as needed

**Optimal Timing:** Infusion/tea: 2-3 times daily between meals; capsule: after meals twice daily; poultice: applied externally as needed

**Storage:** Dried leaves in airtight containers below 25 deg C, protected from moisture and light. Tincture: amber glass, room temperature. Tea bags: individually sealed, nitrogen-flushed for aroma retention.

**Marker Compounds:** Ellagic acid, Gallic acid, [Rutin](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#rutin), [Quercetin](/reference/quercetinquercetin/), 1,8-Cineole (residual), Eucalyptin, Macrocarpal A

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Tulsi](/herb/tulsi/) leaf for respiratory and immune support
- With Adulsa (Adhatoda), traditionally combined to support bronchial function
- With [Yashtimadhu](/herb/yashtimadhu/) traditionally combined to support the throat
- With [Ginger](/herb/ginger/) for enhanced expectorant and warming effect
