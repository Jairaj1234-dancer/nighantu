## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Capsule, Tablet, Granules, Pak (confection with sugar/ghee), Avaleha, Soft gelatin capsule

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g root powder per day; 500-1000 mg standardized extract twice daily; 5-10 g Musli Pak with warm milk

**Bioavailability:** Saponin [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) (safed musli [saponins](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/saponins/)) have moderate oral bioavailability (20-30%), enhanced by milk and ghee co-administration (traditional Anupana). [Polysaccharides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polysaccharides) ([galactomannan](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#galactomannan), [glucomannan](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#glucomannan) — 30-40% of dry weight) act as prebiotics and have local GI effects rather than systemic absorption. Sapogenin aglycones are lipophilic and benefit from lipid-based formulations. Phytosome complexation improves saponin bioavailability by 2x.

**Optimal Timing:** With warm milk at bedtime for [Vajikarana](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#vajikarana) (reproductive tonic) action; morning dose with ghee for strength-building (Balya) applications

**Standardized Extract:** Root extract (10:1 in 60% ethanol), standardized to NLT 25% total saponins (as [diosgenin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#diosgenin) equivalents by HPLC-ELSD); polysaccharide-enriched aqueous extract NLT 40% total polysaccharides by phenol-sulfuric acid method

**Shelf Life:** 24 months for churna; 36 months for standardized extract capsules; 12 months for Musli Pak (moisture-sensitive confection)

**Storage:** Store below 25 deg C in airtight containers with desiccant. Extremely hygroscopic due to [mucilage](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#mucilage) — RH <40% mandatory. Musli Pak in glass jars, refrigerate after opening. Protect from moisture and insect infestation.

**Marker Compounds:** Safed musli saponins ([stigmasterol](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/stigmasterol/) glycosides), Diosgenin, Tigogenin, Stigmasterol, Beta-[sitosterol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), Galactomannan (polysaccharide marker)

### Extraction Methods

- Aqueous extraction for polysaccharide enrichment
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50-70% ethanol) for saponin enrichment
- Traditional Pak preparation with ghee and sugar
- Spray-drying for instant powder
- Supercritical CO2 for sapogenin-enriched fraction
