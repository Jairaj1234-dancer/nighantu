## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (flower powder), Capsule, Tablet, Swarasa (fresh flower juice), Taila (hair oil), Kwath (decoction), Gulkand-like preserve

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g dried flower powder twice daily; 500mg extract; 10-20ml fresh flower juice; external oil as needed

**Optimal Timing:** With meals for internal use; hair oil application at night before washing

**Storage:** Cool, dry place below 25°C, protected from light (critical for anthocyanin stability). Amber containers. Nitrogen-flushed packaging for extract powder.

**Marker Compounds:** Cyanidin-3-sophoroside, Cyanidin-3-glucoside, Quercetin, [Kaempferol](/reference/kaempferol/), Hibiscus acid, Vitamin C (ascorbic acid), Mucilage [polysaccharides](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polysaccharides)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Bhringaraj for hair growth (external)
- With Amalaki for [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) action
- With [Shatavari](/herb/shatavari/) for female reproductive health
- With [Brahmi](/herb/brahmi/) oil for hair vitality (external)
