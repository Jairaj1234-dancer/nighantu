## What is it made of?

### Curcuminoids (Diarylheptanoids)

- Curcumin (diferuloylmethane, 75-80%)
- Demethoxycurcumin (15-20%)
- Bisdemethoxycurcumin (3-5%)
- Cyclocurcumin (trace)

### Metabolites

- Tetrahydrocurcumin
- Hexahydrocurcumin
- Curcumin glucuronide
- Curcumin sulfate

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna ([turmeric](/herb/turmeric/) powder), Capsule (standardized extract), Tablet, Soft gel, Liposomal liquid, Nanoemulsion, Phytosome complex, Topical cream/gel, Golden milk (Haldi Doodh)

**Standard Dosage:** [Turmeric](/herb/turmeric/) powder: 3-6g daily; Standardized curcumin extract: 500-1500mg daily; Phytosome form: 200-500mg; Nano/liposomal: 100-250mg (enhanced absorption); with 5-10mg [piperine](/reference/piperine/) as bioenhancer

**Optimal Timing:** With meals containing fat for maximum absorption; with piperine source ([black pepper](/herb/black-pepper/)); divided doses (2-3x daily) preferred over single large dose; with warm milk (traditional Haldi Doodh) for lipid-based delivery

**Storage:** Protect from light (photodegradation is the primary stability concern). Amber containers or opaque packaging mandatory. Cool, dry conditions below 25°C. Avoid alkaline contact during storage. Nitrogen-flush capsule bottles to prevent oxidation.

**Marker Compounds:** Curcumin (diferuloylmethane), [Demethoxycurcumin](/glossary/compounds-c-d/#demethoxycurcumin), [Bisdemethoxycurcumin](/glossary/compounds-a-c/#bisdemethoxycurcumin), Ar-[turmerone](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#turmerone), Alpha-turmerone, Beta-turmerone, Curcumol, Cyclocurcumin

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Piperine ([Black Pepper](/herb/black-pepper/)), the definitive bioavailability synergy (2000% enhancement)
- With Boswellia for anti-inflammatory synergy (joint health)
- With [Ashwagandha](/herb/ashwagandha/) for stress and inflammation
- With [Quercetin](/reference/quercetinquercetin/) for enhanced anti-inflammatory action
- With [Ginger](/herb/ginger/) (Shunthi) for digestive and anti-inflammatory synergy
- With Omega-3 fatty acids for neuroinflammation
