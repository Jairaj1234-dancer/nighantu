## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Capsule, Lepa (paste for external use), Taila (medicated oil)

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3 g powder twice daily; 30-60 mL decoction twice daily; external application as needed

**Bioavailability:** Martynoside and other phenylethanoid [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) show moderate oral bioavailability (~20-30%) with intestinal hydrolysis by beta-glucosidases releasing the active aglycone. Iridoid glycosides undergo gut microbial activation. [Flavonoids](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/flavonoids/) are moderately absorbed with typical Phase II conjugation. External application (Lepa/Taila) provides local anti-inflammatory and [analgesic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/analgesic/) activity through transdermal absorption of lipophilic [terpenoids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-t-z/#terpenoids).

**Optimal Timing:** After meals for internal use; external application at night for joint/musculoskeletal conditions

**Standardized Extract:** Hydroethanolic extract standardized to >2% total [iridoids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#iridoids) (by UV spectrophotometry) and >1.5% total flavonoids (as [luteolin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#luteolin) equivalent)

**Shelf Life:** Powder: 12-18 months; Capsules: 24 months; Medicated oil: 18-24 months; Decoction: prepare fresh

**Storage:** Airtight containers at 15-25 deg C, protected from light and moisture. Medicated oil in amber glass bottles.

**Marker Compounds:** Martynoside, Luteolin, [Apigenin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#apigenin), Ursolic acid, beta-[Sitosterol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), Chlorogenic acid, Iridoid glycosides

### Extraction Methods

- Aqueous decoction
- Hydroethanolic extraction (50-70% ethanol)
- Methanol extraction for iridoid-rich fraction
- Traditional Taila Paka for medicated oil
- Cold maceration
