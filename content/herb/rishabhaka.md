## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory mechanism involving inhibition of COX and LOX [enzymes](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#enzymes) by triterpenoid saponins, with novel saponin isolates showing dose-dependent activity
- [Antidiabetic](/reference/antidiabetic/) action through alpha-glucosidase and alpha-amylase inhibition by flavonoid constituents ([quercetin](/reference/quercetinquercetin/), myricetin), reducing postprandial [glucose](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#glucose) absorption
- Immunostimulatory mechanism involving activation of macrophages and enhancement of phagocytic activity, supporting its traditional use as a tonic

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- [Aphrodisiac](/glossary/pharmacology/#aphrodisiac) and tonic: traditionally used in [Vajikarana](/glossary/pharmacology/#vajikarana). Preclinical studies have reported [adaptogenic](/reference/adaptogenic/) effects.
- Traditionally used in infectious and dental conditions. Studies have reported antibacterial activity against several pathogenic strains.
- Traditionally used for digestive disorders. Animal studies have reported antiulcer and gastroprotective activity.

## Recent safety updates

- Fruits are edible and consumed traditionally, suggesting a favorable safety profile for fruit extracts; bark and root extracts require further toxicological evaluation
- No significant adverse effects reported in available preclinical studies at therapeutic doses; however, comprehensive human safety data is lacking

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Vati (tablet), Capsule, Asava/Arishta (fermented preparation)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g churna per day in divided doses; 50-100 mL kwatha twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** Take with warm milk or ghee after meals for enhanced absorption; morning dose for strength-building (Balya) applications

**Storage:** Store below 25 deg C in airtight containers, protected from light and moisture. Relative humidity <60%.

**Marker Compounds:** Beta-sitosterol, Taraxerol, Quercetin, Myricetin, Protobassic acid, Saponins (total)

### Synergistic Combinations

- [Ashwagandha](/herb/ashwagandha/) (adaptogenic synergy)
- [Shatavari](/herb/shatavari/) (reproductive tonic combination)
- [Vidari](/herb/vidari/) (anabolic synergy for Balya formulations)
