## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Casein [proteins](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#proteins) (alpha-s1, alpha-s2, beta, kappa casein)
- Whey proteins: [lactoferrin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#lactoferrin) (7.3%), [immunoglobulins](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#immunoglobulins) (17.1%), beta-lactoglobulin, alpha-lactalbumin
- A2 beta-casein (in indigenous Indian breeds like Gir, Sahiwal)
- Saturated and unsaturated fatty acids, conjugated linoleic acid (CLA)
- Calcium, [phosphorus](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#phosphorus), [potassium](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#potassium), [magnesium](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#magnesium)
- [Vitamins](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-t-z/#vitamins) A, D, B2 ([riboflavin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#riboflavin)), B12
- Bioactive [peptides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#peptides) (opioid, [antihypertensive](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#antihypertensive), [immunomodulatory](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/immunomodulatory/))
- Lactose and oligosaccharides

## How does it work?

- Ojas enhancement: Rich bioactive peptides, immunoglobulins, and lactoferrin strengthen innate immunity and provide [antimicrobial](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antimicrobial/) defense
- Tridosha balancing (esp. Vata-Pitta): Cooling potency and sweet taste pacify Pitta, unctuous heavy qualities ground Vata; proper processing prevents Kapha aggravation
- [Rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/rasayana/) (rejuvenation): Complete amino acid profile, calcium, and growth factors support tissue regeneration and dhatu nourishment across all seven tissues
- Shodhana (detoxification) vehicle: Polar and non-polar dissolving capacity enables effective toxin extraction in Ayurvedic purification processes

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Rasayana (rejuvenation therapy): Confirmed by modern evidence of immunoglobulin, lactoferrin, and growth factor content supporting tissue repair and immune function
- Insomnia and mental health (Nidrajanana): Tryptophan content and opioid peptides from casein digestion validated as promoting sleep and calming the nervous system
- Vehicle for herbal medicines (Anupana): Scientific evidence supports that milk proteins and fats enhance bioavailability of lipophilic herbal compounds

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **A2 milk consumption and its health benefits: an update** (2024): A2 milk (from indigenous cow breeds) avoids BCM-7 production during digestion, reducing gastrointestinal discomfort, systemic inflammation, and potentially lowering risk of type-1 diabetes and cardiovascular disease compared to A1 milk.
- **Rationalized approach towards milk consumption integrating Ayurveda principles and recent advances for health promotion** (2024): Integrating Ayurvedic principles of milk processing (boiling with specific herbs, proper timing) with modern understanding enhances bioavailability and reduces potential adverse effects of modern dairy production methods.
- **Medicinal Potential of Milk: A Meta-Analysis of Bioactive Compounds** (2025): Meta-analysis confirms cow milk bioactive compounds including lactoferrin and immunoglobulins demonstrate antimicrobial, antiviral, and immunomodulatory activities, though clinical evidence in humans remains limited compared to in vitro and animal studies.

## Recent safety updates

- A1 beta-casein milk (from Holstein-Friesian breeds) may release BCM-7, linked to gastrointestinal inflammation, exacerbation of lactose intolerance symptoms, and potential cardiovascular risks
- Lactose intolerance affects 60-70% of the global adult population; raw milk consumption carries risk of Brucella, Salmonella, and E. coli infections
- Modern adulteration with hormones (rBST), antibiotics, and pesticides is a significant safety concern; organic and indigenous breed milk recommended

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Fresh milk, Boiled milk, Kshirapaka (medicated milk)

**Standard Dosage:** 100–250 mL twice daily

**Bioavailability:** High; contains A1 and A2 casein variants; A2 preferred in Ayurveda

**Optimal Timing:** Morning or evening; warm with spices ([turmeric](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/turmeric/), cardamom)

**Standardized Extract:** N/A — whole food vehicle

**Shelf Life:** Fresh: 24–48 hours refrigerated; boiled: 48–72 hours

**Storage:** Refrigerated at 4°C

**Marker Compounds:** Casein proteins, Whey proteins, Calcium, Lactoferrin, Immunoglobulins

**Quality Parameters:** Fat >3.5%, SNF >8.5%, bacterial count <2×10^5/mL, no adulteration

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Most common anupana in Ayurveda; vehicle for bhasmas, Rasayanas, and [Vajikarana](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#vajikarana) drugs
