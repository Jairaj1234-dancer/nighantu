## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Tablet (with excipients to prevent sublimation), Taila (camphorated oil), Balm/Ointment, Inhalation preparation, Lepa (paste), [Arka](/nighantu/herb/arka/) (distillate)

**Standard Dosage:** Internal: 60-125 mg with appropriate Anupana (honey, ghee); External: 2-10% concentration in oil/ointment base; Inhalation: 2-5% in steam

**Bioavailability:** [Camphor](/nighantu/herb/camphor/) is rapidly and almost completely absorbed through all routes — oral, inhalation, and transdermal. Oral bioavailability >90%. Rapidly metabolized hepatically to hydroxycamphor and glucuronic acid conjugates. Peak plasma levels in 15-30 minutes. Narrow therapeutic index; doses above 500 mg may cause toxicity. Transdermal delivery through lipophilic bases provides sustained local effects.

**Optimal Timing:** External applications: as needed, 2-4 times daily; inhalation: before bed for respiratory congestion; internal: after meals under medical supervision only

**Standardized Extract:** Natural camphor: minimum 96% d-camphor (dextrorotatory) by GC; camphor oil standardized to 50-65% camphor with [cineole](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-c-d/#cineole) and [safrole](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#safrole) content <0.1%

**Shelf Life:** 12 months for tablets (sublimation loss); 36 months for oil preparations; 24 months for balms/ointments in sealed containers

**Storage:** Store in airtight, hermetically sealed containers at 15-25°C. Camphor sublimes readily — loose containers will lose content. Keep away from heat, flame (highly flammable), and children. Double containment for bulk camphor storage.

**Marker Compounds:** d-Camphor (natural), dl-Camphor (synthetic), [Borneol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-a-c/#borneol), [Camphene](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-a-c/#camphene), 1,8-Cineole, Alpha-pinene, [Linalool](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#linalool)

### Extraction Methods

- Steam distillation from Cinnamomum camphora wood
- Sublimation and resublimation for purification
- Fractional distillation of camphor oil
- Synthetic production from alpha-pinene (turpentine-derived — must be labeled if synthetic)

### Synergistic Combinations

- [Menthol](/nighantu/herb/menthol/) for cooling [analgesic](/nighantu/reference/analgesic/) balms
- Nilgiri Taila ([Eucalyptus](/nighantu/herb/eucalyptus/) oil) for respiratory decongestant
- [Ajwain](/nighantu/herb/ajwain/) for topical pain relief
- Kapoor Kachri for aromatic medicinal preparations
- Gandhapura Taila (Wintergreen oil) for counter-irritant formulations
