## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (seed powder), Hima (cold infusion), Kashayam (decoction), Essential oil, Tablet, Capsule, Dhanyakadi compound formulations

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g seed powder; 50-100ml cold infusion (Sheeta Kashayam); 500mg extract capsule; essential oil: 1-3 drops; seeds freely as culinary spice

**Bioavailability:** [Linalool](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#linalool) (primary essential oil component, 60-80% of oil) shows excellent oral and dermal bioavailability (>70%). Flavonoid [glycosides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) ([rutin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#rutin), [quercetin](/nighantu/reference/quercetinquercetin/)) show moderate oral bioavailability (20-30%). Cold infusion (Hima/Sheeta Kashayam) is the classical method for this Pitta-pacifying herb—preserves volatile aromatics better than hot decoction. Seed [mucilage](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#mucilage) provides mild sustained-release characteristics.

**Optimal Timing:** Cold infusion early morning for Pitta conditions; with meals as digestive; after meals for bloating and flatulence; anytime as cooling beverage

**Standardized Extract:** Essential oil standardized to linalool NLT 65% (European type) or NLT 45% (Indian type); seed extract standardized to total [flavonoids](/nighantu/reference/flavonoids/) NLT 3%. Oleoresin with volatile oil NLT 15%.

**Shelf Life:** 3 years (whole seeds); 1 year (powder—volatile oil loss); 3 years (essential oil); 3 years (capsule/tablet)

**Storage:** Whole seeds in airtight containers (very stable). Ground coriander loses aroma quickly—use within months and store airtight. Essential oil in amber glass, cool storage. Cool, dry conditions.

**Marker Compounds:** Linalool, Geranyl acetate, Alpha-pinene, Gamma-terpinene, [Camphor](/nighantu/herb/camphor/) (trace), Rutin, Quercetin, [Kaempferol](/nighantu/reference/kaempferol/), Chlorogenic acid, Caffeic acid

### Extraction Methods

- Steam distillation for essential oil (yield 0.5-1.0%)
- Cold infusion (Hima): soak crushed seeds in cold water overnight
- Aqueous decoction for Kashayam
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (60:40) for flavonoid-rich extract
- Supercritical CO2 for premium oleoresin
- Hydrodistillation for complete volatile profile

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Cumin](/nighantu/herb/cumin/) and [Fennel](/nighantu/herb/fennel/) ([Dhanyaka](/nighantu/herb/dhanyaka/)-[Jeeraka](/nighantu/herb/jeeraka/)-Saunf) for digestive triad formulations
- With [Guduchi](/nighantu/herb/guduchi/) for fever management (Guduchyadi Kashayam)
- With Amalaki for Pitta pacification
- With [Mishreya](/nighantu/herb/mishreya/) (fennel) for digestive comfort
- With Sunthi for appetite stimulation
