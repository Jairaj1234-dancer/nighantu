## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (seed powder), Hima (cold infusion), Kashayam (decoction), Essential oil, Tablet, Capsule, Dhanyakadi compound formulations

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g seed powder; 50-100ml cold infusion (Sheeta Kashayam); 500mg extract capsule; essential oil: 1-3 drops; seeds freely as culinary spice

**Optimal Timing:** Cold infusion early morning for Pitta conditions; with meals as digestive; after meals for bloating and flatulence; anytime as cooling beverage

**Storage:** Whole seeds in airtight containers (very stable). Ground coriander loses aroma quickly, use within months and store airtight. Essential oil in amber glass, cool storage. Cool, dry conditions.

**Marker Compounds:** Linalool, Geranyl acetate, Alpha-pinene, Gamma-terpinene, [Camphor](/herb/camphor/) (trace), Rutin, Quercetin, [Kaempferol](/reference/kaempferol/), Chlorogenic acid, Caffeic acid

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Cumin](/herb/cumin/) and [Fennel](/herb/fennel/) ([Dhanyaka](/herb/dhanyaka/)-[Jeeraka](/herb/jeeraka/)-Saunf) for digestive triad formulations
- With [Guduchi](/herb/guduchi/), traditionally used for fever (Guduchyadi Kashayam)
- With Amalaki for Pitta pacification
- With Sunthi for appetite stimulation
