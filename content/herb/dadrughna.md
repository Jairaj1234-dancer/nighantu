## Names and identification

| Language | Name |
|----------|------|
| English | Dadrughna |

Dadrughna is a Sanskrit synonym of [Chakramarda](/herb/chakramarda/) (Cassia tora L., syn. Senna tora) in Bhavaprakasha Nighantu and Raja Nighantu (4.198-200); see that page for the fuller profile.

## Ayurvedic pharmacology (Dravyaguna)

| Property | Value | Description |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| **Rasa** (Taste) | Katu | Primary taste |
| **Guna** (Quality) | Laghu, Ruksha | Physical quality |
| **Virya** (Potency) | Ushna | Heating/Cooling effect |
| **Vipaka** (Post-digestive) | Katu | Post-digestive effect |

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Lepa (topical paste), Taila (medicated oil), Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction for external wash), Ointment

**Standard Dosage:** External use: paste applied to affected area 2-3 times daily; Internal: 3-5g powder twice daily with honey or warm water (limited internal use data)

**Optimal Timing:** Topical application after bathing on clean, dry skin; internal use before meals if prescribed

**Storage:** Cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Topical preparations stored below 30 deg C. Fresh paste to be used within 24 hours of preparation.

**Marker Compounds:** Total phenolics (gallic acid equivalents), [Flavonoids](/reference/flavonoids/), [Anthraquinones](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#anthraquinones) (where applicable)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Karanja](/herb/karanja/) (Pongamia) oil for enhanced antifungal action
- With [Haridra](/herb/haridra/) ([turmeric](/herb/turmeric/)) for anti-inflammatory and [antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) synergy
- With [Nimba](/herb/nimba/) ([neem](/herb/neem/)) for broad-spectrum dermatological use
