## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Lepa (topical paste), Taila (medicated oil), Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction for external wash), Ointment

**Standard Dosage:** External use: paste applied to affected area 2-3 times daily; Internal: 3-5g powder twice daily with honey or warm water (limited internal use data)

**Bioavailability:** Primarily used topically; percutaneous absorption of antifungal constituents depends on vehicle and skin integrity. Oil-based formulations (Taila) enhance dermal penetration of lipophilic actives. For internal use, limited pharmacokinetic data available. Traditional practice emphasizes external application. Enhancement: incorporation into liposomal gels or nanostructured lipid carriers for improved dermal delivery.

**Optimal Timing:** Topical application after bathing on clean, dry skin; internal use before meals if prescribed

**Standardized Extract:** Aqueous or hydroalcoholic extract standardized by TLC fingerprint and total phenolic content (NLT 3% as gallic acid equivalent). Extract ratio 5:1 typical.

**Shelf Life:** 1 year (powder); 1.5 years (ointment); 2 years (Taila/medicated oil)

**Storage:** Cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Topical preparations stored below 30 deg C. Fresh paste to be used within 24 hours of preparation.

**Marker Compounds:** Total phenolics (gallic acid equivalents), [Flavonoids](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/flavonoids/), [Anthraquinones](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#anthraquinones) (where applicable)

### Extraction Methods

- Cold-pressed juice of fresh plant
- Aqueous paste (Kalka) preparation
- [Sesame oil](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila)-based Taila Paka (traditional oil processing)
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (70:30)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Karanja](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/karanja/) (Pongamia) oil for enhanced antifungal action
- With [Haridra](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/haridra/) ([turmeric](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/turmeric/)) for anti-inflammatory and [antimicrobial](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antimicrobial/) synergy
- With [Nimba](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/nimba/) ([neem](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/neem/)) for broad-spectrum dermatological use
- With [Chakramarda](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/chakramarda/) (Cassia tora) for ringworm treatment
