## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Sodium borate (Na2B4O7) in anhydrous/calcined form
- Boron oxide (B2O3) from thermal decomposition
- Sodium oxide (Na2O)
- Trace minerals from herbal processing media
- Residual organic compounds from milk/herbal trituration

## How does it work?

- Kaphahara (Kapha-reducing) action through mucolytic and [expectorant](/reference/expectorant/) properties, breaking down mucus viscosity and facilitating respiratory clearance
- Anti-inflammatory effect through suppression of TNF-alpha and IL-6 inflammatory cytokines, reducing respiratory tract inflammation
- Kshara (alkaline) action, described in Ayurvedic doctrine as acting through pH modulation in digestive and urinary systems
- Emmenagogue action through stimulation of uterine contractions and menstrual flow regulation; contraindicated in pregnancy

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Respiratory disorders (Kasa, Shwasa): traditionally used for cough and bronchitis. Animal studies have reported anti-inflammatory activity, with decreased TNF-alpha and IL-6.
- Skin disorders and acne: traditionally applied topically for skin disorders and acne. A dermatological study reported reduced acne severity.
- Dysmenorrhea (Kashtartava): traditionally used for its emmenagogue action, based on traditional clinical observation. It is contraindicated in pregnancy.

## Recent safety updates

- PREGNANCY CONTRAINDICATED: Tankan Bhasma can cross the placental barrier. POSSIBLY UNSAFE in breastfeeding.
- MALE FERTILITY CAUTION: Long-term use (>2 months) in men seeking fertility treatment may cause oligospermia (low sperm count) due to Kshara properties
- BORON ACCUMULATION: Gaps exist in long-term safety data regarding boron accumulation in the body. Periodic monitoring recommended.

### Mineral/Elemental Profile

- **Primary component:** Calcined mineral/metal oxide (after Shodhana and Marana)
- **Particle size:** Sub-micron to nanoparticle range
- **Note:** Exact composition depends on source material and preparation method

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Shodhan Tankana (purified borax), Bhasma

**Standard Dosage:** 125–250 mg

**Optimal Timing:** With honey for respiratory conditions; with warm water for other uses

**Storage:** Airtight, moisture-proof container

**Marker Compounds:** Sodium tetraborate, Boron compounds, Sodium oxide (post-calcination)

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Honey for Kapha conditions; warm water
