## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Fresh root (culinary/medicinal), Churna (dried root powder), Swarasa (fresh juice), Tablet, Capsule, Avaleha

**Standard Dosage:** Fresh root: 50-100g daily in diet; Dried powder: 3-6g twice daily; 50-100ml fresh juice; 500mg extract capsule twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Daucus carota (carrot / Grinjana) root contains [carotenoids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-a-c/#carotenoids) (beta-carotene, alpha-carotene, [lutein](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#lutein)), [polyacetylenes](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polyacetylenes) (falcarinol, [falcarindiol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#falcarindiol)), and [anthocyanins](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#anthocyanins) (in purple varieties). Beta-carotene bioavailability is highly dependent on food matrix and fat content: raw carrot (~3% absorption) vs cooked with oil (~30-40%). Carotenoids are fat-soluble - co-administration with lipids (ghee, oil) dramatically enhances absorption. Traditional Ayurvedic practice of cooking carrots with ghee aligns with modern bioavailability science. Polyacetylenes have moderate oral bioavailability. Enhancement: cooking and pureeing disrupts cell walls releasing carotenoids; oil-based formulations; nano-emulsions.

**Optimal Timing:** With meals containing fat/oil for maximum carotenoid absorption; fresh juice in the morning; Avaleha after meals as nutritive tonic

**Standardized Extract:** Carrot extract standardized to NLT 10% total carotenoids (as beta-carotene) by HPLC. Cold-pressed carrot seed oil: rich in carotol and daucol. Freeze-dried carrot powder: beta-carotene NLT 200 ppm.

**Shelf Life:** Fresh root: 2-4 weeks refrigerated; Dried powder: 1.5 years (protect from light - carotenoid degradation); Capsule: 2 years; Juice: 7 days refrigerated, 6 months pasteurized sealed

**Storage:** Fresh: refrigerate at 2-8 deg C. Dried powder and extracts: airtight, opaque containers below 25 deg C - carotenoids degrade rapidly on light exposure. Nitrogen-flush packaging recommended. Seed oil: refrigerate after opening.

**Marker Compounds:** Beta-carotene, Alpha-carotene, Lutein, Falcarinol, Falcarindiol, Carotol (seed oil), Anthocyanins (purple carrot varieties)

### Extraction Methods

- Fresh juice expression (Swarasa)
- Hot water extraction for Kashayam
- Supercritical CO2 for carotenoid-rich extract
- Oil-based extraction for fat-soluble carotenoids
- Freeze-drying for preservation of labile nutrients
- Ethanol extraction for polyacetylene fraction

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) (ghee) for maximum carotenoid absorption
- With Amalaki for [antioxidant](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antioxidant/) synergy (vitamin A + C)
- With [Shatavari](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/shatavari/) for nutritive and [Rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/rasayana/) combination
- With [Yashtimadhu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/yashtimadhu/) for eye health formulation
