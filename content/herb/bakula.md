## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory action via triterpenoid-mediated (betulinic acid) inhibition of 5-LOX and COX-2 pathways
- Studies of betulinic acid (a constituent) in cancer cells have reported [anticancer](/reference/anticancer/) activity through mitochondrial apoptosis and caspase cascade activation
- [Anxiolytic](/reference/anxiolytic/) and anticonvulsant effects via GABAergic receptor modulation by bark alkaloid fractions
- Reported [antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) activity against Streptococcus mutans and astringent tannin effects on gingival tissue, which may support oral health

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Traditionally used for dental and gum health. Studies have reported antimicrobial activity against S. mutans and periodontal pathogens.
- Animal studies in rats have reported anti-ulcer activity in aspirin- and ethanol-induced gastric ulcer models.
- Traditionally used as a heart tonic (Hridya). Studies in isoproterenol-induced myocardial infarction models have reported [cardioprotective](/reference/cardioprotective/) effects.
- Traditionally used in snakebite first aid. Preliminary studies have reported anti-venom and anti-inflammatory activity.

## Recent safety updates

- Bark and flower decoctions are traditionally used for dental care and are traditionally considered well-tolerated
- Formal human clinical safety trials are limited; acute toxicity studies in rodents indicate LD50 > 2000 mg/kg for most extracts

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Kwath (decoction concentrate), Manjan (tooth powder), Taila (oil), Arishta (fermented preparation)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 50-100ml decoction; bark powder 1-3g for dental use

**Optimal Timing:** After meals for general use; as gargle/mouthwash after meals for dental applications; decoction on empty stomach for uterine tonification

**Storage:** Cool, dry place away from light and moisture. Flower preparations require airtight amber containers.

**Marker Compounds:** Mimusopside, Quercetin, Ursolic acid, Beta-[sitosterol](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), Gallic acid, Lupeol, Mi-saponin A

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Khadira](/herb/khadira/) (Acacia catechu) for oral health formulations
- With [Lodhra](/herb/lodhra/) for astringent gynecological preparations
- With [Yashtimadhu](/herb/yashtimadhu/) for throat and gum care
- With [Jatiphala](/herb/jatiphala/) for dental pain relief
