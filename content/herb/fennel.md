## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Essential oil, Capsule (oil or extract), Tablet, Gripe water (infant formulation), Tea/infusion, Tincture, [Arka](/herb/arka/) (distillate)

**Standard Dosage:** Essential oil: 0.2 mL daily, as a single dose or in divided doses (EMEA/HMPC Community herbal monograph on bitter fennel fruit oil, 2007); seed: 1-4g as tea/infusion 2-3 times daily; tincture: 2-5ml three times daily; gripe water: as directed for infants

**Optimal Timing:** After meals for digestive support; fennel tea throughout the day as a gentle carminative; gripe water for infants after feeding

**Storage:** Whole seeds: airtight containers at room temperature. Essential oil: amber glass above 25 deg C (to prevent crystallization) in sealed containers. Ground seeds: nitrogen-flushed, sealed containers, use promptly. Protect from light.

**Marker Compounds:** trans-Anethole, Fenchone, Estragole (methylchavicol) - monitored for safety, [Limonene](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#limonene), Alpha-pinene, Rosmarinic acid, [Quercetin](/reference/quercetinquercetin/)-3-glucuronide

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Jeeraka](/herb/jeeraka/) ([cumin](/herb/cumin/)) and [Dhanyaka](/herb/dhanyaka/) ([coriander](/herb/coriander/)) for digestive triad
- With Mishri (sugar candy) as traditional mouth freshener and digestive
- With Sunthi for enhanced carminative action
- With [Yashtimadhu](/herb/yashtimadhu/) for gastric soothing
- With [Shatavari](/herb/shatavari/) for [galactagogue](/glossary/pharmacology/#galactagogue) (lactation support) combination
