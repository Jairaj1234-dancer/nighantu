## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- [Alkaloids](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids)

- [Anthraquinones](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#anthraquinones)
- [Terpenoids](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#terpenoids)
- Lysine and serine (amino acids)
- Aspartic acid
- Phytohormones

## How does it work?

- Hypoglycemic activity through enhancement of insulin secretion and improvement of [glucose](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#glucose) uptake by peripheral tissues, mediated by saponin fraction
- Anti-inflammatory and [analgesic](/reference/analgesic/) mechanisms involving inhibition of lipoxygenase pathway and reduction of pro-inflammatory mediators
- Bronchodilatory action through tracheorelaxant effect on bronchial smooth muscle, with [antispasmodic](/glossary/pharmacology/#antispasmodic) properties mediated by calcium channel modulation

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Animal studies using Plasmodium models and fever-inducing models have reported antimalarial and [antipyretic](/reference/antipyretic/) activity.
- Traditionally used in urinary disorders and as a body purifier. Experimental studies have reported [diuretic](/reference/diuretic/) activity.
- Traditionally used for infections. Laboratory studies have reported antibacterial and antifungal activity against several disease-causing organisms.

## Recent safety updates

- Classical literature does not document adverse effects when Meda is used as part of Ashtavarga formulations; modern safety data are not established
- Endangered/critically endangered status means most commercial products may contain adulterants or substitutes; authentication and quality control are essential safety considerations

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Capsules, Tablets, [Ghrita](/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee), Avaleha (confection), Lehya

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g tuber powder per day; 50-100 mL decoction twice daily; 500-1000 mg extract capsules daily

**Optimal Timing:** With meals or with milk for [Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/) and nutritive effects; morning for tonic action; with Ghrita for reproductive health

**Storage:** Fresh tubers at 5-10 deg C. Dried material below 25 deg C in airtight containers with desiccant due to hygroscopic mucilage. Ghrita in glass containers at room temperature. Protect from light and excessive heat.

**Marker Compounds:** Diosgenin, Polygonatum saponins, Convallarin, Convallamarin, Polysaccharides (fructans), Beta-sitosterol, [Stigmasterol](/reference/stigmasterol/)
