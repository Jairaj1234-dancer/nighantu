## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Calcium (66%) as primary constituent (EDAX analysis of one bhasma sample, World Journal of Ayurveda Science, 2022; EDAX is a semi-quantitative surface method)
- [Phosphorus](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#phosphorus) (32.46%)
- [Potassium](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#potassium) (1.41%)
- [Magnesium](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#magnesium) and carbonates
- Sulfates, chlorides, [tannins](/reference/tannins/), and residual [proteins](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#proteins)

## How does it work?

- Supplies calcium and phosphorus in a natural Ca:P ratio (approximately 2:1)
- Respiratory tract support through mucolytic and anti-inflammatory actions
- [Cardioprotective](/reference/cardioprotective/) action through calcium and potassium provision supporting cardiac muscle contraction, electrical conduction, and rhythm regulation

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Respiratory infections (pneumonia, bronchitis, influenza): traditionally used for these conditions. This traditional use is extensively documented.
- Bone disorders and osteoporosis: traditionally used for Asthi Kshaya. An animal study reported an optimal Ca:P ratio and a protective effect against osteoporosis.
- Cardiac conditions (Hritshula - cardiac pain): traditionally used for angina pectoris and cardiac pain. This use is documented since the Samhita period.

## What do recent studies show?

[An ayurvedic proprietary herbal preparation, Calci-7, prevents ovariectomy-induced osteoporosis in rats](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29491679/). *Ayu*.   Calci-7 formulation containing [Mrigashringa Bhasma](/herb/mrigashringa-bhasma/) prevented ovariectomy-induced osteoporosis in rat models. Demonstrated bone-protective effects through calcium and phosphorus supplementation.

## Recent safety updates

- WILDLIFE CONSERVATION: Only naturally shed antlers should be used. Ethical sourcing important under CITES and wildlife protection regulations.
- HYPERCALCEMIA RISK: High calcium content means overdosage can cause hypercalcemia. Dose monitoring essential for prolonged use.
