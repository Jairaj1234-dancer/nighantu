## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Svarasa (fresh juice), Capsule, Tablet, Syrup, [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) ([Brahmi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/brahmi/) Ghrita), Tincture, Standardized extract capsule

**Standard Dosage:** 3-5 g powder twice daily; 10-20 mL fresh juice daily; 300-450 mg standardized extract (20-50% bacosides) twice daily; 1-2 teaspoons Brahmi Ghrita

**Bioavailability:** Bacosides A and B (primary triterpenoid [saponins](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/saponins/)) have moderate oral bioavailability (~25-35%) limited by P-glycoprotein efflux and hepatic metabolism. Bacoside A3, bacopaside I, II, and X are the specific active saponins. Bioavailability is enhanced 1.5-2x by lipid co-administration (ghee, [phospholipids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#phospholipids)). Brahmi Ghrita (traditional ghee-based formulation) provides superior bacoside delivery through lipid solubilization. Bacosides cross the blood-brain barrier, which is essential for [nootropic](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#nootropic) activity. Tmax: 2-4 hours; elimination half-life: 6-8 hours.

**Optimal Timing:** Morning and evening with meals (reduces GI side effects); Brahmi Ghrita at bedtime for cognitive [Rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/rasayana/) effect; allow 8-12 weeks for nootropic effects to manifest

**Standardized Extract:** Standardized extract: 20-55% total bacosides (as bacoside A + bacopaside I) by HPLC; KeenMind: 55% bacosides; BacoMind: 25% bacosides (9 bioactives); fresh juice standardized to >2% total bacosides

**Shelf Life:** Fresh juice: 24-48 hours refrigerated; Powder: 12-18 months; Standardized extract capsules: 24-36 months; Brahmi Ghrita: 24 months; Tincture: 36 months

**Storage:** Standardized extract in airtight, light-resistant containers at 15-25 deg C. Fresh plant material refrigerated at 4-8 deg C. Brahmi Ghrita in glass jars at room temperature. Bacosides are moderately photosensitive; opaque packaging preferred.

**Marker Compounds:** Bacoside A3, Bacopaside I, Bacopaside II, Bacopaside X, Bacopasaponin C, [Jujubogenin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#jujubogenin), Pseudojujubogenin, [Apigenin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#apigenin), [Luteolin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#luteolin), Brahmine, Herpestine

### Extraction Methods

- Hydroethanolic extraction (50-70% ethanol) for bacoside-rich extract
- Aqueous extraction/decoction
- Fresh juice expression
- Traditional Ghrita Paka (ghee processing)
- Supercritical CO2 extraction
- Spray drying for standardized powder
