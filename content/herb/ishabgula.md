## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Seed powder, Churna (powder), Capsule, Decoction

**Standard Dosage:** 5-10g seed powder with adequate water; 3-6g seed decoction

**Optimal Timing:** With meals or 30 minutes before meals with a full glass of water

**Storage:** Cool, dry place below 25°C, protected from moisture. Whole seeds more stable than powder. Oil must be stored in dark, sealed containers under nitrogen.

**Marker Compounds:** Mucilage [polysaccharides](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polysaccharides), Aucubin, Planteose, Linoleic acid (in seed oil fraction)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Yashtimadhu](/herb/yashtimadhu/) for GI mucosal protection
- With [Ajwain](/herb/ajwain/) for digestive comfort
- With [Draksha](/herb/draksha/) for mild [laxative](/reference/laxative/) action
