## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Tablet, Capsule, Component of compound formulations

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3g powder twice daily; 50-100ml decoction; 500mg extract capsule. Pungent herb—start with lower doses.

**Bioavailability:** Piper chaba (syn. P. retrofractum)—closely related to [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/). [Piperine](/nighantu/reference/piperine/) and [piperlongumine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#piperlongumine) show excellent oral bioavailability (>85%). Self-bioenhancing herb through CYP3A4 and P-gp inhibition. Rapid GI absorption of piperine [alkaloids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids). Thermogenic effect increases splanchnic blood flow and absorption of co-administered compounds. Essentially functions as a bioenhancer (Yogavahi) like [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/).

**Optimal Timing:** Before or with meals for digestive enhancement; as bioenhancer taken concurrently with target formulation

**Standardized Extract:** Fruit extract standardized to piperine NLT 5%; total alkaloids NLT 8%. Oleoresin with piperine NLT 30%.

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (powder); 3 years (capsule/tablet); 3 years (extract)

**Storage:** Airtight containers away from light. Cool, dry conditions. Separate from less pungent herbs during storage to prevent aroma transfer.

**Marker Compounds:** Piperine, Piperlongumine, [Pipernonaline](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#pipernonaline), Retrofractamide A-D, [Chavicine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-c-d/#chavicine), Beta-caryophyllene

### Extraction Methods

- Hydroalcoholic extraction (80:20) for piperine enrichment
- Ethanol extraction (95%) for alkaloid profiling
- Aqueous decoction for traditional use
- Supercritical CO2 for piperine-rich oleoresin

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Shunthi and Maricha as [Trikatu](/nighantu/herb/trikatu/) variant for digestive enhancement
- With Chitraka for Agni (digestive fire) stimulation
- With [Haridra](/nighantu/herb/haridra/)—enhances [curcumin](/nighantu/herb/curcumin/) bioavailability (piperine effect)
- With [Guduchi](/nighantu/herb/guduchi/) for immunomodulation with enhanced absorption
