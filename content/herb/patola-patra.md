## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Swarasa (fresh leaf juice), Kwatha (decoction), Churna (leaf powder), Capsule, Tablet, Lepa (paste)

**Standard Dosage:** 10-15 mL fresh leaf juice daily; 2-4 g dried leaf powder daily; 15-20 mL decoction twice daily; 500 mg leaf extract twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Leaf-specific [flavonoids](/nighantu/reference/flavonoids/) ([quercetin](/nighantu/reference/quercetinquercetin/), [kaempferol](/nighantu/reference/kaempferol/) [glycosides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides)) from Trichosanthes dioica leaves show moderate bioavailability (15-30%). [Chlorophyll](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-c-d/#chlorophyll)-associated compounds require bile salt emulsification for absorption. Leaf [saponins](/nighantu/reference/saponins/) have better bioavailability than fruit saponins due to lower molecular weight profiles. Ascorbic acid from fresh leaves is rapidly absorbed (>80%).

**Optimal Timing:** Fresh juice: morning on empty stomach for blood purification. Decoction: between meals. Leaf powder supplements: after meals with warm water.

**Standardized Extract:** [Patola](/nighantu/herb/patola/) leaf extract (10:1) standardized to total flavonoids >4% (as quercetin equivalents), total phenolics >8% GAE, and ascorbic acid >1% (in fresh preparations).

**Shelf Life:** Fresh leaves: 1-2 days refrigerated; Fresh juice: 4-6 hours; Dried leaf powder: 12-18 months; Extract: 18-24 months; Capsules: 24 months

**Storage:** Fresh leaves: refrigerated 5-10 deg C in perforated bags. Dried powder: airtight, below 25 deg C, RH <50%, protected from light (to preserve chlorophyll). Extracts: amber containers, below 25 deg C.

**Marker Compounds:** Quercetin, Kaempferol, Gallic acid, Chlorogenic acid, Ascorbic acid, Beta-carotene, [Luteolin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#luteolin), Beta-[sitosterol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol)

### Extraction Methods

- Fresh leaf juice extraction by mechanical pressing
- Aqueous decoction
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50-60% ethanol)
- Shade-drying followed by powdering
- Methanol extraction for flavonoid profiling
