## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory: Rottlerin inhibits both COX-1 and COX-2 more potently than ibuprofen and etoricoxib; also inhibits protein kinase C-delta (PKC-delta)
- [Anticancer](/reference/anticancer/): Rottlerin induces apoptosis through inhibition of PKC-delta, NF-kB suppression, and activation of caspase-3/caspase-9 cascade
- Anthelmintic: [Kamala](/herb/kamala/) (pericarp powder) contains rottlerin and isorottlerin which damage tegumental membrane of cestodes, causing paralysis and death

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Krimighna (anthelmintic): traditionally used against tapeworms. In vitro and animal studies have reported activity against cestodes.
- Traditionally used for inflammation. Studies have reported COX-1 and COX-2 inhibition.
- Traditionally used for skin diseases. Studies have reported [antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) and antifungal activity of [Kamala](/herb/kamala/) extract against dermatophytes.

## Recent safety updates

- Safety data for pericarp preparations ([Kamala](/herb/kamala/)) at traditional anthelmintic doses are limited
- High doses may cause gastrointestinal irritation and purgation; not recommended during pregnancy; topical use of [Kamala](/herb/kamala/) dye may cause contact sensitization in rare cases

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Tablet, Capsule, Lepa (external paste), Taila (oil for external application)

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3 g powder with honey or warm water; for anthelmintic use: 3-6 g single dose; external application as needed

**Optimal Timing:** Early morning on empty stomach for anthelmintic use; external application any time as needed

**Storage:** Store in airtight, light-resistant containers at 15-30°C. Keep away from moisture. The red glandular hair powder is prone to caking if exposed to humidity.

**Marker Compounds:** Rottlerin (mallotoxin), Isorottlerin, Kamalin, Rottlerin dimethyl ether, Mallotophenone

### Synergistic Combinations

- [Vidanga](/herb/vidanga/) (Embelia ribes) for enhanced anthelmintic activity
- [Haritaki](/herb/haritaki/) for purgative and anthelmintic combination
- [Nimba](/herb/nimba/) ([Neem](/herb/neem/)) for parasitic infections
- [Karanja](/herb/karanja/) for dermatological applications
