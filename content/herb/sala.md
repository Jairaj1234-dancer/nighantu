## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory action via triterpenoid-mediated inhibition of NF-kB and reduction of pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-6)
- Wound healing through bergenin-mediated stimulation of collagen synthesis, fibroblast proliferation and angiogenesis
- [Antidiabetic](/reference/antidiabetic/) activity via alpha-amylase/alpha-glucosidase inhibition and enhancement of GLUT4 translocation by ursolic acid
- [Immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/) effects through modulation of macrophage activation and cytokine balance

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Traditionally used for wounds. Animal studies using excision wound models have reported faster wound closure and improved histological markers.
- Animal studies using carrageenan-induced paw edema and cotton pellet granuloma models have reported anti-inflammatory activity.
- [Antipyretic](/reference/antipyretic/) activity has been reported in animal studies using a Brewer's yeast-induced fever model in rodents.
- Laboratory studies have reported antibacterial activity against common wound pathogens, including S. aureus and E. coli.

## Recent safety updates

- Resin (sal dammar) has traditionally been used externally and internally; it is also used in food grade applications. Formal human safety data are limited.
- Seed oil is considered safe for consumption as an edible oil substitute; no reports of significant adverse effects. Formal clinical safety trials in humans remain limited.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Taila (medicated oil), Lepa (paste for external use), Ghana vati (solid extract tablet), Capsule

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g bark/resin powder per day; 50-100 mL kwatha twice daily; external application of resin paste as needed

**Optimal Timing:** After meals; externally as needed

**Storage:** Store below 25 deg C. Resin should be kept in airtight containers to prevent oxidation. Protect from direct sunlight. RH <55%.

**Marker Compounds:** Alpha-amyrin, Beta-amyrin, Oleanolic acid, Ursolic acid, Asiatic acid, Shorea-phenol
