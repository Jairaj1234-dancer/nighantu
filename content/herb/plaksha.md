## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory activity via inhibition of pro-inflammatory cytokines and COX [enzymes](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#enzymes) mediated by [flavonoids](/reference/flavonoids/) sorbifolin and scutellarein
- Proposed [antidiabetic](/reference/antidiabetic/) action through alpha-glucosidase inhibition and enhancement of insulin sensitivity by [triterpenoids](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#triterpenoids)
- Proposed wound-healing action through astringent tannin action, [antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) activity and promotion of collagen synthesis
- Immunostimulatory effects via modulation of macrophage activity and enhancement of phagocytic index

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Traditionally used for wounds and ulcers. Clinical and preclinical studies on non-healing ulcers have reported wound-healing and anti-ulcer activity.
- Traditionally used for inflammation and pain. Preclinical studies in carrageenan-induced paw edema models have reported anti-inflammatory and [analgesic](/reference/analgesic/) effects.
- Traditionally used for its [Hemostatic](/glossary/pharmacology/#hemostatic) (bleeding disorder) action. In vitro clotting studies have reported clotting activity, along with astringent tannin activity.
- Traditionally used for diarrhea. Preclinical studies have reported reduced gastrointestinal motility.

## Recent safety updates

- No significant toxicity reported in traditional use at recommended Ayurvedic doses; limited formal toxicological studies available
- Latex may be irritant; topical application should be patch-tested first in sensitive individuals. Pregnancy use not studied sufficiently.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Kwatha (bark decoction), Churna (bark powder), Capsule, Tablet, Kshara (alkali preparation), Lepa (paste), Vaginal douche

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g bark powder daily; 20-40 mL bark decoction twice daily; 500 mg - 1 g standardized extract twice daily; Kshara 500 mg - 1 g

**Optimal Timing:** Bark decoction: morning and evening between meals. For gynecological conditions: as directed, often paired with menstrual cycle timing. Kshara: after meals with honey or warm water.

**Storage:** Dried bark: cool, dry, pest-free storage. Powder: airtight, below 25 deg C, RH <55%. Kshara: moisture-proof containers (highly hygroscopic). Use HPMC capsules for tannin compatibility.

**Marker Compounds:** Leucocyanidin, Friedelin, Beta-sitosterol, Beta-sitosterol glucoside, Lupeol, Gallic acid, Ellagic acid, [Bergapten](/glossary/compounds-a-c/#bergapten)
