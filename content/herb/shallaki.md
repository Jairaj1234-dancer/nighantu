## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory: AKBA is a potent direct inhibitor of 5-lipoxygenase (5-LOX), blocking leukotriene synthesis; also inhibits NF-kB, COX-2, MAPK, and NLRP3 inflammasome
- Anti-arthritic: Boswellic acids are reported to inhibit MMP-3 and MMP-9 (matrix metalloproteinases), enzymes involved in cartilage degradation
- [Immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/): Modulates T-cell and B-cell function and shifts Th1/Th2 balance

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Traditionally used for Sandhivata (arthritic conditions). Randomized clinical trials have reported improvement in knee osteoarthritis symptoms comparable to NSAIDs.
- Traditionally used for inflammatory conditions. Studies have reported 5-LOX inhibition and NF-kB suppression. Clinical trials in inflammatory bowel disease have reported improvement.
- Traditionally used as Shwasahara for asthma. Clinical trials in bronchial asthma patients have reported improvement in FEV1 and a reduction in eosinophil count.

## Recent safety updates

- Well-tolerated in clinical trials with only mild adverse effects (nausea, acid reflux, mild GI upset); no serious or irreversible adverse effects reported
- No convincing evidence of hepatotoxicity; may interact with NSAIDs (synergistic anti-inflammatory effect); limited data in pregnancy and lactation

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Capsule, Tablet, Cream/Gel (topical), Churna (powder), [Guggulu](/formulation/guggulu/)-based formulation, Vati (tablet), Soft gelatin capsule

**Standard Dosage:** 400-1200 mg standardized extract per day (typically 300-400 mg three times daily); 3-6 g crude resin powder per day; topical gel 5-10% applied 2-3 times daily

**Optimal Timing:** With meals (high-fat meal preferred) for optimal boswellic acid absorption; three divided doses for sustained anti-inflammatory effect

**Storage:** Store below 25 deg C, protected from light and heat. Resin tends to soften above 30 deg C, critical for tropical storage. Capsules in HDPE bottles with desiccant or aluminum blisters. Topical products in aluminum tubes or airless pump dispensers.

**Marker Compounds:** 11-Keto-beta-boswellic acid (KBA), Acetyl-11-keto-beta-boswellic acid (AKBA), Alpha-boswellic acid, Beta-boswellic acid, Acetyl-alpha-boswellic acid, Acetyl-beta-boswellic acid, Incensole acetate
