## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Swarasa (fresh juice), Churna (powder), Capsule, Tablet, Kwath Ghana (concentrated extract), Tincture

**Standard Dosage:** 30-60ml fresh juice daily; 1-3g powder twice daily; 500mg standardized extract capsule twice daily; traditionally, fruit consumed as vegetable in diet

**Optimal Timing:** Traditionally taken early morning on an empty stomach; 30 minutes before meals; fresh juice preferred over dried forms

**Storage:** Fresh juice under refrigeration (2-8°C) in glass containers. Powder in airtight containers with desiccant and nitrogen flushing. Protect from moisture and heat.

**Marker Compounds:** Charantin, Momordicin, Vicine, Polypeptide-p, Cucurbitacin B, Momordicoside A-F, Goyaglycoside a-d, Beta-[sitosterol](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol)

### Synergistic Combinations

- Traditionally combined with Meshashringi (Gymnema sylvestre) for blood sugar support
- Traditionally combined with [Methi](/herb/methi/) (fenugreek) for blood sugar support
- With [Haridra](/herb/haridra/) ([turmeric](/herb/turmeric/)) for metabolic syndrome
- Traditionally combined with [Jambu](/herb/jambu/) (Syzygium cumini) in blood sugar formulations
- Traditionally combined with [Neem](/herb/neem/) for blood purification
