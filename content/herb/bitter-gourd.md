## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Swarasa (fresh juice), Churna (powder), Capsule, Tablet, Kwath Ghana (concentrated extract), Tincture

**Standard Dosage:** 30-60ml fresh juice daily; 1-3g powder twice daily; 500mg standardized extract capsule twice daily; traditionally, fruit consumed as vegetable in diet

**Bioavailability:** [Charantin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-c-d/#charantin) shows moderate oral bioavailability (20-30%). Polypeptide-p (p-insulin) is degraded by gastric proteases—limited oral bioavailability for the intact peptide (enteric coating helps). [Vicine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-t-z/#vicine) and charantin are better absorbed from juice than powder form. [Momordicin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#momordicin) has good lipophilicity. Cucurbitane [triterpenoids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-t-z/#triterpenoids) show 25-35% oral bioavailability. Note: fresh juice is considered most bioactive in both Ayurvedic and modern perspectives.

**Optimal Timing:** Early morning on empty stomach for maximum [antidiabetic](/nighantu/reference/antidiabetic/) effect; 30 minutes before meals; fresh juice preferred over dried forms

**Standardized Extract:** Fruit extract standardized to charantin NLT 2%; total [saponins](/nighantu/reference/saponins/) NLT 10%; momordicin NLT 0.5%. Lyophilized juice powder at 5:1 concentration.

**Shelf Life:** 2 days (fresh juice, refrigerated); 2 years (powder/capsule); 3 years (tablet)

**Storage:** Fresh juice under refrigeration (2-8°C) in glass containers. Powder in airtight containers with desiccant and nitrogen flushing. Protect from moisture and heat.

**Marker Compounds:** Charantin, Momordicin, Vicine, Polypeptide-p, Cucurbitacin B, Momordicoside A-F, Goyaglycoside a-d, Beta-[sitosterol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol)

### Extraction Methods

- Fresh fruit juice extraction
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (70:30) for charantin enrichment
- Aqueous extraction for polypeptide-p
- Ethanol extraction for cucurbitane [glycosides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides)
- Lyophilization of fresh juice for stable powder
- Supercritical CO2 for lipophilic triterpenoids

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Meshashringi (Gymnema sylvestre) for blood sugar management
- With [Methi](/nighantu/herb/methi/) (fenugreek) for diabetic support
- With [Haridra](/nighantu/herb/haridra/) ([turmeric](/nighantu/herb/turmeric/)) for metabolic syndrome
- With [Jambu](/nighantu/herb/jambu/) (Syzygium cumini) for comprehensive antidiabetic formulations
- With [Neem](/nighantu/herb/neem/) for blood purification
