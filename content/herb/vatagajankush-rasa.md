## How does it work?

- Multi-mineral anti-inflammatory action: The combination of copper ([Tamra Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/tamra-bhasma/)), iron ([Loha Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/loha-bhasma/)), and mica (Abhrak Bhasma) bhasmas provides trace minerals that modulate inflammatory pathways including COX-2 and lipoxygenase inhibition
- [Neuroprotective](/nighantu/reference/neuroprotective/) and [analgesic](/nighantu/reference/analgesic/): Ras Sindoor (HgS) and Shring Bhasma provide neuroactive mineral compounds; Citrullus colocynthis has documented anti-nociceptive properties
- Vata-pacifying mechanism: [Trikatu](/nighantu/herb/trikatu/) ([ginger](/nighantu/herb/ginger/), pepper, long pepper) improves digestion and bioavailability, while castor-based processing enhances anti-Vata (anti-degenerative/anti-neurological) properties per Ayurvedic pharmacology

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Sciatica (Gridhrasi): Clinical study (2023) showed significant symptom improvement, validating the traditional use in Vata-predominant nerve pain conditions
- Joint pain and arthritis (Sandhivata/Amavata): The multi-mineral anti-inflammatory composition provides mechanistic support for traditional use in musculoskeletal disorders; clinical practice experience supports efficacy

## Recent safety updates

- Contains MULTIPLE heavy metals including mercury (as HgS), lead ([Naga Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/naga-bhasma/)), tin ([Vanga Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/vanga-bhasma/)), and copper ([Tamra Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/tamra-bhasma/)) - cumulative heavy metal burden is a significant concern
- Lead content ([Naga Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/naga-bhasma/)) is particularly concerning as there is no known safe level of lead exposure according to WHO and CDC; even properly prepared lead bhasma may contribute to body lead burden
- Must be sourced from GMP-certified manufacturers with strict batch-wise testing for heavy metal content within Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia limits
- Strictly requires physician supervision; not for long-term continuous use; periodic monitoring of blood lead levels, renal function, and hepatic function recommended
- Contraindicated in pregnancy, lactation, children, and patients with renal, hepatic, or hematological disorders

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Vati

**Standard Dosage:** 125 mg twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Good; multi-metal Rasaushadhi with musculoskeletal tropism

**Optimal Timing:** With Dashamula Kwatha, [Eranda](/nighantu/herb/eranda/) Taila, or warm milk

**Standardized Extract:** Kajjali + Loha + Vanga + Naga + anti-Vata herbs

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite

**Storage:** Airtight container

**Marker Compounds:** Mercury sulfide, Iron oxide, Tin oxide, Lead oxide, [Guggulu](/nighantu/formulation/guggulu/) compounds

**Quality Parameters:** Standard Rasaushadhi tests, free metals below limits

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Dashamula Kwatha, [Eranda](/nighantu/herb/eranda/) Taila (castor oil), or warm milk
