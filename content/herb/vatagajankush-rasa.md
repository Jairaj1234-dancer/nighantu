## How does it work?

- Multi-mineral anti-inflammatory action: The combination of copper ([Tamra Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/tamra-bhasma/)), iron ([Loha Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/loha-bhasma/)), and mica (Abhrak Bhasma) bhasmas provides trace minerals that modulate inflammatory pathways including COX-2 and lipoxygenase inhibition
- [Neuroprotective](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/neuroprotective/) and [analgesic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/analgesic/): Ras Sindoor (HgS) and Shring Bhasma provide neuroactive mineral compounds; Citrullus colocynthis has documented anti-nociceptive properties
- Vata-pacifying mechanism: [Trikatu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/trikatu/) ([ginger](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ginger/), pepper, long pepper) improves digestion and bioavailability, while castor-based processing enhances anti-Vata (anti-degenerative/anti-neurological) properties per Ayurvedic pharmacology

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Sciatica (Gridhrasi): Clinical study (2023) showed significant symptom improvement, validating the traditional use in Vata-predominant nerve pain conditions
- Joint pain and arthritis (Sandhivata/Amavata): The multi-mineral anti-inflammatory composition provides mechanistic support for traditional use in musculoskeletal disorders; clinical practice experience supports efficacy

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **A Clinical Study on the Effect of Vatagajankusha Rasa in Gridhrasi (Sciatica)** (2023): Clinical study demonstrated significant improvement in sciatica symptoms including pain reduction, improved mobility, and reduced nerve compression signs. The formulation showed efficacy in managing Vata-predominant musculoskeletal disorders.
- **Ayurveda-modern medicine interface: Critical appraisal of studies of Ayurvedic medicines to treat osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis** (2011): Review noted that Ayurvedic herbo-mineral formulations including [Vatagajankush Rasa](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/vatagajankush-rasa/) showed promise in managing chronic pain and reducing inflammatory markers, but more large-scale randomized controlled trials are needed for standardized dosing and safety parameters.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains MULTIPLE heavy metals including mercury (as HgS), lead ([Naga Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/naga-bhasma/)), tin ([Vanga Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/vanga-bhasma/)), and copper ([Tamra Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/tamra-bhasma/)) - cumulative heavy metal burden is a significant concern
- Lead content ([Naga Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/naga-bhasma/)) is particularly concerning as there is no known safe level of lead exposure according to WHO and CDC; even properly prepared lead bhasma may contribute to body lead burden
- Must be sourced from GMP-certified manufacturers with strict batch-wise testing for heavy metal content within Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia limits
- Strictly requires physician supervision; not for long-term continuous use; periodic monitoring of blood lead levels, renal function, and hepatic function recommended
- Contraindicated in pregnancy, lactation, children, and patients with renal, hepatic, or hematological disorders

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite

**Quality Parameters:** Standard Rasaushadhi tests, free metals below limits
