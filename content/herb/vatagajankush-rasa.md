## How does it work?

- [Neuroprotective](/reference/neuroprotective/) and [analgesic](/reference/analgesic/): Ras Sindoor (HgS) and Shring Bhasma provide neuroactive mineral compounds; Citrullus colocynthis has documented anti-nociceptive properties
- Vata-pacifying mechanism: [Trikatu](/herb/trikatu/) ([ginger](/herb/ginger/), pepper, long pepper) improves digestion and bioavailability, while castor-based processing enhances anti-Vata (anti-degenerative/anti-neurological) properties per Ayurvedic pharmacology

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Sciatica (Gridhrasi): traditionally used for Vata-predominant nerve pain conditions. A clinical study reported improvement in symptoms.
- Joint pain and arthritis (Sandhivata/Amavata): used in classical practice for musculoskeletal disorders.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains MULTIPLE heavy metals including mercury (as HgS), lead ([Naga Bhasma](/herb/naga-bhasma/)), tin ([Vanga Bhasma](/herb/vanga-bhasma/)), and copper ([Tamra Bhasma](/herb/tamra-bhasma/)) - cumulative heavy metal burden is a significant concern
- Lead content ([Naga Bhasma](/herb/naga-bhasma/)) is particularly concerning as there is no known safe level of lead exposure according to WHO and CDC; even properly prepared lead bhasma may contribute to body lead burden
- Must be sourced from GMP-certified manufacturers with strict batch-wise testing for heavy metal content within Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia limits
- Strictly requires physician supervision; not for long-term continuous use; periodic monitoring of blood lead levels, renal function, and hepatic function recommended
- Contraindicated in pregnancy, lactation, children, and patients with renal, hepatic, or hematological disorders

## What is it made of?

### Mineral/Elemental Profile

- **Primary component:** Mercury-based preparation (Parada) with herbal processing
- **Note:** Restricted use, requires strict Shodhana protocols and expert supervision

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Vati

**Standard Dosage:** 125 mg twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** With Dashamula Kwatha, [Eranda](/herb/eranda/) Taila, or warm milk

**Storage:** Airtight container

**Marker Compounds:** Mercury sulfide, Iron oxide, Tin oxide, Lead oxide

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Dashamula Kwatha, [Eranda](/herb/eranda/) Taila (castor oil), or warm milk
