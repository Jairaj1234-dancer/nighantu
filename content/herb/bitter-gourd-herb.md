## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Swarasa (leaf/vine juice), Churna (dried leaf/vine powder), Kashayam (decoction), Capsule, Lepa (paste for external use)

**Standard Dosage:** 10-20ml leaf juice twice daily; 2-4g vine/leaf powder twice daily; 50-100ml decoction; external paste as needed for skin conditions

**Bioavailability:** Aerial parts (leaves, stems, tendrils) contain similar but not identical phytochemical profile to fruit. [Momordicin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#momordicin) concentration is higher in leaves than fruit. Leaf [flavonoids](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/flavonoids/) show moderate oral bioavailability (20-35%). [Chlorophyll](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-c-d/#chlorophyll)-rich fractions may provide additional [antioxidant](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antioxidant/) protection during GI transit. Juice from fresh leaves provides optimal bioavailability.

**Optimal Timing:** Morning on empty stomach for metabolic benefits; leaf juice before meals; external paste applied to affected areas twice daily

**Standardized Extract:** Leaf extract standardized to total flavonoids NLT 5%; momordicin NLT 1%. Vine extract 8:1 aqueous extract ratio.

**Shelf Life:** 3 days (fresh juice, refrigerated); 18 months (leaf powder); 2 years (capsule)

**Storage:** Fresh juice refrigerated in glass containers. Dried material in airtight, light-protected containers. Cool, dry conditions below 25°C.

**Marker Compounds:** Momordicin, [Vicine](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-t-z/#vicine), Rosmarinic acid, Gallic acid, Gentisic acid, Chlorogenic acid, [Catechin](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/catechin/), [Epicatechin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#epicatechin)

### Extraction Methods

- Fresh leaf juice extraction (Swarasa)
- Aqueous decoction of dried aerial parts
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (60:40) for flavonoid-rich fractions
- Ethanol extraction for alkaloid profiling
- Shade-drying followed by mechanical grinding for powder

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Neem](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/neem/) leaves for skin and blood purification
- With [Guduchi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/guduchi/) for [immunomodulatory](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/immunomodulatory/) formulations
- With [Haridra](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/haridra/) for anti-inflammatory and [antimicrobial](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antimicrobial/) use
- With [Karanja](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/karanja/) leaves for dermatological applications
