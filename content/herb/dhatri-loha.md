## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Bhaishajya Ratnavali, Shoolaroga Adhikara, Chapter 30, Verses 142-147; also referenced in Rasaratna Samuchaya

## How does it work?

- [Loha Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/loha-bhasma/) provides bioavailable nano-particulate iron (Fe3O4/gamma-Fe2O3, ~34 nm) for direct hemoglobin synthesis and erythropoiesis
- Amalaki (Emblica officinalis) is rich in vitamin C, markedly enhancing non-heme iron absorption in the GI tract and acting as a potent [antioxidant](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antioxidant/)
- [Yashtimadhu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/yashtimadhu/) (licorice) provides gastroprotective and anti-inflammatory effects, reducing GI irritation from iron supplementation
- [Guduchi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/guduchi/) (Tinospora cordifolia) has [immunomodulatory](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/immunomodulatory/) and [hepatoprotective](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/hepatoprotective/) properties, supporting liver function for optimal iron metabolism
- Ushna virya (mildly heating potency) of [Loha Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/loha-bhasma/) kindles Agni (digestive fire), transforming Ama (metabolic toxins) and improving Dhatu (tissue) nourishment

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Iron deficiency anemia (Pandu Roga) - validated in multiple clinical trials showing significant hemoglobin improvement (1.5-2 g/dL rise over 8 weeks)
- Digestive disorders with acid peptic symptoms - traditional use supported by the gastroprotective action of Amalaki and [Yashtimadhu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/yashtimadhu/) components

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **Dhatri Lauha in the management of iron deficiency anemia: A prospective open-label single-arm multi-center trial** (2023): Multi-center trial across 12 centers (40 participants/site) showed Dhatri Lauha 500 mg twice daily for 45 days significantly improved hemoglobin, RBC, and iron metabolism parameters in IDA patients; well tolerated and clinically safe
- **Dhatri Lauha in garbhini pandu (iron deficiency anemia in pregnancy)** (2021): Clinical trial demonstrated efficacy of Dhatri Lauha in managing iron deficiency anemia during pregnancy with improved hematological parameters
- **Comparative clinical study of [Swarnamakshika](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/concepts-m-y/#swarnamakshika) Bhasma and Dhatri Lauha in Pandu w.s.r. to Iron Deficiency Anaemia** (2025): Comparative study showed both [Swarnamakshika](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/concepts-m-y/#swarnamakshika) Bhasma and Dhatri Lauha effective in managing Pandu (anemia) with significant improvements in hemoglobin levels

## Recent safety updates

- Multi-center trial (2023) confirmed Dhatri Lauha is well tolerated with no significant adverse effects at 500 mg twice daily for 45 days
- Prolonged unsupervised use may lead to iron overload (hemochromatosis-like symptoms) - serum ferritin monitoring recommended beyond 8 weeks
- Contraindicated in patients with hemochromatosis, thalassemia major, or conditions with existing iron overload
- [Loha Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/loha-bhasma/) toxicity study showed safety at therapeutic dose and 5x therapeutic dose; only at highest doses were biochemical alterations evident, confirming dose-dependent safety profile
- Heavy metal contaminant testing should confirm compliance with API limits for lead, arsenic, and mercury

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Vati/tablet

**Standard Dosage:** 250–500 mg (1–2 tablets) twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Enhanced iron absorption due to [Amla](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/amla/) (Dhatri) vitamin C content facilitating Fe3+ → Fe2+ conversion

**Optimal Timing:** After meals with honey or warm water

**Standardized Extract:** Iron bhasma combined with [Amla](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/amla/) and [Triphala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/triphala/)

**Shelf Life:** 3–5 years

**Storage:** Airtight container; cool, dry place

**Marker Compounds:** Iron oxide (from [Loha Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/loha-bhasma/)), Ascorbic acid/[tannins](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/tannins/) (from Dhatri), [Triphala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/triphala/) [polyphenols](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polyphenols)

**Quality Parameters:** Iron content as labeled, disintegration <30 min, hardness 4–6 kg/cm², uniformity of weight ±5%

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Honey, warm water, or Amla juice
