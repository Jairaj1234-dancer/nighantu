## How does it work?

- Nervine tonic: Phenolic acids (vanillic, syringic) and [flavonoids](/reference/flavonoids/) have been reported in studies to show [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) activity; blood-brain barrier penetration, neuroprotection and BDNF effects are not established
- Anti-inflammatory: Protocatechuic acid and kaempferol inhibit COX-2, iNOS, and NF-kB-mediated inflammatory gene expression
- Cardiotonic: preliminary studies have reported a positive inotropic effect and reduced myocardial oxidative stress; the constituents responsible are not established

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Nervine tonic (Medhya): traditionally used as a nervine tonic. Studies have reported antioxidant activity in brain tissue and [neuroprotective](/reference/neuroprotective/) properties of phenolic constituents.
- [Aphrodisiac](/glossary/pharmacology/#aphrodisiac) and rejuvenative ([Vajikarana](/glossary/pharmacology/#vajikarana)-[Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/)): used in classical practice as an aphrodisiac and rejuvenative.
- Cardiac tonic (Hridya): traditionally used as a cardiac tonic. Preliminary studies have reported a positive inotropic effect and [cardioprotective](/reference/cardioprotective/) antioxidant activity.

## Recent safety updates

- Long traditional use; no significant toxicity reported in the sources reviewed at Ayurvedic therapeutic doses; modern toxicological evaluation is limited
- Dual identity (Grewia hirsuta vs. Sida veronicifolia) used as Nagabala creates potential for botanical confusion; ensure correct identification for safe use

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (root powder), Kwatha (decoction), Capsules, Tablets, Taila (medicated oil), [Ghrita](/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee), Lehya/Avaleha

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g root powder per day; 50-100 mL decoction twice daily; 500-1000 mg extract capsules daily; external oil application as needed

**Optimal Timing:** Morning for tonic and strength-building effects; with warm milk for Rasayana; Taila massage in evening before bed for Vata pacification; Ghrita with meals

**Storage:** Root powder below 25 deg C in airtight containers with desiccant (hygroscopic due to mucilage). Taila in amber glass at room temperature. Ghrita in clean glass containers. Protect all forms from moisture and light.

**Marker Compounds:** 20-Hydroxyecdysone (ecdysterone), Beta-sitosterol, [Stigmasterol](/reference/stigmasterol/), [Lupeol](/reference/lupeol/), [Friedelin](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#friedelin), Mucilage polysaccharides, Ephedrine (species-dependent, must be declared)
