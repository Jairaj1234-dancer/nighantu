## How does it work?

- In vitro studies have reported that juglone induces apoptosis in cancer cell lines through reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation and mitochondrial membrane depolarization, activating caspase-3 and caspase-9 pathways
- Animal studies have reported anti-inflammatory effects of the [polyphenols](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polyphenols) ellagic acid and gallic acid. These studies reported inhibition of NF-kB signaling and reduced COX-2/LOX expression.
- Studies have reported that alpha-linolenic acid (ALA) may lower serum LDL-cholesterol and triglycerides via upregulation of hepatic LDL receptors and inhibition of HMG-CoA reductase

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Akshotaka is traditionally used in Ayurveda for Prameha (diabetes). Clinical and preclinical studies have reported [Antidiabetic](/reference/antidiabetic/) effects, including improved glycemic control.
- Walnut bark and leaf preparations are traditionally applied to the skin. In vitro and animal studies have reported anti-inflammatory and wound healing activity.
- Clinical studies have reported [Neuroprotective](/reference/neuroprotective/) and cognitive benefits. They linked walnut consumption to improved memory and cognitive function in elderly people.

## Recent safety updates

- Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) for dietary consumption; walnut allergy (IgE-mediated) is a significant concern affecting approximately 1-2% of the population in Western countries
- Juglone in bark and green husk may cause contact dermatitis and skin staining; topical preparations require careful concentration limits
- No significant herb-drug interactions documented at standard dietary doses; high doses of walnut leaf preparations should be avoided in pregnancy due to limited safety data

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (kernel powder), Taila (walnut oil), Capsule, Lehyam (confection), Topical oil

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g kernel powder twice daily; 5-10ml oil internally; 1-2 walnut kernels daily as dietary supplement

**Optimal Timing:** With meals or as snack. Oil taken in morning on empty stomach for general health. Kernel powder with warm milk at bedtime for brain nourishment.

**Storage:** Kernels and powder in airtight containers, refrigerated or below 15°C. Oil in amber glass, nitrogen-flushed headspace, refrigerated after opening. Protect from heat, light, and oxygen.

**Marker Compounds:** Alpha-linolenic acid (ALA), Ellagic acid, Juglone (hull), Gallic acid, Tellimagrandin, Gamma-tocopherol, [Pedunculagin](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#pedunculagin)
