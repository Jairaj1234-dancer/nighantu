## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Capsule, Tablet, Avalehya (confection)

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3g powder twice daily; 50-100ml decoction; 250-500mg extract capsule. Lower doses recommended initially due to potent sedative properties.

**Bioavailability:** [Protopine](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#protopine) and related isoquinoline [alkaloids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) show moderate to good oral bioavailability (30-50%). Alkaloids are well absorbed from the GI tract due to lipophilicity. Traditional milk vehicle reduces potential GI irritation and may enhance absorption of lipophilic alkaloids. Note: limited modern pharmacokinetic studies specific to this species.

**Optimal Timing:** At bedtime with warm milk for sleep disorders; between meals for anxiety; not recommended before driving or operating machinery

**Standardized Extract:** Root/rhizome extract standardized to total isoquinoline alkaloids NLT 2%; protopine NLT 0.5%

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (powder); 3 years (capsule/tablet)

**Storage:** Store in airtight, light-protected containers. Alkaloids are photosensitive. Cool, dry conditions below 25°C.

**Marker Compounds:** Protopine, Bicuculline, Corydine, Corydaline, Bulbocapnine, Alpha-allocryptopine

### Extraction Methods

- Hydroalcoholic extraction (70:30 ethanol:water)
- Alkaloid-enriched extraction with acidified water followed by basification and organic solvent partitioning
- Aqueous decoction for traditional use
- Methanol extraction for research-grade alkaloid profiling

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Jatamamsi for [anxiolytic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/anxiolytic/) and sedative synergy
- With [Ashwagandha](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ashwagandha/) for stress and insomnia
- With [Brahmi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/brahmi/) for cognitive support with calming effect
- With Shankhapushpi for neurological conditions
- With [Tagara](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/tagara/) for sleep disorders
