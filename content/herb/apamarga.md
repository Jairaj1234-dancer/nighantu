## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory: Oleanolic acid and [saponins](/reference/saponins/) inhibit pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6) via NF-kB pathway suppression
- Wound healing (proposed): a computational study reported that Biochanin A may bind wound-healing targets MMP-9 and TGF-beta
- [Diuretic](/reference/diuretic/): Betaine and [potassium](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#potassium)-rich alkaline compounds enhance renal sodium and water excretion

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Kshara (alkaline preparation): traditionally used for urinary calculi. In vitro studies have reported lithotriptic activity and renal stone dissolution.
- Traditionally used for hemorrhoids. Studies in rectal tissue models have reported wound repair and anti-inflammatory effects.
- Mutrala (diuretic): traditionally used to increase urine flow. Animal studies have reported increased urine output and sodium excretion.

## Recent safety updates

- Generally safe at traditional Ayurvedic doses; acute toxicity studies in animals show LD50 > 2000 mg/kg for aqueous extracts
- Contraindicated in pregnancy due to reported abortifacient activity in animal studies; patients on anticoagulants should exercise caution

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kshara (alkali preparation), Kashayam (decoction), Capsule, Tablet, Taila (medicated oil), Dhupana (fumigation, dried plant)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 0.5-1g Kshara with buttermilk/water; 50-100ml decoction

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals with warm water for digestive indications; Kshara with buttermilk after meals for calculi; post-prandial for metabolic conditions

**Storage:** Powder in airtight containers, cool and dry. Kshara in glass containers (alkaline, reacts with metals). Kshara Sutra in airtight tubes at room temperature.

**Marker Compounds:** Oleanolic acid, Achyranthine, Betaine, Ecdysterone (20-hydroxyecdysone), Pentatriacontane, Potassium carbonate (in Kshara)

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Varuna](/herb/varuna/) for urinary calculi (Kshara-based formulation)
- Traditionally combined with [Gokshura](/herb/gokshura/) and Pashanbheda for urinary calculi
- With [Chitrak](/herb/chitrak/) for digestive stimulation
- Apamarga Kshara is traditionally used in Kshara Sutra for fistula
