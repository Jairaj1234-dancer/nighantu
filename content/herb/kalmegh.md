## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Tablet, Capsule, Kashayam (decoction), Swarasa (fresh juice), Standardized extract, Syrup

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3g powder twice daily; 400-600mg standardized extract twice daily; 10-20ml fresh juice; 30-60ml decoction

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals with warm water

**Storage:** Cool, dry place below 25°C, protected from light and moisture. Andrographolide is relatively stable in solid form but degrades in solution. Amber containers for liquid preparations.

**Marker Compounds:** Andrographolide, [Neoandrographolide](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#neoandrographolide), 14-Deoxyandrographolide, 14-Deoxy-11,12-didehydroandrographolide, [Andrograpanin](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#andrograpanin), Isoandrographolide

### Synergistic Combinations

- Traditionally combined with [Guduchi](/herb/guduchi/) in formulations used for fever
- With [Tulsi](/herb/tulsi/) for respiratory infection support
- With [Katuki](/herb/katuki/) (Picrorhiza) for [hepatoprotective](/reference/hepatoprotective/) synergy
- With [Haridra](/herb/haridra/) for anti-inflammatory action
- Traditionally combined with [Neem](/herb/neem/) in [antipyretic](/reference/antipyretic/) formulations
