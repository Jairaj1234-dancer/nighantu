## How does it work?

- [Anticancer](/reference/anticancer/): 70nm aluminum oxide nanoparticles induce mitochondrial-dependent apoptosis in cancer cells through loss of mitochondrial membrane potential, cytochrome c release, and caspase cascade activation - a genuine nanomedicine mechanism
- [Immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/): Ruby nanoparticles may interact with immune cells to enhance innate immunity; the chromium trace content could play a role in [glucose](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#glucose) metabolism regulation

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Immunostimulation and tuberculosis (Rajayakshma): traditionally used to enhance immunity and for Rajayakshma. Studies on ruby nanoparticles have reported anticancer, apoptotic and immunomodulatory activity.
- Cognitive enhancement and neuroprotection: traditionally used for improving Buddhi (intellect) and Smriti (memory). Preclinical studies have reported neuroprotective effects.

## What do recent studies show?

  Manikya Bhasma (70nm particles) reduced viability of MDAMB-231, HeLa, HCT-116, DLD-1, and MG-63 cancer cells with IC50 of 105.73-155.47 micrograms/ml. Cell death followed mitochondrial-dependent apoptosis involving loss of mitochondrial membrane potential, cytochrome c release, caspase activation, and DNA degradation.

## Recent safety updates

- Aluminum oxide (Al2O3) is the primary component - while generally considered inert and safe at low doses, chronic aluminum exposure has been controversially linked to neurodegenerative conditions
- Preclinical toxicity study showed safety of traditionally prepared formulation in rats, but long-term human safety data is lacking
- The nanoparticle size (70nm) raises questions about tissue penetration and accumulation that are relevant to modern nanotoxicology frameworks
- Must be taken strictly under medical supervision at the dose given under Dosage; exceeding recommended doses not advised

## What is it made of?

### Mineral/Elemental Profile

- **Aluminum oxide (Al2O3):** Major component (ruby, corundum)
- **Aluminum (Al):** ~53%
- **Chromium (Cr):** Trace (gives red color)
- **Iron (Fe):** Trace

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Pishti

**Standard Dosage:** 15–30 mg (precious stone, precise dosing)

**Optimal Timing:** With honey or cream of milk

**Storage:** Airtight, premium container

**Marker Compounds:** Aluminium oxide (corundum), Chromium (gives red color), Trace elements

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Honey, Malai, or as part of Ratna (gem) formulations
