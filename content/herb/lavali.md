## How does it work?

- Ursolic acid and oleanolic acid exhibit anti-inflammatory activity through inhibition of NF-kB pathway, suppression of COX-2/iNOS expression, and reduction of pro-inflammatory cytokine production
- Scopoletin exerts [antidiabetic](/reference/antidiabetic/) effects through inhibition of alpha-glucosidase and aldose reductase [enzymes](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#enzymes)
- Lupeol and betulin demonstrate [hepatoprotective](/reference/hepatoprotective/) effects by enhancing [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) enzyme activity (SOD, catalase, GPx) and reducing lipid peroxidation in hepatocytes

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Traditionally used in tribal practice for diabetes. Preliminary pharmacological studies have reported antidiabetic and antihyperglycemic activity.
- Traditionally used in folk medicine for dysentery and skin infections. [Antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) activity against bacterial and fungal pathogens has been reported in vitro.

## Recent safety updates

- Limited formal toxicity studies have been conducted; the plant is used traditionally as food (ripe fruits eaten) and medicine in tribal communities of Northeast India
- Given the limited pharmacological investigation, standardized dose-toxicity relationship data is not yet available for clinical application
- Abortifacient activity documented in traditional use; strictly avoid during pregnancy; further comprehensive toxicity studies are recommended

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kwatha (decoction), Capsules, Tablets, Avaleha (confection), Svarasa (juice)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g fruit powder per day; 50-100 mL decoction twice daily; 10-20 mL fresh juice

**Optimal Timing:** Between meals or in the morning; avoid in patients with kidney disorders

**Storage:** Fresh fruit at 5-10 deg C. Dried extracts in airtight, moisture-proof containers below 25 deg C with desiccant. Protect from light. Nitrogen-flush packaging recommended.
