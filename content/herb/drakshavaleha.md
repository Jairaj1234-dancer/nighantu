## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Ashtanga Hridaya Chikitsasthana 16/29-31 (Pandu Roga / anemia chapter); referenced in Brihat Trayi classical compendia

## How does it work?

- [Draksha](/herb/draksha/) (raisins) provides natural iron, natural sugars, and polyphenol antioxidants
- Amalaki juice provides high vitamin C content that enhances non-heme iron absorption from dietary and supplemental sources
- [Yashtimadhu](/herb/yashtimadhu/) provides gastroprotective action and enhances nutrient absorption while reducing inflammatory damage to GI mucosa
- [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) ([piperine](/reference/piperine/)) enhances hepatic enzyme activity and bioavailability of iron and other nutritional compounds
- Nutritive caloric content from raisins, sugar, and honey provides energy substrate for debilitated and anemic patients

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Pandu (anemia): traditionally used for anemia. A clinical study reported improvement in pregnancy-related anemia.
- Daurbalya (debility/weakness): traditionally used for chronic debility, valued in classical practice for its nutritive and energy-providing properties.
- Yakrit Roga (liver disorders): used in classical practice for hepatic conditions, as indicated in the Ashtanga Hridaya. Its ingredient profile is described as [hepatoprotective](/reference/hepatoprotective/).
- Garbhini Paricharya (pregnancy care): traditionally used in pregnancy care. An animal study reported fetal protection against cyclophosphamide-induced toxicity.

## Recent safety updates

- Cytoprotective effect demonstrated against cyclophosphamide toxicity in pregnancy - fetal protective properties
- High sugar content requires monitoring in diabetic patients; consult practitioner for sugar-free alternatives

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Avaleha (semi-solid confection), Modified tablet (compressed Avaleha granules), Capsule (Avaleha granules)

**Standard Dosage:** 10-20g (1-2 tablespoons) twice daily with warm milk or water. Classical dose as per text reference.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals with warm milk for [Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/) (rejuvenation) effect; before meals for respiratory conditions; morning and evening doses

**Storage:** Clean, dry, airtight glass or food-grade plastic containers at room temperature (below 30 deg C). Use clean dry spoon for dispensing - moisture introduction causes microbial growth. Do not refrigerate unless specified - cold storage may crystallize sugars.

**Marker Compounds:** [Resveratrol](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#resveratrolresveratrol) / OPCs (from [Draksha](/herb/draksha/)), Piperine (from [Pippali](/herb/pippali/)), [Glycyrrhizin](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycyrrhizinglycyrrhizin) (from [Yashtimadhu](/herb/yashtimadhu/)), Total polyphenols, Sugar content (Brix)

### Synergistic Combinations

- Contains multiple synergistic ingredients as per classical formulation
- With warm milk (Anupana) for enhanced Rasayana effect
- With [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) Churna added for respiratory conditions
- Traditionally combined with [Sitopaladi Churna](/formulation/sitopaladi-churna/) for cough and cold
