## Names and identification

| Language | Name |
|----------|------|
| English | [Loha Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/loha-bhasma/) |

## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Iron oxide nanoparticles (Fe2O3/Fe3O4) in 50-200 nm range
- Magnetite phase (Fe3O4) as primary constituent
- Fe2+/Fe3+ ratio approximately 40%:60%
- Trace carbon, sodium, and zinc as contaminants
- Organic compounds from herbal processing media ([Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/), etc.)

## How does it work?

- Iron supplementation via bioavailable nano-iron oxide particles that enhance intestinal absorption through smaller particle size and increased surface area, bypassing typical ferrous iron GI side effects
- Hematopoietic stimulation through provision of elemental iron in oxide form, supporting hemoglobin synthesis and red blood cell production in iron-deficiency states
- Potential magnetic hyperthermia for cancer therapy: nanocrystalline magnetite in Lauha Bhasma can generate localized heat under alternating magnetic field for targeted cancer cell killing
- Digestive fire (Agni) enhancement through iron-mediated stimulation of metabolic [enzymes](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-d-g/#enzymes) and improvement of nutrient absorption in the gastrointestinal tract

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Iron-deficiency anemia (Pandu Roga): Multiple clinical trials including multi-center and double-blind studies confirmed efficacy comparable to ferrous sulfate with better tolerability
- Digestive disorders: Improved digestion and appetite confirmed through clinical observations; Agni-deepana (digestive fire enhancement) property validated
- General debility and weakness (Daurbalya): Iron supplementation effects validated through hemoglobin improvement and clinical symptom resolution in multiple studies

- Srikanth N, Rao BC, Yadav B and others 2021. ## Recent safety updates

- SAFE AT HIGH DOSES: No mortality or toxicity signs up to 100x therapeutic dose in acute study; safe at 5x therapeutic dose for 60 days in subacute study
- FEWER GI SIDE EFFECTS: Clinical trials showed significantly fewer gastrointestinal complaints (constipation, nausea) compared to conventional ferrous sulfate, likely due to nano-particulate oxide form
- DOSE-DEPENDENT CAUTION: At 10x therapeutic dose, some biochemical and histopathological alterations observed. Strict therapeutic dose adherence recommended.
- IRON OVERLOAD RISK: Prolonged unsupervised use may lead to iron overload (hemochromatosis-like symptoms). Periodic hemoglobin and serum ferritin monitoring advised.
- STANDARDIZATION CONCERN: Different preparation methods yield different particle sizes and iron oxide phases, affecting bioavailability and safety. Only classically prepared products recommended.

## What is it made of?

### Mineral/Elemental Profile

- **Iron (Fe2O3/Fe3O4):** Major component (>90%)
- **Calcium (Ca):** Trace
- **Silicon (Si):** Trace
- **Manganese (Mn):** Trace

**Analytical Methods:** XRD (confirms Fe2O3/Fe3O4), ICP-OES, SEM-EDS, Namburi phased spot test

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Bhasma

**Standard Dosage:** 125–250 mg twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Excellent; iron oxide nanoparticles (50–100 nm) — superior bioavailability to ferrous sulfate tablets per clinical studies

**Optimal Timing:** After meals with honey, ghee, or [Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/) Kwatha

**Standardized Extract:** Shataputi [Loha Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/loha-bhasma/) (100-Puta) — standard therapeutic grade

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite

**Storage:** Airtight glass or HDPE container; avoid moisture

**Marker Compounds:** Fe2O3 (hematite, primary), Fe3O4 (magnetite), Trace minerals from Bhavana media

**Quality Parameters:** Total iron >55%, Varitaratva, Rekhapurnatva, Apunarbhava, Nischandratva (lusterless), particle size <100 nm, arsenic <3 ppm, lead <10 ppm

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Honey + ghee (unequal parts), [Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/) Kwatha, [Amla](/nighantu/herb/amla/) juice, or [Mandura Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/mandura-bhasma/) combination
