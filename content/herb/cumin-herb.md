## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Swarasa (fresh juice), Churna (dried herb powder), Kashayam (decoction), Lepa (paste)

**Standard Dosage:** 5-10ml fresh juice; 2-4g dried herb powder twice daily; 30-50ml decoction. [Cumin](/herb/cumin/) herb (aerial parts) is less commonly used than seeds but has traditional applications.

**Optimal Timing:** With meals for digestive support; fresh juice before meals; decoction between meals

**Storage:** Fresh herb refrigerated. Dried material in airtight, light-protected containers. Cool, dry conditions.

**Marker Compounds:** [Apigenin](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#apigenin), [Luteolin](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#luteolin), [Quercetin](/reference/quercetinquercetin/), Chlorogenic acid, Caffeic acid, Gamma-terpinene, Para-cymene, Chlorophyll a and b

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Coriander](/herb/coriander/) leaves for digestive and cooling combinations
- With [Pudina](/herb/pudina/) (mint) for carminative formulations
- With [Tulsi](/herb/tulsi/) for respiratory support
- With [Haridra](/herb/haridra/) for anti-inflammatory preparations
