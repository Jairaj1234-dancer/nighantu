## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (root/whole plant powder), Kashayam (decoction), Capsule, Tablet, Taila (medicated oil — Atibala Taila), Swarasa (fresh juice), [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 50-100ml decoction; 500mg-1g extract capsule; 10-20ml fresh juice; Taila for external Abhyanga

**Bioavailability:** Moderate oral bioavailability. Abutilon indicum (Atibala) contains [mucilage](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#mucilage) (which provides sustained-release matrix), beta-[sitosterol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol) (5-10% absorbed), and [flavonoids](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/flavonoids/). Asparagine (an amino acid present abundantly) has near-complete oral bioavailability. Mucilage creates gel matrix that slows but prolongs drug release — natural sustained-release mechanism. Oil-based preparations (Taila, Ghrita) enhance lipophilic constituent absorption.

**Optimal Timing:** With warm milk at bedtime for [Rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/rasayana/) and strength-building. Before meals with warm water for urinary and digestive indications. Taila for external Abhyanga (oil massage) — morning preferred.

**Standardized Extract:** Root extract (10:1) standardized to minimum 2% beta-sitosterol and minimum 5% total flavonoids. Mucilage content minimum 15% in whole plant material.

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (powder); 3 years (capsules/tablets); 3 years (Taila); 2 years (Ghrita)

**Storage:** Cool, dry place below 25°C. Mucilaginous powders are hygroscopic — use desiccant in packaging. Oil preparations in amber glass.

**Marker Compounds:** Beta-sitosterol, Asparagine, Gallic acid, Caffeic acid, p-Coumaric acid, [Quercetin](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/quercetinquercetin/), [Luteolin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#luteolin) [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides), Mucilage [polysaccharides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polysaccharides)

### Extraction Methods

- Aqueous decoction (traditional Kashaya)
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (60:40)
- Fresh juice expression
- [Sesame oil](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila) infusion (Taila Paka vidhi) for external preparation
- Ghee processing for Ghrita preparation

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Bala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/bala/) (Sida cordifolia) for Balya (strength-promoting) action — classical pair
- With [Ashwagandha](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ashwagandha/) for [adaptogenic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/adaptogenic/) and strengthening effects
- With [Gokshura](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/gokshura/) for urinary tract support
- With [Yashtimadhu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/yashtimadhu/) for ulcers and gastric mucosal protection
