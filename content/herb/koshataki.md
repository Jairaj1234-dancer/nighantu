## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Swarasa (fresh juice), Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Nasya (nasal drops), Capsule, Beeja Churna (seed powder)

**Standard Dosage:** 5-10 mL fresh fruit juice; 2-4 g powder twice daily; 30-50 mL decoction; Nasya: 2-4 drops per nostril; 500 mg capsule

**Bioavailability:** Ribosome-inactivating [proteins](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#proteins) (luffin) are acid-labile with poor oral bioavailability — primarily active topically and via nasal route. Cucurbitane [triterpenoids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-t-z/#triterpenoids) show moderate oral absorption enhanced by lipid carriers. Sapogenins have improved bioavailability in SNEDDS formulations. Nasya administration of juice provides direct nasal mucosal delivery for pratishyaya (rhinitis) management.

**Optimal Timing:** Nasya: early morning on empty stomach for sinus conditions; internal preparations: after meals; juice: morning for respiratory benefit

**Standardized Extract:** Fruit extract standardized to minimum 3% total [saponins](/nighantu/reference/saponins/) and 2% [cucurbitacins](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-c-d/#cucurbitacins); seed extract to minimum 5% protein content with characterized RIP activity

**Shelf Life:** 6 hours for fresh juice (Nasya grade); 18 months for dried powder; 24 months for capsules; 24 months for seed oil

**Storage:** Fresh juice must be used immediately or refrigerated (2-8°C) for max 6 hours. Dried preparations in airtight containers at 15-30°C. Seed oil in dark glass bottles at cool temperature.

**Marker Compounds:** Luffin (ribosome-inactivating protein), Cucurbitacin B, Oleanolic acid, Sapogenins, Linoleic acid, Oleic acid, Beta-[sitosterol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), [Stigmasterol](/nighantu/reference/stigmasterol/)

### Extraction Methods

- Cold pressing of fresh fruit for juice
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50-60% ethanol)
- Aqueous extraction for protein-rich fraction
- Seed oil extraction by cold pressing
- Freeze-drying for protein preservation
