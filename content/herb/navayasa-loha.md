## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Bhaishajya Ratnavali, Pandu Roga Chikitsa (Anemia Treatment Chapter)

## How does it work?

- [Loha Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/loha-bhasma/) (9 parts, 30 calcinations) provides nano-particulate bioavailable iron for hemoglobin synthesis and RBC production
- [Trikatu](/nighantu/herb/trikatu/) (Shunthi + Maricha + [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/)) enhances bioavailability of iron through improved GI absorption and stimulation of digestive [enzymes](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-d-g/#enzymes) (Agni deepana)
- [Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/) ([Haritaki](/nighantu/herb/haritaki/) + Bibhitaka + Amalaki) provides vitamin C for iron absorption enhancement, gentle [laxative](/nighantu/reference/laxative/) action to prevent iron-induced constipation, and [antioxidant](/nighantu/reference/antioxidant/) protection
- Chitraka (Plumbago zeylanica) acts as a potent digestive stimulant (Agni deepana), improving nutrient assimilation and iron uptake
- [Vidanga](/nighantu/herb/vidanga/) (Embelia ribes) provides anthelmintic action removing intestinal parasites that may cause or worsen anemia, plus its own hematinic properties
- Musta (Cyperus rotundus) aids digestion and has anti-inflammatory properties, reducing intestinal inflammation that impairs iron absorption

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Iron deficiency anemia (Pandu Roga) - validated in multiple clinical and observational studies showing statistically significant increases in hemoglobin, PCV, and RBC count
- General debility and weakness associated with anemia - clinical studies confirm improvement in energy levels, appetite, and overall vitality metrics

## Recent safety updates

- Clinical trials showed no side effects on liver or kidney function tests during treatment period
- Prolonged use beyond prescribed duration may risk iron overload; periodic monitoring of serum ferritin and transferrin saturation recommended
- Contraindicated in hemochromatosis and iron storage disorders; pre-treatment iron studies advised
- Contains no mercury or other toxic heavy metals - purely herbo-iron formulation with better safety profile than mercury-containing Rasa preparations
- Chitraka (Plumbago zeylanica) at high doses can be irritant; formulation ratio ensures safe levels but gastric sensitivity should be monitored

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna or Vati

**Standard Dosage:** 250–500 mg twice daily

**Bioavailability:** Good; nine-ingredient formulation with natural absorption enhancers

**Optimal Timing:** After meals with honey or ghee

**Standardized Extract:** [Loha Bhasma](/nighantu/herb/loha-bhasma/) combined with [Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/) and five herbal components (total 9 ingredients: Nava = nine)

**Shelf Life:** 2–3 years (churna); 3–5 years (vati)

**Storage:** Airtight container

**Marker Compounds:** Iron oxide, [Piperine](/nighantu/reference/piperine/) (from [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/)), [Trikatu](/nighantu/herb/trikatu/) [alkaloids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids), [Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/) [polyphenols](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polyphenols)

**Quality Parameters:** Iron content as labeled, weight uniformity, assay of marker compounds

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Honey, warm water
