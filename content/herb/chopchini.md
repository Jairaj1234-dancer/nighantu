## How does it work?

- Steroidal saponin anti-inflammatory pathway: Smilagenin and dioscin modulate NF-kB and MAPK signaling, reducing pro-inflammatory cytokine production (TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6) in chronic skin conditions
- Astilbin [immunomodulatory](/nighantu/reference/immunomodulatory/) action: Flavonoid astilbin selectively suppresses activated T-lymphocyte proliferation while preserving normal immune function, relevant to autoimmune skin diseases like psoriasis and pemphigus
- [Resveratrol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#resveratrolresveratrol) and stilbene [antioxidant](/nighantu/reference/antioxidant/) defense: Oxyresveratrol and [resveratrol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#resveratrolresveratrol) scavenge free radicals, inhibit lipid peroxidation, and activate Nrf2/ARE pathway for endogenous antioxidant protection of skin
- [Antimicrobial](/nighantu/reference/antimicrobial/) and antisyphilitic activity: Traditional use for syphilis-related skin lesions supported by broad-spectrum antimicrobial activity of saponins and flavonoids against pathogenic microorganisms

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Chronic skin diseases (eczema, psoriasis): Validated by demonstrated anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory activities through NF-kB and T-cell modulation
- Syphilis-related skin manifestations: Traditional use supported by antimicrobial activity against various pathogens, though modern antibiotics have replaced this application
- Blood purification for skin clarity: Supported by [hepatoprotective](/nighantu/reference/hepatoprotective/) and detoxification-enhancing properties of saponins and polyphenols

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **Smilax china L.: A review of its botany, ethnopharmacology, phytochemistry, pharmacological activities** (2024): Comprehensive review identified over 200 chemical components including saponins, flavonoids, stilbenes, and polysaccharides, confirming pharmacological activities relevant to inflammatory skin disorders and immune modulation.
- **Tremendous health benefits and clinical aspects of Smilax china** (2019): Smilax china demonstrated anti-inflammatory, immunomodulatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties validating traditional use for skin diseases, pemphigus, chronic eczema, and psoriasis.
- **Overview of current research on traditional Chinese medicine in skin disease treatment: bibliometric analysis 2014-2024** (2024): Bibliometric analysis identified Smilax china (Baqia) as a significant traditional Chinese medicine for inflammatory skin disease treatment, with growing research interest in its stilbene and saponin compounds.

## Recent safety updates

- Long history of safe traditional use in both Ayurvedic and Traditional Chinese Medicine systems. No significant toxicity reported in available pharmacological studies at therapeutic doses
- Saponin content may cause mild gastrointestinal irritation in sensitive individuals with oral use. Topical application is generally well-tolerated. Limited formal long-term safety data in modern clinical settings

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Tablet, Capsule, Kwath Ghana (solid extract)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 50-100ml decoction; 500mg extract capsule; traditionally used in prolonged courses (6-12 weeks) for chronic conditions

**Bioavailability:** Smilax china—steroidal saponins ([sarsasapogenin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sarsasapogenin) glycosides) show moderate oral bioavailability (20-30%). Saponins undergo hydrolysis by gut microbiota to release aglycones which are better absorbed. Flavonoids (kaempferol, [quercetin](/nighantu/reference/quercetinquercetin/)) show moderate absorption. Decoction provides better saponin extraction than powder. Traditional prolonged administration (Prayoga Kala) ensures steady-state tissue levels for chronic conditions like Vatarakta (gout) and Upadamsha (syphilis).

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals with warm water for anti-arthritic and blood-purifying effects; prolonged course of 6-12 weeks recommended for chronic conditions

**Standardized Extract:** Tuber extract standardized to total saponins NLT 5%; sarsasapogenin NLT 0.5%; total flavonoids NLT 2%

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (powder); 3 years (tablet/capsule); 1 year (decoction)

**Storage:** Cool, dry place in airtight containers. Tuber powder is relatively stable. Protect from moisture to prevent [starch](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#starch)-related clumping.

**Marker Compounds:** Sarsasapogenin, Smilacin, Smilagenin, [Diosgenin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-d-g/#diosgenin), Kaempferol, [Quercetin](/nighantu/reference/quercetinquercetin/), [Resveratrol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#resveratrolresveratrol) (trace), Beta-[sitosterol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), [Stigmasterol](/nighantu/reference/stigmasterol/)

### Extraction Methods

- Aqueous decoction (1:16 reduced to 1:4) for traditional Kashayam
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (60:40) for saponin-rich extract
- Ethanol extraction (70%) for flavonoid and saponin co-extraction
- Maceration in cold water for 12-24 hours

### Synergistic Combinations

- With [Guduchi](/nighantu/herb/guduchi/) for Vatarakta (gouty arthritis)
- With [Sariva](/nighantu/herb/sariva/) for blood purification and skin diseases
- With [Manjishtha](/nighantu/herb/manjishtha/) for Raktadosha (blood disorders)
- With [Guggulu](/nighantu/formulation/guggulu/) for anti-inflammatory and anti-arthritic formulations
- With [Haridra](/nighantu/herb/haridra/) for chronic inflammatory conditions
