- **Toxicity profile of standardized Krishna Vajra [Abhraka bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/abhraka-bhasma/) made from biotite mica** (2024): Acute toxicity classified [Abhraka Bhasma](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/abhraka-bhasma/) in GHS Class V (safe) with LD50 > 2000 mg/kg. Shows protective role against ethyl methanesulfonate-induced chromosomal damage and enhanced constitutive DNA base excision repair in mice.
- **Abhrak Bhasma (Biotite mica nanoparticles) Induces Cytotoxicity in Adenocarcinoma Human Alveolar Basal Epithelial Cells (A549)** (2022): Demonstrated selective cytotoxicity against A549 lung adenocarcinoma cells, supporting potential anti-cancer application of mica nanoparticles.

## Recent safety updates

- GHS CLASS V (SAFE): LD50 > 2000 mg/kg in acute toxicity per OECD guidelines. Classified as non-toxic at single dose level.
- DOSE-DEPENDENT HEPATOTOXICITY: Mid and high doses induced REVERSIBLE hepatotoxicity in subacute studies. Low therapeutic doses remain non-toxic.
- NOT GENOTOXIC: Confirmed non-genotoxic and non-reproductive toxic in mouse models. Actually shows DNA repair enhancement against chemical mutagens.
