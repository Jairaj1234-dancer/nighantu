## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Taila (Safflower seed oil), Churna (flower/seed powder), Kashayam (flower decoction), Capsule, Lepa (flower paste for external use)

**Standard Dosage:** 5-10 mL safflower oil daily; 2-4 g flower powder; 30-50 mL flower decoction; 500 mg capsule twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** Oil: with meals; flower preparations: after meals; decoction for emmenagogue effect: during relevant menstrual phase under guidance

**Storage:** Safflower oil in dark glass bottles, refrigerated (2-8°C) after opening. Flower powder in airtight, light-protected containers at 15-25°C. Nitrogen-flushed packaging extends oil stability.

**Marker Compounds:** Hydroxysafflor yellow A (HSYA), Carthamin, Carthamidin, Safflor yellow B, Linoleic acid, Oleic acid, [Serotonin](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#serotonin) derivatives, N-feruloyl serotonin
