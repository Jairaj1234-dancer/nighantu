## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Swarasa (fresh juice), Capsule, Lepa (external paste)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-5 g powder twice daily; 50-80 mL decoction; 10-20 mL juice; external paste as needed

**Bioavailability:** [Triterpenoids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-t-z/#triterpenoids) and flavonoid [glycosides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) show moderate oral bioavailability. Mucilaginous [polysaccharides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polysaccharides) slow absorption but provide sustained release. Traditional co-administration with ghee or [sesame oil](/nighantu/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila) enhances lipophilic constituent absorption. Decoction form hydrolyzes glycosides improving aglycone availability.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals with appropriate Anupana (vehicle) based on condition; external applications twice daily

**Standardized Extract:** Standardized to minimum 3% total triterpenoids (as oleanolic acid equivalents) and 2% total [flavonoids](/nighantu/reference/flavonoids/)

**Shelf Life:** 18 months for powder; 24 months for capsules; decoction consumed within 24 hours; fresh juice within 6 hours

**Storage:** Store in airtight containers at 15-30°C. Protect from moisture and insect infestation. Mucilaginous preparations are prone to microbial growth — ensure moisture content <10%.

**Marker Compounds:** Oleanolic acid, Betulinic acid, Beta-[sitosterol](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), [Quercetin](/nighantu/reference/quercetinquercetin/), Gallic acid, [Tannins](/nighantu/reference/tannins/)

### Extraction Methods

- Water decoction (traditional)
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50-60% ethanol)
- Cold maceration for [mucilage](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#mucilage)-rich fraction
- Soxhlet extraction for comprehensive phytochemical profiling

### Synergistic Combinations

- [Kumuda](/nighantu/herb/kumuda/) and [Kumud](/nighantu/herb/kumud/) for cooling, Pitta-pacifying combinations
- [Padmaka](/nighantu/herb/padmaka/) for cardiac and cooling formulations
- [Sariva](/nighantu/herb/sariva/) for blood purification
- [Chandana](/nighantu/herb/chandana/) for urinary and cooling preparations
