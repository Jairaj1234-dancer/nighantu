## Key Phytochemical Constituents

- Nano-crystalline lead sulfide (PbS) ~60 nm as primary species
- Lead in Pb2+ oxidation state
- Residual lead oxide phases
- Organic content from herbal processing media
- Nutrient trace elements from herbs (Fe, Zn, Cu)

## How does it work?

- Proposed anti-diabetic action through pancreatic stimulation (traditional claim for Madhumeha), though modern evidence does not support lead as a safe therapeutic agent for diabetes
- Claimed Kaphahara (Kapha-reducing) properties through metabolic modulation, attributed to lead's heavy metal effects on cellular processes
- Pleehodara: traditionally described as having a splenic decongestant action. The mechanism is unknown, and modern research has not backed this traditional claim.
- Alleged anti-diarrheal action through astringent properties of lead compounds on intestinal mucosa

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Diabetes (Madhumeha): traditionally used for this condition. No modern clinical evidence shows lead to be safe for this use. The risk far outweighs any theoretical benefit.
- Spleen enlargement (Pleehodara): traditionally used for this condition. No clinical trial evidence exists for this use. Lead toxicity itself causes organ damage.
- Skin diseases (Kushtha): traditionally used for these conditions. This use is untested and dangerous. Lead exposure causes more skin and systemic toxicity.

## Recent safety updates

- EXTREME TOXICITY RISK: Lead has NO safe level of exposure per CDC, WHO, and FDA. Even properly prepared [Naga Bhasma](/herb/naga-bhasma/) contains lead as its primary constituent. FATAL CASES documented.
- FATAL CASE REPORT (2024): Death from lead toxicity in a Parkinson's patient taking Ayurvedic supplements containing lead. Blood lead levels were 9x above acceptable threshold.
- FDA EMBARGO AND WARNINGS: FDA has issued specific warnings against lead-containing Ayurvedic products (e.g., Balguti Kesaria). Products found to contain levels hundreds of thousands of times above FDA limits.
- NEUROLOGICAL DAMAGE: Lead is a proven neurotoxin causing irreversible brain damage, cognitive impairment, and developmental delays especially in children. No purification process can eliminate this risk.
- VICTORIAN HEALTH ADVISORY: Victoria Department of Health (January 2025) issued updated health advisory about Ayurvedic medicines containing lead and other heavy metals.
- 65% CONTAMINATION RATE: Studies found lead in 65% of 252 Ayurvedic medicine samples tested, indicating widespread contamination beyond just [Naga Bhasma](/herb/naga-bhasma/).

**Optimal Timing:** As directed by experienced physician; with specific anupana only

**Storage:** Airtight, labeled container; restricted drug storage
