## How does it work?

- [Antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) activity via flavonoid-mediated (hesperidin, eriocitrin) free radical scavenging and metal chelation
- Anti-inflammatory action through diosmin and hesperidin-mediated inhibition of TNF-alpha, IL-6 and NF-kB signaling
- [Anticancer](/reference/anticancer/) potential via D-limonene-induced apoptosis, phase II enzyme induction, and inhibition of tumor angiogenesis
- [Antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) activity through essential oil [monoterpenes](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#monoterpenes) disrupting bacterial and fungal cell membrane integrity

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Digestive aid (Deepana-Pachana): traditionally used as a digestive aid. Clinical observations have reported carminative and stomachic effects.
- Scurvy: traditionally used for scurvy and valued as a source of Vitamin C. Nutritional studies have reported it to be a source of Vitamin C.
- Antimicrobial and preservative: traditionally used as an antimicrobial and preservative. Food science and MIC studies have reported activity against multiple pathogens.
- Kidney stones: traditionally used for kidney stones. Clinical studies have reported an increase in urinary citrate.

## Recent safety updates

- Generally Recognized as Safe (GRAS) by FDA for food and flavoring use; lemon juice is safe at dietary levels
- Excessive consumption may cause dental erosion due to [citric acid](/glossary/compounds-c-d/#citric-acidcitric-acid) content; phototoxicity risk from [psoralen](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#psoralen)/furanocoumarin compounds in peel essential oil upon UV exposure

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Fresh juice, Oral liquid/syrup, Effervescent tablet, Lozenges, Spray-dried powder, Essential oil (external/aromatherapy), Cream/gel (topical)

**Standard Dosage:** 10-30 mL fresh lemon juice with water daily; 1-3 g spray-dried juice powder; 2-5 drops essential oil for aromatherapy; topical gel 2-5% citrus extract

**Optimal Timing:** Morning on empty stomach with warm water. Before meals as appetite stimulant. Post-meal for digestive comfort.

**Storage:** Fresh juice: 2-8 deg C, protect from light. Powder: below 25 deg C, RH <35%, moisture-barrier packaging with desiccant. Essential oil: amber glass, 15-25 deg C, tightly sealed. All forms: protect from heat and direct sunlight.

**Marker Compounds:** Limonene, Citric acid, Ascorbic acid (Vitamin C), Hesperidin, Eriocitrin, Citral (geranial + neral), Beta-pinene, Gamma-terpinene
