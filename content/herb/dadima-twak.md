## How does it work?

- Punicalagin and ellagic acid are hydrolyzed by gut microbiota to urolithins (A, B, C, D), which exert anti-inflammatory effects by inhibiting NF-kB and MAPK signaling pathways
- Pelletierine and isopelletierine [alkaloids](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) have been reported to have anthelmintic activity, paralyzing tapeworm musculature through nicotinic receptor agonism.
- Gallic acid and [catechins](/glossary/compounds-c-d/#catechins) provide [antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) activity through disruption of bacterial cell membrane integrity and inhibition of biofilm formation by chelating metal ions essential for bacterial adhesion

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- [Dadima](/herb/dadima/) Twak is traditionally used in Ayurveda for Krimi (intestinal parasites), including tapeworms. Pelletierine alkaloids have been identified in the rind.
- Traditionally used for Atisara (diarrhea) and Pravahika (dysentery). Its tannins have been reported to have an astringent effect on the intestinal mucosa.
- Pomegranate rind decoction is traditionally used for Mukha Roga (oral diseases). Antimicrobial activity against oral pathogens and gastrointestinal bacteria has been reported.

## Recent safety updates

- WHO monograph for Pericarpium Granati confirmed no genotoxic effects; Cosmetic Ingredient Review Expert Panel (2019) deemed pomegranate-derived ingredients safe for use
- Pelletierine alkaloids in bark/rind can cause nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea at high doses; traditional preparation methods (decoction) help control alkaloid concentration
- Pomegranate juice/extract may interact with CYP3A4-metabolized drugs (statins, calcium channel blockers) similarly to grapefruit; monitor patients on warfarin for enhanced anticoagulant effects

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Kashayam (decoction), Tablet, Capsule, Kwath (decoction concentrate)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6g powder twice daily; 50-100ml decoction twice daily; 500mg tablet/capsule twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals with warm water; for anthelmintic use, on an empty stomach in the morning

**Storage:** Store in airtight containers below 25 deg C, protected from light and moisture. Tannins oxidize on prolonged light exposure leading to darkening and potency loss.

**Marker Compounds:** Punicalagin (alpha and beta), Ellagic acid, Punicalin, Gallic acid, Granatin B

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Kutaja (Holarrhena), traditionally paired for Atisara (diarrhea)
- With Musta (Cyperus rotundus), traditionally paired as a GI astringent
- With [Haritaki](/herb/haritaki/), traditionally paired for Krimi (intestinal parasites)
- With Sunthi ([ginger](/herb/ginger/)), traditionally paired to offset its astringency
