## How does it work?

- Purgative action: Croton tiglium seeds contain croton oil with phorbol esters (particularly 12-O-tetradecanoylphorbol-13-acetate/TPA) that activate protein kinase C, stimulating intestinal secretion and peristalsis causing powerful catharsis
- Mercury (as HgS from Kajjali) historically was used as a purgative in Western medicine as well (calomel); the mercurial component may enhance the cathartic action through irritation of intestinal mucosa
- Shunthi ([ginger](/nighantu/herb/ginger/)) and Maricha (pepper) serve as bioavailability enhancers and carminatives to reduce the griping pain associated with the powerful purgative action

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Strong purgation (Virechana) for Panchakarma: The pharmacological basis is well-established - Croton oil's phorbol esters are potent activators of intestinal secretion and motility, validating the traditional use as a powerful cathartic
- Ascites (Jalodara) and severe constipation: Historical use in fluid retention and obstinate constipation is mechanistically supported by the formulation's powerful secretagogue and peristalsis-stimulating properties

## Recent safety updates

- CRITICAL: This is an extremely potent purgative - Croton tiglium is classified as highly toxic; even small overdoses can cause severe bloody diarrhea, abdominal cramping, dehydration, and electrolyte imbalance
- Contains mercury as Kajjali (HgS) - in addition to purgative risks, mercury content adds nephrotoxicity and neurotoxicity concerns
- Self-medication is EXPLICITLY DANGEROUS - multiple sources warn against unsupervised use; should only be administered by experienced Ayurvedic physicians in controlled settings as part of Panchakarma Virechana therapy
- Not for regular or repeated use - intended as a single-dose or short-course therapeutic purgative only; prolonged use can cause severe GI damage, electrolyte depletion, and mercury accumulation
- Strictly contraindicated in pregnancy, lactation, children, elderly, debilitated patients, inflammatory bowel disease, hemorrhoids, and renal/hepatic impairment

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Vati

**Standard Dosage:** 125–250 mg (potent purgative — single dose or as directed)

**Bioavailability:** Rapid onset purgative action within 4–6 hours

**Optimal Timing:** Bedtime with warm water or [Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/) Kwatha

**Standardized Extract:** Kajjali (purified mercury-sulfur complex) with [Jayapala](/nighantu/herb/jayapala/) (Croton)

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite

**Storage:** Airtight container; clearly labeled as potent purgative

**Marker Compounds:** Mercury sulfide, Croton oil (from [Jayapala](/nighantu/herb/jayapala/)), Sulfur compounds

**Quality Parameters:** Free mercury <1 ppm, standard Rasaushadhi tests, uniform potency

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water or [Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/) Kwatha
