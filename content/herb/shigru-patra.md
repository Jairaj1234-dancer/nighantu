## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (powder), Capsule, Tablet, Swarasa (fresh juice), Tea/Infusion, Vati (tablet), Granules

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g leaf powder per day; 500-1000 mg standardized extract capsule twice daily; 10-20 mL swarasa daily; 1-2 cups infusion daily

**Bioavailability:** Isothiocyanate compounds (derived from [glucosinolates](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#glucosinolates) by [myrosinase](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#myrosinase) activation) show good oral bioavailability (50-70%) when consumed from fresh/minimally processed leaves. Heat processing above 70 deg C inactivates myrosinase and reduces isothiocyanate formation — use raw or mildly heated preparations. [Quercetin](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/quercetinquercetin/) and [kaempferol](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/kaempferol/) [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) show moderate bioavailability (20-35%). Iron and calcium from leaves have moderate bioavailability (10-20%), enhanced by vitamin C content.

**Optimal Timing:** Morning with breakfast for nutritional supplementation; before meals for blood sugar management

**Standardized Extract:** Freeze-dried leaf powder retaining NLT 0.5% total [isothiocyanates](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#isothiocyanates) (as moringin equivalents); standardized extract (5:1) NLT 3% total [flavonoids](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/flavonoids/) (as [quercetin](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/quercetinquercetin/) equivalents) and NLT 1% chlorogenic acid by HPLC

**Shelf Life:** 18 months for leaf powder in nitrogen-flushed packets; 24 months for capsules; 12 months for tea bags

**Storage:** Store below 25 deg C in airtight, light-proof containers. Nitrogen-flushed packaging critical for powder to prevent [chlorophyll](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-c-d/#chlorophyll) degradation and lipid oxidation. RH <50%.

**Marker Compounds:** Moringin (4-(alpha-L-rhamnopyranosyloxy)benzyl isothiocyanate), Quercetin-3-glucoside, Kaempferol-3-glucoside, Chlorogenic acid, Beta-carotene, Niazimicin, Niazirin

### Extraction Methods

- Low-temperature aqueous extraction (<50 deg C to preserve myrosinase)
- Hydroalcoholic extraction (50-60% ethanol)
- Fresh leaf juice (cold pressing)
- Spray-drying of aqueous extract at low temperature
- Freeze-drying for maximum nutrient and enzyme preservation

### Synergistic Combinations

- Amalaki (vitamin C enhances iron absorption from [Moringa](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/moringa/))

- [Ashwagandha](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ashwagandha/) ([adaptogenic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/adaptogenic/) and nutritive synergy)
- [Guduchi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/guduchi/) ([immunomodulatory](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/immunomodulatory/) formulations)
