## How does it work?

- Piperine enhances bioavailability of co-administered drugs/nutrients by inhibiting CYP3A4 and P-glycoprotein efflux pump, and stimulating intestinal amino acid transporters
- Piperlongumine has been reported to raise reactive oxygen species (ROS) in cancer cells through inhibition of glutathione S-transferase pi 1 (GSTP1), with less effect on normal cells with adequate [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) defenses
- Sesamin and diaeudesmin from root exhibit anti-inflammatory effects through dual inhibition of COX-2 and 5-LOX pathways, reducing both prostaglandin and leukotriene synthesis

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Yogavahi (bioenhancer): traditionally used to enhance the action of co-administered substances. Pharmacological studies have reported that piperine can increase the absorption of co-administered compounds.
- Shwasa, Kasa (respiratory disease): traditionally used for respiratory conditions. Studies have reported anti-inflammatory, bronchodilatory, and [immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/) effects. High-quality randomized controlled trials are still lacking.
- Deepana-Pachana (digestive stimulant): traditionally used to stimulate digestion. Studies have reported increased gastric secretion and gastrointestinal motility.

## Recent safety updates

- [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) Moola used in over 135 classical Ayurvedic formulations with long traditional safety record; however, no standardized human clinical safety data available
- Piperine's bioavailability enhancement can increase plasma levels of co-administered drugs (phenytoin, theophylline, propranolol); significant drug interaction potential requiring monitoring
- Avoid high doses during pregnancy (potential uterotonic effects); may exacerbate gastric acidity in patients with GERD or peptic ulcers

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (root powder), Kwatha (decoction), Capsule, Tablet, [Ghrita](/herb/ghrita/), Vati

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3 g root powder daily; 15-20 mL decoction twice daily; 500 mg standardized root extract twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** After meals for digestive benefits. Before meals with honey for respiratory conditions. Part of [Trikatu](/herb/trikatu/) or digestive formulations taken with first morsel of food.

**Storage:** Dried root: cool, dry, ventilated storage, below 25 deg C, protected from pest infestation. Powder and extracts: airtight containers, below 25 deg C, RH <55%, protected from light.

**Marker Compounds:** Piperine, Piperlongumine, Piperlonguminine, Methyl piperate, Sesamin, Dihydrostigmasterol, [Piperanine](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#piperanine), Brachystamide B
