## What it is

The record classes Mukha-Abhyaṅga as *kriyā-bāhya*, an external procedure, glossed in English as therapeutic facial massage. *Mukha* is the face and *abhyaṅga* is oil massage. The record's variant name, "intensive", distinguishes it from a lighter cosmetic version but is not defined anywhere in the record.

## What it involves

Two recorded steps, and nothing else:

1. Warm medicated *taila* applied with massage over marma points.
2. A session of 20 to 30 minutes.

The record gives no preparation, no aftercare, no course length, no frequency and no seasonal guidance. For the marma component, see [Marma-Cikitsā](/nighantu/practice/marma-cikitsa/), where the classical basis of the 107 marma points is set out along with the important caveat that the classical chapter maps them as sites to protect rather than as points of treatment.

## What is used

The record names two oils:

- **Aṇu Taila**
- **Mahānārāyaṇa Taila**

One of those is worth a question. Aṇu Taila is classically a nasal oil: its formulation is given in Aṣṭāṅga Hṛdaya Sūtra Sthāna 20, the chapter on *nasya* or nasal instillation, at verses 37 to 38, and it is also referred to Caraka Sūtra 5. Its classical route of use is instillation into the nose, not application to the face. Its presence in a facial-massage record may reflect current practice, or may be a curation error, and it is a fair thing to raise with a practitioner who proposes it.

## Where the citation stands

The record cites *Sahasrayogam*. As with the other Kerala-tradition entries in this dataset, Sahasrayogam is structured as a formulary of formulations rather than a manual of procedures, and I could not confirm that it describes Mukha-Abhyaṅga. Recorded as unverified.

## What to ask before booking

- which oil, and if Aṇu Taila is proposed, why an oil formulated for nasal instillation is being used on the face
- whether the oil has been used on your skin before, and what is done if it does not suit
- how pressure over facial marma points is judged
- how many sessions are proposed, since the record gives no course length
