## What it is

Hṛdaya Basti is one of the localised oil-pool treatments, in which basti means a reservoir rather than the internal enema of the same name. The source record classes it as kriyā-bāhya, an external procedure, in the hṛdaya subcategory, and glosses it as cardiac region oil pool, meaning the anatomical site rather than any claim about what it does. It is also known as Uro Basti.

## What a session involves

The record gives three steps: a dough ring built on the chest over the precordial area; warm taila pooled inside it; and thirty to forty-five minutes of retention. It does not specify the dough material, the oil temperature, how warmth is maintained, or anything done before or after.

## What is used

There is an internal inconsistency in the record worth flagging. Its procedure step names Mahānārāyaṇa or Aśvagandhā taila, while its separate list of common oils gives Mahānārāyaṇa Taila, Bala Taila and Kṣīrabalā Taila. Aśvagandhā taila appears in one field and not the other, and Bala and Kṣīrabalā appear in the other and not the first. Mahānārāyaṇa Taila is the only oil the record names consistently in both places.

## Where the citation stands

The record cites Sahasrayogam and itself qualifies this as regional Kerala. The qualification is closer to the truth than the citation. Secondary sources place the textual references for Uro Basti in Kerala works, specifically Yogāmṛta and Cikitsāmañjarī, rather than in the major saṃhitās; the closely related Kaṭi Basti is likewise attributed to Cikitsāmañjarī and described as absent from the classical textbooks. Sahasrayogam is a formulary organised by preparation type, which makes it a plausible source for the oils and an implausible one for the procedure. See the citation note.

## What to ask before booking

The record carries no contraindications, no red flags, no adverse-event data, no frequency and no course length, so it constrains nothing about who this is suitable for. Worth establishing with the practitioner: which oil, and why that one given the record's own inconsistency; how the oil temperature is set and held across the retention period; and what screening they do before applying sustained warmth over the chest.
