Traditional enema apparatus consisting of a bladder-like bag (Basti Putaka, made from animal bladder or leather) attached to a tube (Basti Netra, made from metal). Used to administer medicated decoctions (Niruha Basti) or oils (Anuvasana Basti) rectally.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sushruta Samhita Chikitsasthana Chapter 35-38; Charaka Samhita Siddhisthana Chapters 1-12. Basti is considered 'Ardha Chikitsa' (half of all treatment) by Charaka.

## How is it used traditionally?

Primary therapy for Vata disorders, constipation, lower back pain, sciatica, arthritis, neurological conditions. Niruha Basti (decoction enema) for cleansing; Anuvasana Basti (oil enema) for nourishment.

## Traditional materials

- Animal bladder (Basti Putaka)
- Metal nozzle/tube (Basti Netra) made of gold, silver, or brass
- Leather bag
- Bone or ivory nozzle tips

## Modern variants

- Stainless steel Basti cans
- Rubber enema bags
- Disposable PVC enema kits
- Silicone nozzles

## Regulatory status

- **India Cdsco:** Modern enema equipment is classified under Class A/B medical devices when marketed for therapeutic use.
- **Us Fda:** Enema kits are FDA-regulated Class I medical devices (Product Code FEF/FEG). 510(k) exempt in many cases.
- **Eu Mdr:** Classified under Class I or IIa depending on invasiveness and intended use under EU MDR 2017/745.
