A set of equipment for Pizhichil (Sarvanga Dhara) therapy where warm medicated oil is continuously poured over the entire body from a height while simultaneously massaging. Includes oil vessels, squeezing cloth, Droni (trough/tub), and oil warming apparatus.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Kerala Ayurvedic tradition (Ashtavaidya texts); references in Sahasrayogam. Also known as Sarvanga Sneha Dhara. Called 'the royal treatment' (Raja Sweda) as it was traditionally reserved for royalty.

## How is it used traditionally?

Rheumatic diseases, paralysis (Pakshavadha), neurological disorders, musculoskeletal degeneration, sexual debility, anti-aging, and complete body rejuvenation (Rasayana).

## Traditional materials

- Cotton cloth pieces (Vasana) for squeezing oil
- Brass or copper oil vessel (Dhara Patra)
- Wooden Droni (trough/tub) for patient to lie in
- Earthen pot for warming oil

## Modern variants

- Automatic oil circulation systems
- Thermostat-controlled oil warmers
- Stainless steel Droni tubs
- Fiber-body treatment tables with oil collection channels
