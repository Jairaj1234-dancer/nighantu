Specialized tools designed for stimulating Marma points (vital energy points described in Ayurveda and Kalari Payattu martial art). Include pointed wooden sticks, rounded metal tips, and ergonomic handheld devices for applying precise pressure to the 107 Marma points on the body.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sushruta Samhita Sharira Sthana Chapter 6 (Pratyeka Marma Nirdesham Shariram) describes 107 Marma points. Ashtanga Hridaya Sharira Sthana Chapter 4. Marma therapy also described in Kalari martial art traditions of Kerala.

## How is it used traditionally?

Stimulation of Marma points for pain relief, energy balancing, muscle relaxation, improved circulation, stress relief, and treatment of specific organ systems connected to each Marma. Part of Marma Chikitsa (Marma therapy).

## Traditional materials

- Sandalwood sticks ([Chandana](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/chandana/) Danda)
- Ivory points (historical)
- Metal-tipped probes
- Therapist's fingers and thumbs

## Modern variants

- Wooden Marma sticks (ergonomic design)
- Kansa-tipped Marma tools
- Acupressure-style Marma point devices
- Electric Marma stimulation devices

## Regulatory status

- **India Cdsco:** Not classified as medical devices. Marketed as wellness tools.
- **Us Fda:** Not regulated as medical devices unless electronic or making specific medical claims. Acupressure devices are generally unregulated.
- **Eu Mdr:** Not classified as medical devices. May require classification if electronic stimulation is involved.
