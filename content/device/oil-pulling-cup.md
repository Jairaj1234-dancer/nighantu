A small cup or vessel used to measure and hold medicated oils, decoctions, or other liquids for Kavala (gargling/swishing with smaller quantity) and Gandusha (holding liquid to full mouth capacity) practices.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Sutrasthana Chapter 5 (Dinacharya); Sushruta Samhita Chikitsasthana Chapter 40; Ashtanga Hridaya Sutrasthana Chapter 22.

## How is it used traditionally?

Traditionally used for oral health, the teeth and gums, jaw disorders, voice quality and oral ulcers. Recommended with [sesame oil](/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila) ([Tila](/herb/tila/) Taila) as primary medium.

## Traditional materials

- Copper (Tamra)
- Silver (Rajata)
- Brass (Pittala)
- Earthenware

## Modern variants

- Stainless steel measuring cups
- Glass cups
- Pre-measured oil pulling sachets

## Regulatory status

- **India (CDSCO):** Not classified as a medical device. The cup itself is a general vessel.
- **US (FDA):** Not regulated as a medical device. The oils used may be subject to cosmetic or supplement regulations.
- **EU (MDR):** Not classified as a medical device.
