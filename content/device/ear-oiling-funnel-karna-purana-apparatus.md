A small funnel or cone-shaped device used to instill warm medicated oil into the ear canal during Karna Purana therapy. Modern versions include temperature-controlled oil dispensers.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Sutrasthana Chapter 5 (Dinacharya); Sushruta Samhita Chikitsasthana. Karna Purana (filling the ears with oil) is part of daily Ayurvedic routine.

## How is it used traditionally?

Ear disorders (tinnitus, hearing loss, earache), TMJ disorders, neck stiffness, Vata balance, and prevention of ear diseases. Commonly used oils: [Bilva](/nighantu/herb/bilva/) Taila, [Apamarga](/nighantu/herb/apamarga/) Kshara Taila.

## Traditional materials

- Silver funnel
- Copper dropper
- Cotton wick soaked in warm oil

## Modern variants

- Silicone ear funnels
- Glass droppers with bulb
- Temperature-controlled ear oil dispensers

## Regulatory status

- **India Cdsco:** Not specifically classified as a medical device.
- **Us Fda:** Ear drops delivery devices may fall under Class I/II medical devices depending on design and claims.
- **Eu Mdr:** May require classification if marketed as a medical device for ear treatment.
