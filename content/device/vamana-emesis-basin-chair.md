A wide basin or receptacle used to collect vomitus during therapeutic emesis (Vamana karma). The Vamana Peeth is a specialized chair allowing the patient to sit comfortably while leaning forward during the procedure.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Kalpasthana Chapters 1-12; Sushruta Samhita Chikitsasthana Chapter 33. Vamana is the first of the five Panchakarma procedures.

## How is it used traditionally?

Therapeutic emesis for Kapha-dominant disorders: bronchial asthma, chronic cold, skin diseases (Kushtha), obesity, diabetes (Prameha), and lymphatic congestion.

## Traditional materials

- Brass (Pittala) basin
- Copper (Tamra) basin
- Earthenware (Mrittika)
- Stainless steel

## Modern variants

- Stainless steel emesis basins
- Adjustable Vamana therapy chairs
- Graduated measuring vessels for Vegas (bouts) assessment

## Regulatory status

- **India Cdsco:** Not classified as a medical device. Considered general therapy furniture/equipment.
- **Us Fda:** Emesis basins are general hospital supplies, not requiring specific FDA clearance.
- **Eu Mdr:** Not classified as a medical device.
