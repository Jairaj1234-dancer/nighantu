A set of metal rods (Shalaka) of specific shapes - Suchi (needle-point), Bindu (dot/round), Valaya (ring), Vilekha (line), Pratapta (red-hot) - for Agnikarma (therapeutic thermal cauterization). Also includes [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) (long pepper), Godanta (bovine tooth), and other organic materials that can be heated for cauterization of specific tissues.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sushruta Samhita Sutrasthana Chapter 12 (Agnikarmavidhi Adhyaya).

## How is it used traditionally?

The source notes list its traditional use for chronic musculoskeletal pain, plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, frozen shoulder, calcaneal spur, warts, corn, hemorrhoids and skin tags.

## Traditional materials

- Panchadhatu (five-metal alloy) rods - for muscle/bone cauterization
- Suvarna (gold) rods - for delicate areas
- Iron (Loha) rods - general use
- [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) (Piper longum) - for skin (twak) cauterization
- Godanta (bovine tooth) - for skin (twak) cauterization
- [Madhu](/herb/madhu/) (honey) and [Guda](/glossary/concepts-a-m/#guda) (jaggery) - for vessel, ligament, joint and bone (sira, snayu, sandhi, asthi) cauterization

## Modern variants

- Stainless steel Agnikarma instruments
- Electrocautery adaptations
- Temperature-guided cautery devices

## Regulatory status

- **India (CDSCO):** Traditional Agnikarma instruments not separately classified. Practice regulated under AYUSH para-surgical guidelines. Electrocautery devices are Class B/C medical devices.
- **US (FDA):** Electrocautery equivalents are Class II medical devices. Traditional rods not specifically classified.
- **EU (MDR):** Electrocautery devices classified Class IIa/IIb.
