Equipment for Udvartana therapy involving vigorous upward-stroke dry powder massage or herbal paste massage. Includes powder storage vessels, mixing bowls, and specialized textured gloves or hand tools for application.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Sutrasthana Chapter 5 (Dinacharya); Ashtanga Hridaya Sutrasthana Chapter 2. Charaka describes Udvartana as one of the daily health practices that imparts firmness to limbs, dissolves fat, and gives skin a healthy glow.

## How is it used traditionally?

Obesity management (Sthaulya), Kapha disorders, cellulite, lymphatic drainage, skin exfoliation, improving skin complexion, and strengthening body tissues.

## Traditional materials

- Stone mortar for grinding herbal powders
- Cotton cloth for application
- Copper or stone vessels for powder storage

## Modern variants

- Pre-mixed Udvartana powder kits
- Textured massage gloves
- Automated powder application devices
