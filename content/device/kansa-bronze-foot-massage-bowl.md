A small bronze bowl used with ghee to massage the soles of the feet, stimulating reflex zones and Marma points on the feet. A traditional Kerala practice (Kansa Vataki) modernized as a wellness tool.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Pada Abhyanga (foot massage) described in Charaka Samhita Sutrasthana Chapter 5 (Dinacharya). Kansa as therapeutic metal described in Rasaratna Samuchaya. The Kansa Vataki tradition is from Kerala Ayurvedic lineages.

## How is it used traditionally?

Foot massage for improving sleep, reducing eye strain, calming Pitta, stimulating internal organs via foot reflex zones, relieving fatigue, and promoting relaxation.

## Traditional materials

- Kansa (bronze - copper + tin alloy)
- Cow ghee (Goghrita) as lubricant

## Modern variants

- Ergonomic Kansa foot bowls
- Kansa foot massage plates
- Combined Kansa bowl and wand sets
