A vessel containing sand in which another smaller vessel (containing the material to be heated) is placed. The sand distributes heat evenly and prevents direct flame contact, providing gentle and uniform heating.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Rasaratna Samuchaya; Rasa Tarangini. Described as a fundamental heating apparatus in Rasashastra.

## How is it used traditionally?

Gentle, uniform heating for Swedana (boiling) of minerals, preparation of medicated oils (Taila Paka), and other processes requiring controlled heating without scorching.

## Traditional materials

- Iron or earthen outer vessel
- Clean sand (Valuka/Sikata)
- Inner glass or earthen processing vessel

## Modern variants

- Electric sand baths with thermostatic control
- Laboratory heating mantles
- Oil baths
