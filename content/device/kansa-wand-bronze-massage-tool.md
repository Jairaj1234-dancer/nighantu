A dome-shaped massage tool with a handle, made from Kansa (traditional bronze alloy of copper and tin in specific proportions - approximately 78% copper and 22% tin). Used for facial and body massage. The metal is considered a 'healing metal' in Ayurveda that balances skin pH and electromagnetic energy.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Kansa (bronze/bell-metal) is described in Rasaratna Samuchaya and Charaka Samhita as balancing Pitta and purifying blood. The wand format is a modern innovation based on traditional Kansa Thali (bronze plate) foot massage practiced in Kerala.

## How is it used traditionally?

Facial massage for lymphatic drainage, reducing puffiness, improving circulation, balancing skin pH, reducing fine lines, Marma point stimulation on the face, de-stressing, and promoting skin glow.

## Traditional materials

- Kansa (bronze) - copper and tin alloy
- Rosewood or sandalwood handle

## Modern variants

- Various sizes (small face wand, large body wand)
- Kansa Gua Sha tools
- Kansa face rollers
- Kansa foot massage bowls
