India's first CDSCO-acknowledged Ayurvedic diagnostic device. A patented AI-based system that uses three highly sensitive pressure sensors to replicate the manual Nadi Pariksha process. Records pulse in 1 minute and generates a report with 22 Ayurvedic health parameters including Tridosha balance, stress levels, digestive health, and overall wellbeing with over 85% accuracy.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Based on principles from Sharangdhara Samhita, Bhavaprakasha, and Yogaratnakara Nadi Pariksha chapters, digitized using modern sensor technology and AI algorithms.

## How is it used traditionally?

Objective, reproducible Ayurvedic pulse diagnosis. Assessment of Tridosha balance, stress levels, digestive function, metabolic health, and wellness parameters. Used in clinical practice, research, and preventive health screening.

- **India Cdsco:** CDSCO license applied and acknowledged. AIPL is ISO 13485:2016 certified. First Ayurvedic diagnostic device to receive CDSCO recognition.
- **Us Fda:** Not FDA-cleared. Would likely require 510(k) or De Novo classification as a novel diagnostic device.
- **Eu Mdr:** Not CE-marked. Would require classification under EU MDR, likely Class IIa as a non-invasive diagnostic device.
