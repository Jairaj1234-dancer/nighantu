Tools for preparing and applying medicated herbal pastes (Lepa) to the body. Includes stone mortar for grinding, spatulas for mixing, and brushes or flat applicators for spreading paste on affected areas.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Chikitsasthana (various chapters); Sushruta Samhita Chikitsasthana. Sharangdhara Samhita Madhyama Khanda Chapter 11 describes Lepa Kalpana in detail - paste should be applied against the direction of hair growth (Pratiloma), thickness of buffalo hide.

## How is it used traditionally?

Skin diseases (Kushtha), inflammation (Shotha), pain relief, wound healing, cosmetic applications (Mukha Lepa/facial), joint disorders, headache (Shirolepa).

## Traditional materials

- Stone slab and pestle (Shila Pattika) for grinding
- Wooden spatula (Daru Lepa Patra)
- Copper or bronze mixing bowl
- Banana leaf or lotus leaf (Padma Patra) as applicator

## Modern variants

- Silicone spatulas
- Synthetic brush applicators
- Electric herb grinders
- Pre-mixed paste tubes

## Regulatory status

- **India Cdsco:** Application tools not classified as medical devices. Herbal pastes regulated as Ayurvedic medicines.
- **Us Fda:** Not regulated as medical devices.
- **Eu Mdr:** Not classified as medical devices.
