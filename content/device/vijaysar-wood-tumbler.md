A tumbler carved from the heartwood of [Vijaysar](/nighantu/herb/vijaysar/) (Pterocarpus marsupium / Indian Kino Tree / Beejaka). Water stored overnight in this tumbler takes on a reddish-brown color and active phytochemicals ([pterostilbene](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#pterostilbene), epicatechin, marsupsin) that leach from the wood.

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Bhavaprakasha Nighantu (Vatadi Varga) describes [Vijaysar](/nighantu/herb/vijaysar/)/Beejaka as Pramehaghna (anti-diabetic). Charaka Samhita lists [Asana](/nighantu/herb/asana/)/Beejaka under Tikta Rasayana herbs. Sharangdhara Samhita references its use.

## How is it used traditionally?

Blood sugar management (Prameha/Madhumeha), cholesterol reduction, anti-obesity, liver support, and metabolic syndrome. Water is consumed empty stomach for 30+ days.

## Traditional materials

- Pterocarpus marsupium heartwood ([Vijaysar](/nighantu/herb/vijaysar/)/Beejaka/[Asana](/nighantu/herb/asana/))

## Modern variants

- Machine-turned [Vijaysar](/nighantu/herb/vijaysar/) tumblers
- [Vijaysar](/nighantu/herb/vijaysar/) wooden cups with metal lining outside

## Regulatory status

- **India Cdsco:** Not classified as a medical device. However, [Vijaysar](/nighantu/herb/vijaysar/)-based oral preparations are approved Ayurvedic medicines under AYUSH. The tumbler is sold as a traditional wellness product.
- **Us Fda:** Not regulated as a medical device or drug. Would be considered a dietary supplement delivery method if health claims are made.
- **Eu Mdr:** Not classified as a medical device.
