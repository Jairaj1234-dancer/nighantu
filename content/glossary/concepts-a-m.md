| Property | Value | Description |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| **Rasa** (Taste) | Madhura, Kashaya | Primary taste |
| **Guna** (Quality) | Laghu, Ruksha | Physical quality |
| **Virya** (Potency) | Sheeta | Heating/Cooling effect |

#### What is it made of?

### Nutritional Profile

- **Protein:** 3.6g/100ml
- **Fat:** 3.8g/100ml (rich in medium-chain fatty acids)
- **Lactose:** 4.1g/100ml
- **Calcium:** 134mg/100ml
- **Phosphorus:** 111mg/100ml
- **Vitamin A:** 185 IU/100ml
- **Oligosaccharides:** Rich (prebiotic)

#### Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Fresh milk, Boiled milk, Medicated milk (Kshirapaka)

**Standard Dosage:** 100–200 mL twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** Morning or before bed; warm

**Storage:** Refrigerated at 4°C

**Marker Compounds:** A2 beta-casein, Medium-chain triglycerides, Calcium, [Phosphorus](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#phosphorus)

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Used as anupana for [Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/) herbs and bhasmas

Highly stable due to >90% saturated fat content. Resistant to oxidation

### DistillationSublimation Apparatus Directional {#distillationsublimation-apparatus-directional}

Apparatus for directional sublimation or distillation: Urdhwa Patana (upward), Adha/Adhah Patana (downward), and Tiryak Patana (lateral/sideways). Each direction uses a different pot arrangement.

#### Where is it described in the classical texts?

Rasa Tarangini Taranga 4; Rasaratna Samuchaya; Rasaprakasha Sudhakara.

#### How is it used traditionally?

Sublimation and distillation of mercury, sulfur, arsenic, and other volatile mineral substances at different temperature ranges and collection directions.

#### Traditional materials

- Earthen pots (Mrit Patra)
- Bamboo or metal connecting tubes
- Mud-cloth-plaster sealing

#### Modern variants

- Glass retort apparatus
- Metal distillation columns
- Rotary evaporators

#### Regulatory status

- **India (CDSCO):** Pharmaceutical equipment under AYUSH GMP.
- **US (FDA):** Subject to cGMP requirements.
- **EU (MDR):** Not classified as a medical device.

**Optimal Timing:** With honey or ghee

Preparation of Suvarna Prashana (gold-based immunization), storage of precious Bhasma preparations, preparation of medicines requiring inert and non-reactive containers.
