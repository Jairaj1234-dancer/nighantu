### Aja Dugdha {#aja-dugdha}

#### Names and identification

| Language | Name |
|----------|------|
| English | [Aja Dugdha](/nighantu/glossary/concepts-a-m/#aja-dugdha) |

#### Ayurvedic pharmacology (Dravyaguna)

| Property | Value | Description |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| **Rasa** (Taste) | Madhura, Kashaya | Primary taste |
| **Guna** (Quality) | Laghu, Ruksha | Physical quality |
| **Virya** (Potency) | Sheeta | Heating/Cooling effect |
| **Vipaka** (Post-digestive) | Madhura | Post-digestive effect |

#### What is it made of?

### Nutritional Profile

- **Protein:** 3.6g/100ml
- **Fat:** 3.8g/100ml (rich in medium-chain fatty acids)
- **Lactose:** 4.1g/100ml
- **Calcium:** 134mg/100ml
- **Phosphorus:** 111mg/100ml
- **Vitamin A:** 185 IU/100ml
- **Oligosaccharides:** Rich (prebiotic)

**Analytical Methods:** AOAC methods, HPLC (sugars), ICP-OES (minerals)

#### Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Fresh milk, Boiled milk, Medicated milk (Kshirapaka)

**Standard Dosage:** 100–200 mL twice daily

**Bioavailability:** High; easily digestible due to smaller fat globules and A2 casein

**Optimal Timing:** Morning or before bed; warm

**Standardized Extract:** N/A — used as whole food/vehicle

**Shelf Life:** Fresh: 24–48 hours refrigerated

**Storage:** Refrigerated at 4°C

**Marker Compounds:** A2 beta-casein, Medium-chain triglycerides, Calcium, [Phosphorus](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#phosphorus)

**Quality Parameters:** Fat content 3.5–4%, SNF >8%, no adulteration ([starch](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-q-t/#starch)/urea negative)

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Used as anupana for [Rasayana](/nighantu/reference/rasayana/) herbs and bhasmas

**Bioavailability:** Limited modern pharmacokinetic data. Traditional herb with limited modern investigation. Presumed moderate bioavailability based on phytochemical class ([flavonoids](/nighantu/reference/flavonoids/), [terpenoids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-t-z/#terpenoids)). **Standardized Extract:** Limited standardization data. Develop HPTLC fingerprint for identity confirmation. Document extractive values (water-soluble and alcohol-soluble).

- Fresh juice expression
- Cold maceration

- With [Nimba](/nighantu/herb/nimba/) for skin disorders

### DistillationSublimation Apparatus Directional {#distillationsublimation-apparatus-directional}

Apparatus for directional sublimation or distillation: Urdhwa Patana (upward), Adha/Adhah Patana (downward), and Tiryak Patana (lateral/sideways). Each direction uses a different pot arrangement.

#### Where is it described in the classical texts?

Rasa Tarangini Taranga 4; Rasaratna Samuchaya; Rasaprakasha Sudhakara.

#### How is it used traditionally?

Sublimation and distillation of mercury, sulfur, arsenic, and other volatile mineral substances at different temperature ranges and collection directions.

#### Traditional materials

- Earthen pots (Mrit Patra)
- Bamboo or metal connecting tubes
- Mud-cloth-plaster sealing

#### Modern variants

- Glass retort apparatus
- Metal distillation columns
- Rotary evaporators

#### Regulatory status

- **India Cdsco:** Pharmaceutical equipment under AYUSH GMP.
- **Us Fda:** Subject to cGMP requirements.
- **Eu Mdr:** Not classified as a medical device.

### Gold Silver Vessels for Medicine Preparation {#gold-silver-vessels-for-medicine-preparation}

Vessels made of gold (Suvarna) or silver (Rajata) used for preparation and storage of high-value Ayurvedic medicines, especially Bhasma and Prash preparations. Gold and silver have specific therapeutic attributes in Rasashastra.

#### Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita describes gold vessels as Tridosha-balancing and silver as cooling (Sheeta). Rasaratna Samuchaya extensively describes properties of gold and silver in medicine.

#### How is it used traditionally?

Preparation of Suvarna Prashana (gold-based immunization), storage of precious Bhasma preparations, preparation of medicines requiring inert and non-reactive containers.

#### Traditional materials

- Pure gold (Suvarna)
- Pure silver (Rajata)

#### Modern variants

- Gold-plated vessels
- Silver-coated copper vessels

#### Regulatory status

- **India Cdsco:** Not classified as medical devices.
- **Us Fda:** Not regulated as medical devices.
- **Eu Mdr:** Not classified as medical devices.

Localized blood-letting for Vata-Rakta disorders, musculoskeletal pain, deep-tissue blood stagnation.
