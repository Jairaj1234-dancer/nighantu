## Key Ingredients

- Amalaki (Phyllanthus emblica / Indian gooseberry) - primary ingredient
- [Ghrita](/herb/ghrita/) (cow ghee)
- [Madhu](/herb/madhu/) (honey)
- Ratio: Amalaki powder : Ghee : Honey = 2:1:4

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Chikitsa Sthana (Rasayana Adhyaya) - described as Vayasthapana Rasayana; Ashtanga Hridayam - designated as foremost among all Rasayana plants

## How does it work?

- High vitamin C content enhances neutrophil function and collagen synthesis, supporting immune defense and tissue repair
- Tannin complexes slow reactive oxygen species (ROS) formation, providing potent [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) cellular protection
- Flavonoid [glycosides](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) influence cellular pathways related to inflammation, apoptosis, and cell proliferation
- Telomerase activation prevents erosion of telomeres in aged individuals, potentially slowing cellular aging
- DNA strand break repair maintained stably through enhanced nucleotide excision and base excision repair mechanisms

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Vayasthapana (anti-aging/rejuvenation): traditionally used for rejuvenation. A clinical study in humans reported telomerase activation and maintenance of telomere length.
- Hridya ([cardioprotective](/reference/cardioprotective/)): traditionally used for the heart. A study in a hypertrophy model reported improved cardiac mitochondrial function and contractile performance.
- Rasayana (comprehensive rejuvenation): used in classical practice for rejuvenation. A review has reported antioxidant, [immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/), and anti-aging activity.
- DNA repair and cellular protection: a clinical study reported maintained DNA strand break repair in aged individuals.

## Recent safety updates

- A clinical study of 720 participants (686 completed) reported good tolerability in an elderly population
- Mild acidity or loose stools possible in Pitta-predominant individuals; take with milk to mitigate
- Honey component should not be heated; classical preparation methods ensure honey is added after cooling

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Rasayana/Avaleha (semi-solid), Churna (powder), Capsules (modern), Tablets (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g twice daily or 10-20 g as Avaleha.

**Optimal Timing:** Early morning on empty stomach and/or at bedtime

**Storage:** Store in airtight amber glass or HDPE containers in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Temperature not exceeding 25°C.

**Marker Compounds:** Ascorbic acid, Gallic acid, Ellagic acid, Emblicanin A, Emblicanin B

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm milk or honey
