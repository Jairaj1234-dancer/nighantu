## Key Ingredients

- Amalaki (Phyllanthus emblica / Indian gooseberry) - primary ingredient
- [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) (cow ghee)
- [Madhu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/madhu/) (honey)
- Ratio: Amalaki powder : Ghee : Honey = 2:1:4

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Chikitsa Sthana (Rasayana Adhyaya) - described as Vayasthapana Rasayana; Ashtanga Hridayam - designated as foremost among all Rasayana plants

## How does it work?

- High vitamin C content enhances neutrophil function and collagen synthesis, supporting immune defense and tissue repair
- Tannin complexes slow reactive oxygen species (ROS) formation, providing potent [antioxidant](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antioxidant/) cellular protection
- Flavonoid [glycosides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#glycosides) influence cellular pathways related to inflammation, apoptosis, and cell proliferation
- Telomerase activation prevents erosion of telomeres in aged individuals, potentially slowing cellular aging
- DNA strand break repair maintained stably through enhanced nucleotide excision and base excision repair mechanisms

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Vayasthapana (anti-aging/rejuvenation): telomerase activation and telomere length maintenance validated in human clinical study
- Hridya ([cardioprotective](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/cardioprotective/)): enhanced cardiac mitochondrial function and contractile performance validated in hypertrophy model
- Rasayana (comprehensive rejuvenation): 2024 comprehensive review validates antioxidant, [immunomodulatory](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/immunomodulatory/), and anti-aging mechanisms
- DNA repair and cellular protection: clinical study confirms maintained DNA strand break repair in aged individuals

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **Influence of [Amalaki Rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/formulation/amalaki-rasayana/) on telomerase activity and telomere length in human blood mononuclear cells** (2017): 45-day administration in healthy aged volunteers (45-60 years) showed increased telomerase activity that facilitated maintenance of telomere length, potentially promoting healthy aging
- **Pharmacological insights and therapeutic potential of [Amalaki Rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/formulation/amalaki-rasayana/): A comprehensive review** (2024): Comprehensive review validates antioxidant, anti-aging, cardioprotective, [antihypertensive](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#antihypertensive), [anticancer](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/anticancer/), [antidiabetic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antidiabetic/), and immunomodulatory effects of [Amalaki Rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/formulation/amalaki-rasayana/)
- **[Amalaki rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/formulation/amalaki-rasayana/) enhances cardiac mitochondrial and contractile functions and improves cardiac function in rats with hypertrophy** (2017): [Amalaki Rasayana](/ayurveda-atlas/formulation/amalaki-rasayana/) enhanced cardiac mitochondrial function and contractile performance, improving cardiac function in rats with cardiac hypertrophy

## Recent safety updates

- Large-scale clinical study (686 of 720 participants completing study, 95.3% completion rate) confirms safety and tolerability in elderly population
- Mild acidity or loose stools possible in Pitta-predominant individuals; take with milk to mitigate
- Honey component should not be heated; classical preparation methods ensure honey is added after cooling

## What is it made of?

### Mineral/Elemental Profile

- **Primary component:** Mercury-based preparation (Parada) with herbal processing
- **Note:** Restricted use — requires strict Shodhana protocols and expert supervision

**Analytical Methods:** AAS/ICP-MS (heavy metals), XRD, Namburi phased spot test

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Rasayana/Avaleha (semi-solid), Churna (powder), Capsules (modern), Tablets (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 3-6 g twice daily or 10-20 g as Avaleha, as per AFI/API

**Bioavailability:** Tannin-ascorbic acid complex provides slow-release antioxidant activity with 6-8 hour sustained action. Bhavana processing with self-juice (swarasa bhavana) enhances particle-level impregnation of phytoconstituents.

**Optimal Timing:** Early morning on empty stomach and/or at bedtime

**Shelf Life:** 2 years from date of manufacture as per ASU guidelines

**Storage:** Store in airtight amber glass or HDPE containers in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Temperature not exceeding 25°C.

**Marker Compounds:** Ascorbic acid, Gallic acid, Ellagic acid, Emblicanin A, Emblicanin B

**Quality Parameters:** Ascorbic acid content (NLT 0.5% in churna), gallic acid by HPLC, total [tannins](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/tannins/), total ash, acid-insoluble ash, loss on drying (<10%), particle size for churna, pH for avaleha, microbial limits, heavy metals, aflatoxins

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm milk or honey
