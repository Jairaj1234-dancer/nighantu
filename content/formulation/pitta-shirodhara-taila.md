## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Shirodhara is a classical murdha-taila procedure described in **Ashtanga Hridaya** and **Sushruta Samhita**. Among its traditional indications is Pitta-vaishamya of the head region: over-active, sharp, heated mental states with burning sensation on the scalp or forehead, and difficulty letting the mind settle. This formulation follows the Pitta-shamaka taila principle — shita-virya (cooling potency), madhura/tikta rasa and medhya dravyas — compounded in a blended Tila/sunflower base to moderate the virya profile of sesame for Pitta-predominant clients.

## How does it work?

- Shirodhara produces measurable reductions in sympathetic activity and subjective stress across healthy-volunteer and anxiety-spectrum studies; the cooler stream temperature used in the Pitta variant is traditionally described as supporting thermal and autonomic quiescence.
- Bacopa monnieri bacosides and Tinospora cordifolia tinocordiside/tinosporin are associated with modulation of oxidative-stress and neuro-inflammatory pathways; in Shirodhara the contribution is via scalp contact and olfactory-limbic input, not oral dosing.
- Cooling aromatic volatiles (coriander linalool, cyperus sesquiterpenes, lavandula esters) contribute to the sensory profile of the cooling shiro-dhara stream.
- Manjistha and Yashtimadhu saponins/polyphenols contribute to classical scalp-soothing and complexion-supporting effects; traditional use is external.

## Traditional Uses (External Shirodhara Context)

- Traditionally used in Shirodhara to help soothe a heated, over-focused, sharp Pitta mind.
- Supports a feeling of coolness, composure and ease when applied externally as shiro-abhyanga or shiro-dhara.
- Traditionally used in pada-abhyanga before sleep per classical Pitta-pacifying routines.

## Directions for Use (External Only)

- **Shirodhara:** warm gently to ~36–38 °C (a little cooler than a Vata Shirodhara). Pour in a continuous unbroken stream over the ajna marma for 30–45 minutes.
- **Shiro-abhyanga:** massage a small quantity into scalp and temples — preferably in the cooler part of the day — leave 30–60 min and cleanse.
- **Pada-abhyanga:** apply a small quantity to the soles of the feet before sleep.

## Safety, contraindications and cautions

- Not to be ingested.
- Discontinue if irritation occurs. Avoid eyes and broken skin. Patch-test before first use.
- Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
- Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
