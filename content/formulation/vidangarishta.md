## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sharangadharasamhita Madhyamakhanda. Also referenced in Bhavaprakasha Nighantu (16th century CE) and Yogaratnakara for anthelmintic indications.

## How does it work?

- [Embelin](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#embelin) (from [Vidanga](/herb/vidanga/)) exerts potent anthelmintic action by paralyzing intestinal parasites (roundworms, tapeworms)
- Kutaja provides anti-dysenteric and anti-amoebic action complementing the antiparasitic activity
- [Pippali](/herb/pippali/)-mediated bioavailability enhancement increases systemic absorption of embelin and other active compounds
- [Triphala](/herb/triphala/) and [Rasna](/herb/rasna/) are traditionally regarded as [immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/)

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Intestinal parasites (Krimi Roga): traditionally used for intestinal parasites. A clinical pilot study reported a reduction in worm load.
- Abdominal bloating and flatulence: traditionally used as a digestive and carminative for abdominal bloating and flatulence.
- Skin abscesses and boils: traditionally used for skin abscesses and boils. Antibacterial profiling studies have reported antibacterial activity.
- Urinary disorders and prostate enlargement: traditionally used for urinary disorders and prostate enlargement. Its components are traditionally regarded as anti-inflammatory and [diuretic](/reference/diuretic/).

## Recent safety updates

- Contains 5-10% self-generated alcohol. Embelin has a narrow therapeutic index; adherence to recommended dosage is critical. Overdose may cause nausea and vomiting.
- Not recommended during pregnancy (embelin has anti-implantation properties). Liver function monitoring recommended during prolonged use. May interact with conventional anthelmintic drugs.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Arishta/Asava (fermented liquid), Syrup (modern adaptation)

**Standard Dosage:** 15-30ml with equal water for anthelmintic action

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily

**Storage:** Room temperature, tightly sealed, away from direct sunlight. Do not refrigerate.

**Marker Compounds:** Embelin, Vilangin

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Equal quantity of water after meals

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: [Vidanga](/herb/vidanga/) (Embelia ribes), [Dhataki](/herb/dhataki/), [Guda](/glossary/concepts-a-m/#guda); fermentation creates unique metabolite profile with enhanced therapeutic activity
