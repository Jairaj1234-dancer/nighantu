## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Bhaishajya Ratnavali, Prameha Pidika Adhyaya (chapter on diabetic complications). Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita extol [Sariva](/herb/sariva/) as a premier herb for pacifying Pitta dosha.

## How does it work?

- Blood purification (Raktashodhana) through elimination of toxins via hepatic and renal pathways
- Anti-inflammatory and cooling (Sheeta Virya) action of [Sariva](/herb/sariva/) pacifies Pitta dosha and reduces skin inflammation
- [Guduchi](/herb/guduchi/), [Tulsi](/herb/tulsi/), and Amalaki are traditionally regarded as [immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/)
- Detoxification through enhanced liver and kidney function promotes elimination of metabolic waste products

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Blood purification and detoxification: traditionally used for blood purification and detoxification. Mechanism studies have reported hepatic and renal detoxification activity.
- Skin disorders (eczema, psoriasis, acne): traditionally used for skin disorders such as eczema, psoriasis and acne. An open-label trial reported improvement in PASI scores.
- Fever (adjunct): traditionally used as an adjunct in fever. A pilot study in dengue reported faster temperature reduction.
- Urinary disorders and diabetes complications (Prameha Pidika): traditionally used for urinary disorders and diabetes complications (Prameha Pidika).

## Recent safety updates

- Contains 5-10% self-generated alcohol. Generally safe at recommended dosage.
- Not recommended during pregnancy. May interact with immunosuppressive medications. Patients with diabetes should monitor blood sugar as some ingredients may have hypoglycemic effects.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Arishta/Asava (fermented liquid), Syrup (modern adaptation)

**Standard Dosage:** 12 to 24 ml with equal water, as given in the Ayurvedic Formulary of India, Part I, entry 1:37. The formulary attributes this formula to Bhaisajyaratnavali, Pramehapidakadhikara; 22-25.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily

**Storage:** Room temperature, tightly sealed, away from direct sunlight. Do not refrigerate.

**Marker Compounds:** Hemidesmine, 2-hydroxy-4-methoxybenzaldehyde

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Equal quantity of water after meals

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: [Sariva](/herb/sariva/) (Hemidesmus), [Dhataki](/herb/dhataki/), [Guda](/glossary/concepts-a-m/#guda); fermentation creates unique metabolite profile with enhanced therapeutic activity
