## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sahasrayogam (Taila Prakarana, 12th century CE Kerala tradition). One of the most celebrated Keshya (hair care) formulations in Kerala Ayurveda. Updated API formulations use [Daruharidra](/herb/daruharidra/) stem extract instead of traditional Anjana (Lead Sulphide) for safety.

## How does it work?

- [Wedelolactone](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#wedelolactone) and eclalbatin from [Bhringraj](/herb/bhringraj/) are reported to promote hair follicle proliferation via the Wnt/beta-catenin signaling pathway
- [Indirubin](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#indirubin) from Neeli (Indigofera) is reported to have anti-fungal activity against dermatophytes
- Gallic acid and vitamin C from [Amla](/herb/amla/) have reported [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) activity
- Nardostachin from [Jatamansi](/herb/jatamansi/) is proposed to improve scalp microcirculation

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Hair fall and hair growth: traditionally used for hair fall and to promote hair growth. A clinical study reported reduced hair shedding, and a hair density study reported increased hair density compared with placebo.
- Premature greying (Palitya): traditionally used for premature greying of hair. The oil's ingredients include Neeli (Indigofera) and [Amla](/herb/amla/).
- Dandruff and scalp infections: traditionally used for dandruff and scalp infections. An in vitro study reported anti-dermatophyte activity of Neeli extract against Trichophyton strains.

## Recent safety updates

- Modern formulations per API standards use [Daruharidra](/herb/daruharidra/) (Berberis aristata) instead of traditional Anjana (Lead Sulphide), with each batch subject to testing. Patch test recommended for first-time users as natural ingredients can cause allergic reactions in some individuals.
- Each batch tested for microbes per Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API) standards. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue if scalp irritation, redness, or itching occurs.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Thailam (medicated [coconut oil](/glossary/concepts-a-m/#coconut-oil)), External application

**Standard Dosage:** Scalp application: 10-15ml

**Optimal Timing:** Before bath or at bedtime

**Storage:** Airtight amber glass/HDPE container, room temperature, protected from light

**Marker Compounds:** Eclipta [saponins](/reference/saponins/), Gallic acid, [Indigotin](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#indigotin)

**Vehicle (Anupana):** External: massage into affected area

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: Neeli (Indigofera), Bhringaraj, Amalaki; oil base acts as lipid carrier for enhanced tissue penetration
