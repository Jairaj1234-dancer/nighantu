## Key Ingredients

- Patranga (Caesalpinia sappan / Sappan wood)
- [Khadira](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/khadira/) (Acacia catechu)
- [Vasaka](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/vasaka/) (Adhatoda vasica)
- Semal flowers (Bombax ceiba)
- [Bala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/bala/) (Sida cordifolia)
- Gurhal (Hibiscus rosa-sinensis)
- [Trikatu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/trikatu/) ([Pippali](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/pippali/), Maricha, Shunthi)
- Trivrutta (Operculina turpethum)
- [Devadaru](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/devadaru/) (Cedrus deodara)
- [Dhataki](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/dhataki/) pushpa (Woodfordia fruticosa)
- Jaggery (fermentation medium)

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Brihat Trayi (the three great classics of Ayurveda). Mentioned in Charaka Samhita (1st-2nd century CE) under Asava-Arishta category. Sushruta Samhita references it for gastrointestinal and gynecological disorders.

## How does it work?

- [Hemostatic](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#hemostatic) and astringent action of Patranga and [Khadira](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/khadira/) controls heavy menstrual bleeding (menorrhagia)
- Anti-inflammatory action of [Vasaka](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/vasaka/) and [Devadaru](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/devadaru/) reduces uterine inflammation and pain
- Uterine tonic properties of [Bala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/bala/) and Gurhal strengthen reproductive tissue and improve hormonal balance
- Antacid and digestive action addresses associated digestive complaints with less rebound acidity than conventional antacids

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Heavy menstrual bleeding (Raktapradar/Menorrhagia) - validated by hemostatic and astringent mechanism studies
- Leucorrhea (Shweta Pradar) - supported by uterine tonic and [antimicrobial](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antimicrobial/) properties
- PCOS and menstrual irregularity - supported by clinical study showing improved menstrual regularity
- Endometriosis-related pain - corroborated by clinical observation of pain and inflammation reduction

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **Patrangasava in functional dyspepsia: a randomized pilot** (2019): Int J Ayurveda Med: Patrangasava vs standard antacid in functional dyspepsia - both improved, but Patrangasava users had less rebound acidity over 4 weeks.
- **Efficacy of Patrangasava in menstrual regularity in PCOS** (2020): Study found Patrangasava effective in improving menstrual regularity in women with PCOS through hormonal balancing mechanisms.
- **Patrangasava in endometriosis-related pain and inflammation** (2021): Clinical observation showed Patrangasava was effective in reducing pain and inflammation in women with endometriosis.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains 5-10% self-generated alcohol. Not recommended during pregnancy. May interact with hormonal medications and oral contraceptives.
- Generally safe at recommended dosage. Prolonged high-dose use without medical supervision not recommended. Women on anticoagulants should use cautiously due to hemostatic properties.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Arishta/Asava (fermented liquid), Syrup (modern adaptation)

**Standard Dosage:** 15-30ml with equal water for bleeding disorders

**Bioavailability:** Self-generated alcohol enhances extraction and absorption of both water-soluble and fat-soluble compounds; fermentation produces bioactive metabolites not present in raw herbs

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite per classical texts; commercially labeled 5-10 years. Improves with age.

**Storage:** Room temperature, tightly sealed, away from direct sunlight. Do not refrigerate.

**Marker Compounds:** Brazilin, Brazilein

**Quality Parameters:** Alcohol content 5-12% v/v, pH 3.0-4.5, specific gravity 1.01-1.08, total solids 15-25%, marker: Brazilin, Brazilein

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Equal quantity of water after meals

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: Patranga (Caesalpinia sappan), [Dhataki](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/dhataki/), other herbs; fermentation creates unique metabolite profile with enhanced therapeutic activity
