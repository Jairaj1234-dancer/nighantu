## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Ashtanga Hridaya (Brihat Trayi compendia, 1st century CE); Charaka Samhita; Sushruta Samhita; Kashyapa Samhita (12th century) for throat conditions

## How does it work?

- [Dadima](/nighantu/herb/dadima/) (pomegranate) provides iron, folic acid, and [polyphenols](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polyphenols) that directly support hemoglobin synthesis and erythropoiesis
- Chitraka ([plumbagin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#plumbagin)) stimulates Agni (digestive fire), enhancing iron absorption from dietary sources
- [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/) and Shunthi improve bioavailability of nutrients through enhanced hepatic and intestinal enzyme activity
- Ghee base provides fat-soluble vitamin delivery and enhances absorption of mineral supplements
- Deepana (appetizer) and Pachana (digestive) properties improve overall nutritional status and tissue nourishment

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Pandu (anemia): multicenter clinical trial validates significant improvement in serum iron and hemoglobin with 103 patients
- Garbhini Pandu (anemia in pregnancy): validated safe and effective in 35 pregnant subjects without fetal harm
- Deepana (digestive stimulation): confirmed improvement in Agni (digestive power) and nutritional status
- Arsha (hemorrhoids): traditional indication supported by anti-inflammatory and digestive properties of ingredients

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **Clinical Efficacy and Safety of Punarnavadi Mandura and Dadimadi [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/)|Dadimadi Ghrita in the Management of Iron Deficiency Anemia** (2017): 103 patients administered 10g twice daily for 12 weeks showed statistically significant increase in serum iron levels and improvement in anemia symptoms; all safety parameters within stipulated range with no adverse drug reactions
- **Effect of Dadimadi [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/)|Dadimadi Ghrita in Garbhini Pandu (Anaemia in Pregnancy)** (2019): 35 pregnant subjects treated with 10ml daily for 30 days showed improved digestive power and nourishment without side effects to fetus or mother
- **Clinical Efficacy of Dadimadi [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/)|Dadimadi Ghrita in Pandu (Anemia)** (2020): Dadimadi [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/)|Dadimadi Ghrita demonstrated significant improvement in hemoglobin levels, reduction in weakness, fatigue, dizziness, and headache in anemia patients

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee), Soft gelatin capsules (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 6-12 g twice daily, as per AFI Part I

**Bioavailability:** Ghee matrix enhances absorption of pomegranate polyphenols ([punicalagin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#punicalagin), ellagic acid). Lipid base protects [ellagitannins](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-d-g/#ellagitannins) from gastric degradation, enabling colonic conversion to urolithins (bioactive metabolites).

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals or as directed, for Atisara (diarrhea) and Grahani conditions

**Shelf Life:** 2 years from date of manufacture as per ASU guidelines for [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/) preparations

**Storage:** Store in airtight glass or tin containers in a cool, dry place. Protect from moisture and light. Temperature not exceeding 25°C.

**Marker Compounds:** Punicalagin, [Punicalin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-o-q/#punicalin), Ellagic acid, Gallic acid, Punicic acid

**Quality Parameters:** Acid value (<6), peroxide value (<10), saponification value, refractive index, moisture (<0.5%), rancidity test negative, punicalagin content from [Dadima](/nighantu/herb/dadima/) by HPLC, total polyphenols, total [tannins](/nighantu/reference/tannins/), microbial limits, heavy metals

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water or buttermilk
