## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Ashtanga Hridaya (Brihat Trayi compendia, 1st century CE); Charaka Samhita; Sushruta Samhita; Kashyapa Samhita (12th century) for throat conditions

## How does it work?

- [Dadima](/herb/dadima/) (pomegranate) provides iron, folic acid, and [polyphenols](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#polyphenols), and is traditionally regarded as supporting blood formation
- Chitraka ([plumbagin](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#plumbagin)) stimulates Agni (digestive fire), enhancing iron absorption from dietary sources
- [Pippali](/herb/pippali/) and Shunthi improve bioavailability of nutrients through enhanced hepatic and intestinal enzyme activity
- Ghee base provides fat-soluble vitamin delivery and enhances absorption of mineral supplements
- Deepana (appetizer) and Pachana (digestive) properties improve overall nutritional status and tissue nourishment

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Pandu (anemia): traditionally used for anemia. A clinical study reported improvement in serum iron and hemoglobin.
- Garbhini Pandu (anemia in pregnancy): traditionally used for anemia in pregnancy. A clinical study in pregnant subjects reported no fetal harm.
- Deepana (digestive stimulation): traditionally used to stimulate digestion and Agni (digestive power).
- Arsha (hemorrhoids): traditionally used for hemorrhoids.

## Recent safety updates

- A multicenter clinical study reported that, in 103 patients over a 12-week period, all safety parameters stayed within normal range and no adverse drug reactions or events occurred
- A clinical study in pregnant subjects with anemia reported no fetal or maternal side effects at 10ml daily for 30 days
- Standardized as per Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India; procured from GMP-certified pharmaceutical industries

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** [Ghrita](/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee), Soft gelatin capsules (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 6-12 g twice daily. The Ayurvedic Formulary of India attributes this formula to Astangahrdaya, Cikitsasthana, Adhyaya 16; 2-2½.

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals or as directed

**Storage:** Store in airtight glass or tin containers in a cool, dry place. Protect from moisture and light. Temperature not exceeding 25°C.

**Marker Compounds:** Punicalagin, [Punicalin](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#punicalin), Ellagic acid, Gallic acid, Punicic acid

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water or buttermilk
