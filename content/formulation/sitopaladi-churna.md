## Key Ingredients

- Sitopala (Mishri / Rock candy sugar)
- Vanshalochana (Bambusa arundinacea) - Bamboo manna
- [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/) (Piper longum) - Long pepper
- Ela (Elettaria cardamomum) - Cardamom
- Tvak (Cinnamomum zeylanicum) - Cinnamon

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sharangdhara Samhita, Madhyama Khanda, Chapter 6. Also referenced in Chakradatta and Bhaishajya Ratnavali (Kasa Rogadhikara / Cough chapter). One of the foundational respiratory formulations in Ayurveda.

## How does it work?

- Mast cell stabilization: Inhibits mast cell degranulation (65-74% protection), preventing [histamine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-g-l/#histamine) release and allergic responses in respiratory tissue.
- [Bronchodilator](/nighantu/reference/bronchodilator/) action: [Piperine](/nighantu/reference/piperine/) and essential oils relax bronchial smooth muscle, improving airway patency.
- Anti-inflammatory cytokine suppression: 45% inhibition of TNF-alpha in lung cell lines, reducing respiratory tract inflammation.
- Bioavailability synergy: Piperine from [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/) enhances absorption of [cinnamaldehyde](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-c-d/#cinnamaldehyde) (from Tvak) by 30%, demonstrating classical formulation synergy.
- [Antimicrobial](/nighantu/reference/antimicrobial/) activity: Active against S. aureus and S. pneumoniae, providing direct action against respiratory pathogens.

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Cough (Kasa) management (validated by RCT showing significant cough frequency reduction vs. placebo)
- Allergic respiratory conditions (validated by mast cell stabilization comparable to ketotifen)
- Respiratory tract infections (validated by antimicrobial activity against common respiratory pathogens)
- Bronchial asthma and bronchospasm (validated by bronchodilator activity studies)

## What do recent clinical trials show?

PMID [24174826](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24174826/) · [doi:10.4103/0974-8490.118824](https://doi.org/10.4103/0974-8490.118824)  
  Aqueous extract (300 mg/kg) and methanolic extract (150-300 mg/kg) showed 65-74% mast cell degranulation protection, comparable to standard drug ketotifen (79%), with statistical significance (P < 0.0001).

## Recent safety updates

- Generally one of the safest Ayurvedic medicines with no significant side effects at recommended doses. Likely safe for pregnant and lactating women under physician supervision.
- Contains substantial sugar (Mishri/rock candy); diabetics and those with insulin resistance should exercise caution or seek sugar-free alternatives.
- Hot potency (Ushna Virya) may cause issues in summer or when digestion is already strong; recommended to take one month gap after every 2-3 months of usage. May interact with anticoagulants, antiplatelets, and diabetes medications.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Churna (fine powder), Capsule, Tablet

**Standard Dosage:** 1-3g with honey 3-4 times daily

**Bioavailability:** Powder form provides faster disintegration than tablets; particle size reduction enhances surface area for absorption

**Optimal Timing:** Multiple times daily for cough

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (as per ASU guidelines for churna)

**Storage:** Airtight container, cool dry place, protected from moisture

**Marker Compounds:** Piperine, [Cineole](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-c-d/#cineole)

**Quality Parameters:** Mesh size NLT 85#, moisture NMT 6%, total ash per pharmacopoeia, assay of marker compounds: Piperine, Cineole

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Honey

### Synergistic Combinations

- With Honey as vehicle for enhanced absorption
