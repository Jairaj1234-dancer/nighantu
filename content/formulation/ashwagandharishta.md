## Where is it described in the classical texts?

The Ayurvedic Formulary of India attributes it to Bhaisajyaratnavali (Murccha chapter). Described as a [Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/) (rejuvenator) specifically for improving Virya (vital strength) and fertility.

## How does it work?

- [Adaptogenic](/reference/adaptogenic/) action through modulation of HPA axis and cortisol reduction ([Ashwagandha](/herb/ashwagandha/) [withanolides](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#withanolides))
- [Immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/) action; traditionally used during convalescence
- Rejuvenative (Rasayana) action through combined [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/), anti-inflammatory, and anabolic properties

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Stress and anxiety: traditionally used for stress and anxiety. A placebo-controlled clinical study reported lower cortisol levels.
- Chronic fatigue and debility: traditionally used for chronic fatigue and debility. A randomized clinical study reported improvement in fatigue severity.
- Convalescence after febrile illness: traditionally used during convalescence after febrile illness.
- Vitality and reproductive health: traditionally used as a Rasayana for vitality and reproductive health.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains 5-10% self-generated alcohol from fermentation. Contraindicated in pregnancy and during lactation. [Ashwagandha](/herb/ashwagandha/) may alter thyroid hormone levels; monitor in thyroid patients.
- May potentiate sedative and [anxiolytic](/reference/anxiolytic/) medications. Overdosage can cause gastric irritation, nausea, or diarrhea. Use under physician supervision in autoimmune conditions.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Arishta (fermented decoction), Capsules (modern), Syrup (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 15-30 mL twice daily after meals with equal water. The Ayurvedic Formulary of India attributes this formula to Bhaisajyaratnavali, Mirccha ra; 13-17.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily, preferably after lunch and dinner. Best results with long-term use (3-6 months).

**Storage:** Store in well-closed amber glass or HDPE containers in a cool, dry place. Protect from direct sunlight. Temperature not exceeding 30°C.

**Marker Compounds:** Withanolide A, Withaferin A, [Withanone](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#withanone), Withanoside IV, 12-Deoxywithastramonolide

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Equal quantity of warm water
