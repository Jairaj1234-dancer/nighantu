## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Classical Ayurvedic texts under Sandhana Kalpana (fermented preparations). Referenced in Sharangadhara Samhita and Bhaishajya Ratnavali for digestive and respiratory disorders.

## How does it work?

- Bioavailability enhancement by [piperine](/reference/piperine/) increases absorption of co-administered therapeutics by 30-200%
- Agni (digestive fire) stimulation through [Trikatu](/herb/trikatu/) and Chitraka improves digestive and absorptive capacity
- [Pippali](/herb/pippali/), to which [expectorant](/reference/expectorant/) action is attributed, is traditionally used for respiratory conditions including cough and asthma
- Sharangadhara Samhita and Bhaishajya Ratnavali describe the formulation for digestive and respiratory disorders

## What is it traditionally used for?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Indigestion and poor appetite (Agnimandya): traditionally used for indigestion and poor appetite.
- Cough and asthma (Kasa, Shwasa): traditionally used for cough and asthma.
- Anemia: traditionally used for anemia.
- Gastric disorders and malabsorption: traditionally used for gastric disorders and malabsorption. Clinical observations have reported improved Dhatwagni.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains 5-10% self-generated alcohol. Pungent herbs may aggravate hyperacidity and peptic ulcer in Pitta-predominant individuals.
- May potentiate effects of co-administered drugs due to piperine's bioavailability-enhancing properties. Not recommended during pregnancy or in active gastric bleeding.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Arishta/Asava (fermented liquid), Syrup (modern adaptation)

**Standard Dosage:** 12 to 24 ml with equal water, as given in the Ayurvedic Formulary of India, Part I, entry 1:22. The formulary attributes this formula to Sarngadharasamhita, Madhyamakhanda, Adhyaya 10; 28-32.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily

**Storage:** Room temperature, tightly sealed, away from direct sunlight. Do not refrigerate.

**Marker Compounds:** Piperine, [Piperlongumine](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#piperlongumine)

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Equal quantity of water after meals

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: [Pippali](/herb/pippali/), [Chavya](/herb/chavya/), Chitraka, [Dhataki](/herb/dhataki/); fermentation creates unique metabolite profile with enhanced therapeutic activity
