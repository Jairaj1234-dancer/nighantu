## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Earliest reference in Ashtanga Hridaya commentaries. Hints of formula in Vangasena Samhita (circa 11th century CE). Documented extensively in Kerala Ayurvedic tradition for Vata disorders of the musculoskeletal system.

## How does it work?

- Anti-inflammatory activity through inhibition of pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-6) by [Rasna](/herb/rasna/) and [Guduchi](/herb/guduchi/)
- Ama (toxin) digestion by [Rasna](/herb/rasna/)'s digestive action helps resolve metabolic waste contributing to joint inflammation
- Vata-Kapha balancing through the synergistic seven-herb combination reduces joint stiffness and swelling
- [Analgesic](/reference/analgesic/) action from [Devadaru](/herb/devadaru/) and [Eranda](/herb/eranda/) provides direct pain relief in musculoskeletal conditions

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Joint pain and rheumatoid arthritis: traditionally used for joint pain and rheumatoid arthritis. A clinical study reported improvement in pain scores.
- Chronic low back pain: traditionally used for chronic low back pain.
- Fever associated with body aches: traditionally used for fever associated with body aches. [Guduchi](/herb/guduchi/) is described as having anti-inflammatory and [antipyretic](/reference/antipyretic/) properties.
- Sciatica and neuralgia: traditionally used for sciatica and neuralgia. [Devadaru](/herb/devadaru/) and [Eranda](/herb/eranda/) are described as having analgesic properties.

## Recent safety updates

- No significant adverse effects reported at standard dosage. Research gaps include long-term safety profiling and standardized extract optimization.
- Comparative trials against standard NSAIDs for specific arthritis types are still needed. Should be taken under physician supervision for chronic conditions.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Kashayam (decoction), Kashayam tablet, Concentrated liquid

**Standard Dosage:** 15-30ml twice daily. The Ayurvedic Formulary of India, Part II, entry 4:20 specifies 10 to 50 g of the kvatha curna to prepare the decoction, which is a quantity of drug rather than a dose taken, so it is not comparable with the volume above. The formulary attributes this formula to Bhaisajyaratnavali, Amavatadhikara; 9.

**Bioavailability:** Aqueous decoction provides good bioavailability for water-soluble compounds; concentration step may reduce volatile components

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals, typically morning and evening

**Shelf Life:** 1 day (fresh kashayam); 2 years (kashayam tablet); 6 months (concentrated liquid with preservative)

**Storage:** Fresh: refrigerate, use within 24h. Tablet/liquid: cool dry place, airtight

**Marker Compounds:** Isorasnol

**Quality Parameters:** pH 4.0-6.5, specific gravity 1.01-1.05, total solids 2-8%, marker assay: Isorasnol

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm, usually taken on empty stomach
