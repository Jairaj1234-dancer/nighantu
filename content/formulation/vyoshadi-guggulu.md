## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sharangdhara Samhita (Madhyama Khanda, Chapter 7); also referenced in Bhaishajya Ratnavali (Kasa/Pratishyaya Chikitsa) and Chakradatta; described under the [guggulu](/formulation/guggulu/) formulation group for Kapha-Vata disorders

## How does it work?

- Thyroid stimulation: Guggulsterones from [Guggulu](/formulation/guggulu/) are reported to have thyroid stimulating activity
- Anti-inflammatory cytokine inhibition: Guggulsterones inhibit pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-6) while [Trikatu](/herb/trikatu/) promotes GI absorption of these active compounds
- Respiratory decongestant: Hot potency of [Trikatu](/herb/trikatu/) and [Guggulu](/formulation/guggulu/) are classically regarded as reducing Kapha stagnation in the lungs
- Mucosal tonicity: Terminalia fruit [tannins](/reference/tannins/) (from [Triphala](/herb/triphala/)) are traditionally regarded as toning mucosal tissue in the respiratory tract
- Metabolic and lipid regulation: Guggulsterones have been reported in laboratory studies to lower cholesterol through FXR antagonism and to stimulate phagocytosis

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Galaganda (thyroid swelling): traditionally used for thyroid conditions. A randomized controlled trial reported a reduction in TSH levels, with no adverse effects reported.
- Pratishyaya (respiratory congestion and common cold): traditionally used for congestion and colds. A clinical study reported improvement in cough frequency.
- Obesity: used in classical practice for excess weight. A prospective multicenter study reported on the safety and outcomes of Vyoshadi Guggulu combined with [Haritaki](/herb/haritaki/) Churna.
- Voice hoarseness and throat disorders: traditionally used for hoarseness and throat complaints.

## Recent safety updates

- Studies summarized in the source notes reported no adverse effects, with LFT and RFT within normal limits and no adverse drug reactions in the treatment groups
- Standard guggulu precautions apply: contraindicated in pregnancy and lactation; may interact with thyroid medications (synergistic effect could cause hyperthyroidism); avoid in bleeding disorders as guggul may slow clotting; discontinue 2 weeks before surgery

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Vati (pill/tablet), Capsule

**Standard Dosage:** 2 tablets twice daily

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily

**Storage:** Airtight container, cool dry place; guggulu preparations are hygroscopic

**Marker Compounds:** Guggulsterone, [Piperine](/reference/piperine/), [Gingerol](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#gingerol)

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: [Trikatu](/herb/trikatu/) (Vyosha), Guggulu, [Triphala](/herb/triphala/); guggulu potentiates anti-inflammatory and [hypolipidemic](/glossary/pharmacology/#hypolipidemic) actions
