## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita (Vimana Sthana, Chapter 7 - Krimighna Gana/Dashemani); also referenced in Sushruta Samhita, Ashtanga Hridayam (Krimighna Gana group of 10 antiparasitic herbs), and Bhaishajya Ratnavali

## How does it work?

- Parasitic membrane disruption: [Vidanga](/nighantu/herb/vidanga/)'s [embelin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-d-g/#embelin) [alkaloids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) impair parasitic cell membranes by interfering with mitochondrial electron transport chain, causing parasite death
- Growth inhibition: [Neem](/nighantu/herb/neem/)'s [azadirachtin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-a-c/#azadirachtin) disrupts parasitic growth by interfering with ecdysone-mediated molting cycle, preventing larval development and reproduction
- Anti-inflammatory gut healing: [Haridra](/nighantu/herb/haridra/) ([curcumin](/nighantu/herb/curcumin/)) provides anti-inflammatory and [antimicrobial](/nighantu/reference/antimicrobial/) effects in damaged gut mucosa, promoting healing of parasite-induced tissue damage
- Digestive fire enhancement: [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/) and [Vacha](/nighantu/herb/vacha/) enhance Agni (digestive fire), creating an inhospitable environment for parasites and preventing reinfection by improving gut immunity
- Ama mobilization: [Haritaki](/nighantu/herb/haritaki/) gently mobilizes Ama (toxic residue) from the gut while [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/) amplifies absorption through enhanced Srota (channel) penetration

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Anthelmintic activity validated through case study confirming efficacy against Hymenolepis nana tapeworm infection
- Broad-spectrum antiparasitic action validated through demonstrated membrane-disrupting and growth-inhibiting mechanisms of [Vidanga](/nighantu/herb/vidanga/) and [Neem](/nighantu/herb/neem/)
- Intestinal health restoration validated through anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties of [curcumin](/nighantu/herb/curcumin/) ([Haridra](/nighantu/herb/haridra/)) in gut mucosa healing
- Skin parasite management (Kushtha/Krimi) validated through traditional use in parasitic skin conditions with confirmed antimicrobial activity of [Neem](/nighantu/herb/neem/) and [Haridra](/nighantu/herb/haridra/)

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **Krimighna Dashemani - An Ayurvedic Anthelmintic Compound for Hymenolepis nana: A Case Study** (2024): Case study demonstrated efficacy of Krimighna Dashemani formulation against Hymenolepis nana (dwarf tapeworm) infection, confirming traditional anthelmintic claims.
- **Antiparasitic mechanisms of [Vidanga](/nighantu/herb/vidanga/) (Embelia ribes) embelin alkaloids** (2020): Embelin alkaloids from [Vidanga](/nighantu/herb/vidanga/) impair parasitic cell membranes through interference with mitochondrial electron transport chain, showing broad-spectrum anthelmintic activity.
- **[Neem](/nighantu/herb/neem/) (Azadirachta indica) azadirachtin and [nimbin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#nimbin): antiparasitic and antimicrobial evaluation** (2019): Azadirachtin disrupts parasitic growth by interfering with ecdysone-mediated molting; nimbin provides additional antimicrobial action against gut pathogens.

## Recent safety updates

- Generally safe at recommended doses for short-term antiparasitic courses (7-14 days); may cause mild GI discomfort or loose stools during parasite expulsion phase; avoid in pregnancy and lactation
- [Kampillaka](/nighantu/herb/kampillaka/) is a potent purgative - dose must be carefully controlled; not recommended for children under 5 years without physician guidance; should be followed by probiotic restoration after treatment course; confirm parasitic infection through stool examination before use

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Vati (tablet/pill), Modern compressed tablet, Capsule (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 250-500 mg twice or thrice daily with warm water, as per AFI Part I

**Bioavailability:** Embelin (from [Vidanga](/nighantu/herb/vidanga/)) is well-absorbed orally with anthelmintic activity at therapeutic doses. Rottlerin from [Kampillaka](/nighantu/herb/kampillaka/) acts as contact anthelmintic in the GI tract — does not need systemic absorption for action. Combined oral and luminal activity provides comprehensive anthelmintic effect.

**Optimal Timing:** On empty stomach, morning (Abhukta kala), for 3-7 days course. Follow with mild purgative ([Eranda](/nighantu/herb/eranda/) Taila or [Triphala](/nighantu/herb/triphala/)) to expel worms.

**Shelf Life:** 3 years from date of manufacture as per ASU guidelines for Vati

**Storage:** Store in airtight containers in a cool, dry place. Temperature not exceeding 30°C.

**Marker Compounds:** Embelin, Rottlerin (from [Kampillaka](/nighantu/herb/kampillaka/)), Butin (from [Palasha](/nighantu/herb/palasha/)), [Butrin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-a-c/#butrin), Palasonin

**Quality Parameters:** Weight variation (±5%), hardness, friability (<1%), disintegration time (<30 min), moisture (<6%), embelin content by HPLC from [Vidanga](/nighantu/herb/vidanga/), rottlerin from Kampillaka, total [tannins](/nighantu/reference/tannins/), HPTLC fingerprint, microbial limits, heavy metals

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water or buttermilk. Some texts recommend with jaggery for pediatric use.
