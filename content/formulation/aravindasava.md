## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sahasrayogam and Bhaishajya Ratnavali, categorized under Raktaprasadana [Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/) (blood-enhancing tonics). Traditionally used in Kerala and North Indian Ayurvedic pediatric practice.

## How does it work?

- Agni (digestive fire) kindling through Ushna Virya and Katu Vipaka is traditionally held to support appetite and nutrient absorption in children
- [Immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/) action through [alkaloids](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) and [flavonoids](/reference/flavonoids/) is traditionally described as supporting immunity in children
- Raktaprasadana (blood-enriching) activity of [Manjishtha](/herb/manjishtha/) and [Triphala](/herb/triphala/) supports healthy hematopoiesis
- Medhya (intellect-promoting) action of [Vacha](/herb/vacha/) and [Jatamansi](/herb/jatamansi/) supports cognitive and mental development

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Pediatric digestive tonic: traditionally used as a digestive tonic for children. Observational studies have reported improved appetite and weight gain.
- Child growth and development: traditionally used to promote growth and development in children.
- Childhood immunity: traditionally used to strengthen immunity in children. An observational study reported improved IgG and IgA levels.
- Childhood anemia and malnutrition: traditionally used for anemia and malnutrition in children, based on its classical Raktaprasadana (blood-enriching) property.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains self-generated alcohol. NOT suitable for children under 5 years. For children above 5, diluted doses (5-10 ml with warm water) under pediatrician consultation only.
- Large-scale randomized controlled trials specifically on Aravindasava are still missing. Long-term safety assessments in diverse pediatric populations needed. Alcohol content requires careful monitoring.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Asava (fermented infusion), Syrup (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 5-10 mL (pediatric) to 15-25 mL (adult) twice daily after meals with equal water. The Ayurvedic Formulary of India attributes this formula to Bhaisajyaratnavali, Balarogadhikara; 185-188.

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily. For infants: as directed by physician

**Storage:** Store in well-closed amber glass or HDPE containers in a cool, dry place. Protect from sunlight. Temperature not exceeding 30°C.

**Marker Compounds:** [Nuciferine](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#nuciferine), [Neferine](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#neferine), [Liensinine](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#liensinine), Armepavine

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Equal quantity of water (boiled and cooled for pediatric use)
