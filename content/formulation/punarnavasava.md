## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita and Bhaishajya Ratnavali (circa 400-200 BCE origin).

## How does it work?

- [Diuretic](/reference/diuretic/) action of [Punarnava](/herb/punarnava/) and [Gokshura](/herb/gokshura/) is traditionally described for edema and ascites
- [Hepatoprotective](/reference/hepatoprotective/) activity through boeravinones (from [Punarnava](/herb/punarnava/)) and chebulinic acid; studies with boeravinones have reported hepatocyte regeneration
- Nephroprotective action supports kidney function
- Anti-inflammatory synergy from Musta, Amalaki, and [Vidanga](/herb/vidanga/) provides hepatoprotective and immune support

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Edema and dropsy (Shotha): traditionally used for these conditions. A randomized controlled trial in CKD patients reported a reduction in ankle circumference.
- Kidney support: traditionally used to support the kidneys. Studies have reported diuretic and nephroprotective activity.
- Liver disorders and hepatomegaly: traditionally used for these conditions. Studies with boeravinones have reported hepatocyte regeneration.
- Anemia and spleen disorders: traditionally used for these conditions.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains self-generated alcohol. Generally safe at standard dosage. May cause excessive urination at higher doses; maintain adequate hydration.
- Patients on diuretic or [antihypertensive](/glossary/pharmacology/#antihypertensive) medications should use with medical supervision due to additive diuretic effects. Not recommended in severe hepatic or renal failure without physician guidance.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Arishta/Asava (fermented liquid), Syrup (modern adaptation)

**Standard Dosage:** 15-30ml with equal water

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily

**Storage:** Room temperature, tightly sealed, away from direct sunlight. Do not refrigerate.

**Marker Compounds:** Punarnavine, Boeravinone B

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Equal quantity of water after meals

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: [Punarnava](/herb/punarnava/) (Boerhavia diffusa), [Dhataki](/herb/dhataki/), [Guda](/glossary/concepts-a-m/#guda); fermentation creates a unique metabolite profile
