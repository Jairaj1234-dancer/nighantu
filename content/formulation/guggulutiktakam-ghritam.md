## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Ashtanga Hridayam (written circa 750 CE by Vagbhata); also known as Pancatikta [Guggulu](/nighantu/formulation/guggulu/) [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/) or Nimbadi [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/) in various classical texts

## How does it work?

- Guggulsterones from [Guggulu](/nighantu/formulation/guggulu/) provide potent anti-inflammatory action by modulating NF-kB and COX-2 pathways in bone and joint tissues
- Panchatikta (five bitter herbs) synergistically reduce Pitta-mediated inflammation in skin and connective tissue
- [Guduchi](/nighantu/herb/guduchi/) ([berberine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-a-c/#berberine) [alkaloids](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids)) provides [immunomodulatory](/nighantu/reference/immunomodulatory/) action, restoring balanced immune function in autoimmune joint conditions
- Lipid-based ghee medium enhances bioavailability of guggulsterones and other lipophilic active compounds
- Anti-hyperlipidemic action addresses metabolic syndrome components often associated with chronic bone and skin diseases

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Asthigata Vata (bone diseases/osteoporosis): clinical studies validate efficacy in osteoarthritis with reduced pain and improved mobility
- Bhagandara (fistula-in-ano): traditional use supported; [Guggulu](/nighantu/formulation/guggulu/)-based [Ksharasutra](/nighantu/glossary/concepts-a-m/#ksharasutra) is an established Ayurvedic surgical procedure for fistula
- Kushta (chronic skin diseases): anti-inflammatory and anti-microbial actions of ingredients validated pharmacologically
- Vatarakta (gout): anti-hyperuricemic and anti-inflammatory effects of [Guggulu](/nighantu/formulation/guggulu/) confirmed in experimental studies

## Recent safety updates

- Clinical studies show effective outcomes without noxious effects at recommended doses; considered safe for long-term use under supervision
- Caution in patients with thyroid disorders as [Guggulu](/nighantu/formulation/guggulu/) may affect thyroid function; monitor thyroid levels during prolonged use
- Ghee base requires monitoring in patients with hyperlipidemia and cardiovascular conditions

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee), Soft gelatin capsules (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 6-12 g (1-2 teaspoons) twice daily on empty stomach, as per Ashtanga Hridayam/AFI

**Bioavailability:** Ghee matrix provides superior absorption of both lipophilic guggulsterones and hydrophilic bitter principles. [Guggulu](/nighantu/formulation/guggulu/) acts as yogavahi further enhancing bioavailability of co-ingredients. Tikta rasa (bitter taste) stimulates hepatobiliary function, improving systemic absorption. Excellent vehicle for bone and joint conditions.

**Optimal Timing:** On empty stomach, morning and evening. Best for Vatarakta (gouty arthritis), Kushtha (skin diseases), and Asthi-Sandhi Vikara (bone/joint disorders).

**Shelf Life:** 2 years from date of manufacture as per ASU guidelines for [Ghrita](/nighantu/herb/ghrita/)

**Storage:** Store in airtight glass or tin containers in a cool, dry place. Temperature not exceeding 25°C. Protect from light and moisture.

**Marker Compounds:** Z-Guggulsterone, E-Guggulsterone, [Azadirachtin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-a-c/#azadirachtin), [Nimbin](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-l-o/#nimbin) (from [Nimba](/nighantu/herb/nimba/)), [Berberine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-a-c/#berberine) (from [Guduchi](/nighantu/herb/guduchi/)), Gallic acid, [Piperine](/nighantu/reference/piperine/)

**Quality Parameters:** Acid value (<6), peroxide value (<10), saponification value, refractive index, moisture (<0.5%), guggulsterone content (Z+E) by HPLC, azadirachtin from [Nimba](/nighantu/herb/nimba/), [berberine](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-a-c/#berberine) from [Guduchi](/nighantu/herb/guduchi/), rancidity test negative, HPTLC fingerprint, microbial limits, heavy metals

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water or warm milk
