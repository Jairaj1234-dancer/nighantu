## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Ashtanga Hridayam Chikitsasthana 3/9 (main reference); Vidaryadi Gana described in Ashtanga Hridaya Sutrasthana 15/9-10

## How does it work?

- [Vidari](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/vidari/) ([isoflavones](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#isoflavones)) provides anabolic and tissue-building action, supporting recovery from debility and emaciation
- Dashamula group reduces systemic inflammation and soothes respiratory mucosa, addressing chronic bronchitis and asthma
- [Shatavari](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/shatavari/) and [Jivanti](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/jivanti/) provide [adaptogenic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/adaptogenic/) and Ojas-building properties, enhancing vital energy and stamina
- Ghee and milk base enhances bioavailability of fat-soluble nutrients and provides direct caloric nourishment in wasting conditions
- Vata-Pitta shamana (pacification) addresses root cause of respiratory and debility conditions in Ayurvedic pathogenesis

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Rajayakshma (phthisis/pulmonary tuberculosis): classical primary indication supported by respiratory-protective and nutritive actions of ingredients
- Kshaya (debility/emaciation): anabolic and tissue-building properties validated pharmacologically for wasting diseases
- Shwasa-Kasa (asthma and chronic cough): anti-inflammatory and mucosal soothing actions support respiratory use
- Badhirya (hearing loss): recent case series validates broader therapeutic potential including auditory health

## Recent safety updates

- No adverse effects reported in medical literature at recommended doses; considered safe for long-term use under practitioner supervision
- Very high doses may cause diarrhea and indigestion; ghee content requires caution in hyperlipidemic patients
- Referenced in Pharmacopoeia Commission for Indian Medicine & Homoeopathy publications for quality standardization

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee), Capsule (for convenience)

**Standard Dosage:** 12-24ml with milk for strength/nutrition

**Bioavailability:** Ghee is an excellent lipid carrier crossing blood-brain barrier; enhances absorption of fat-soluble phytochemicals by 3-5x compared to aqueous preparations

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals on empty stomach for best absorption

**Shelf Life:** 2 years (as per ASU guidelines for [ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) preparations); improves with aging (Purana Ghrita concept)

**Storage:** Airtight glass/stainless steel container, cool dry place, protected from light and moisture

**Marker Compounds:** [Puerarin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#puerarin), Shatavarin, [Withanolides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-t-z/#withanolides)

**Quality Parameters:** Acid value <4, peroxide value <5 meq/kg, moisture <0.5%, rancidity test negative, marker: Puerarin, Shatavarin, Withanolides

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water or warm milk

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: [Vidari](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/vidari/) (Pueraria tuberosa), [Shatavari](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/shatavari/), [Ashwagandha](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ashwagandha/); ghee enhances tissue penetration (yogavahi property)
