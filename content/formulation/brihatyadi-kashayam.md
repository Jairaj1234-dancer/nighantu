## Key Ingredients

- Shalaparni (Desmodium gangeticum)
- [Gokshura](/herb/gokshura/) (Tribulus terrestris)

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Ashtangahrudayam Chikitsasthana, Mutraghata Adhyaya 11/35. Also referenced in Charaka Samhita for respiratory and urinary system disorders.

## How does it work?

- [Diuretic](/reference/diuretic/) action of [Gokshura](/herb/gokshura/) is reported to increase urine flow; the formulation is traditionally used for urinary calculi
- Anti-inflammatory properties have been reported for [Brihati](/herb/brihati/) and [Kantakari](/herb/kantakari/)
- Coolant and diuretic properties of [Prishniparni](/herb/prishniparni/) support kidney function and reduce burning micturition
- [Antimicrobial](/reference/antimicrobial/) activity has been reported for the combined formulation

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Urinary disorders (dysuria, retention): traditionally used for these conditions. Studies have reported diuretic action of [Gokshura](/herb/gokshura/).
- Urinary calculi (stones): traditionally used for urinary stones. Experimental studies have reported lithotriptic effects.
- Urinary tract infections: traditionally used for these conditions. Studies have reported antimicrobial activity of the combined formulation.
- Respiratory conditions (adjunct use): traditionally used as an adjunct for respiratory conditions. Anti-tussive and [expectorant](/reference/expectorant/) properties have been reported for [Brihati](/herb/brihati/) and [Kantakari](/herb/kantakari/).

## Recent safety updates

- No significant adverse effects reported at standard dosage. Detailed pharmacokinetic studies on the specific decoction blend remain limited.
- May interact with lithium and diuretic medications due to inherent diuretic properties. Patients with renal impairment should use under medical supervision.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Kashayam (decoction), Kashayam tablet (modern), Concentrated liquid (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 15-30 mL (liquid) or 2 tablets twice daily before meals.

**Bioavailability:** Hot decoction form provides rapid absorption of steroidal [alkaloids](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) ([solasodine](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#solasodine)) and [saponins](/reference/saponins/). Oral bioavailability of solasodine is moderate, enhanced by hot aqueous medium.

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals (Bhojana-purva), morning and evening

**Shelf Life:** Same day (fresh), 6 months (preserved liquid), 3 years (tablet)

**Storage:** Fresh kashayam: use same day. Concentrates: amber glass bottles at room temperature. Tablets: airtight containers at room temperature.

**Marker Compounds:** Solasodine, [Solamargine](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#solamargine), [Solasonine](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#solasonine), [Diosgenin](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#diosgenin), [Carpesterol](/glossary/compounds-a-c/#carpesterol)

**Quality Parameters:** pH, specific gravity, total solids, extractive values, solasodine content by HPLC, total alkaloids, HPTLC fingerprint, microbial limits, heavy metals

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water (for tablets); as is (for liquid kashayam)
