## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Ashtanga Hridayam (Sharirasthana), named after Lord Dhanwantari. Also referenced in Yogaratnakara. Described as a premier postpartum tonic and [Vata-balancing](/glossary/concepts-m-y/#vata-balancing) formulation with 42 herbs.

## How does it work?

- COX-2 and 5-LOX inhibition by [Dashamoola](/herb/dashamoola/) has been reported, which may relate to anti-inflammatory activity
- [Bala](/herb/bala/) (Sida cordifolia) is traditionally regarded as a nervine tonic that balances Vata dosha
- Traditionally described as a uterine tonic that supports postpartum recovery
- [Neuroprotective](/reference/neuroprotective/) effects have been proposed but not established

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Postpartum care and uterine tonic: traditionally used after delivery as a uterine tonic. Observational studies have reported improved uterine involution.
- Vata disorders including joint pain and neuralgia: traditionally used for these conditions. Studies have reported COX-2/5-LOX inhibition.
- General weakness and debility after delivery: traditionally used for weakness after childbirth. [Bala](/herb/bala/) and [Ashwagandha](/herb/ashwagandha/) are traditionally regarded as nervine tonics.
- Gynecological disorders: traditionally used for gynecological disorders.

## Recent safety updates

- Generally considered safe under physician supervision.
- It should be avoided during pregnancy without physician guidance due to potent Vata-balancing action. Overdose may cause gastric discomfort.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Kashayam (decoction), Kashayam tablet (modern), Concentrated liquid (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 15-30 mL or 2 tablets twice daily before meals.

**Optimal Timing:** Before meals, morning and evening, especially for Vata-predominant conditions

**Storage:** Fresh: same day. Concentrates: amber glass at room temperature. Tablets: airtight containers at room temperature.

**Marker Compounds:** [Ephedrine](/glossary/compounds-d-g/#ephedrine) (from [Bala](/herb/bala/)), Withanolide A, [Lupeol](/reference/lupeol/), [Marmelosin](/glossary/compounds-l-o/#marmelosin), Beta-[sitosterol](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol)

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water (tablets); as is (liquid kashayam)
