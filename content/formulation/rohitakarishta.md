## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Bhaishajya Ratnavali and Charaka Samhita. [Rohitaka](/herb/rohitaka/) herb is classically prescribed for liver (Yakrit), blood (Rakta), and spleen (Pleeha) disorders. Also referenced in Sushruta Samhita.

## How does it work?

- [Hepatoprotective](/reference/hepatoprotective/) action attributed to betulinic acid, which may support liver function
- Cholagogue action increases bile flow, protecting liver and gallbladder function
- Classically used in spleen (Pleeha) disorders
- Synergistic action of [Trikatu](/herb/trikatu/) (pungent trio) with [Rohitaka](/herb/rohitaka/) enhances bioavailability and deepens tissue penetration

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Liver disorders (hepatomegaly, jaundice): traditionally used for these conditions. Studies of liver-protective activity have reported normalization of liver enzymes comparable to silymarin.
- Splenomegaly (enlarged spleen): traditionally used for this condition. Clinical studies have reported a reduction in spleen size.
- Ascites and edema: traditionally used for these conditions.
- Anemia: traditionally used for this condition.

## Recent safety updates

- Contains 5-10% self-generated alcohol. Generally safe under physician supervision. Tecomella undulata is an endangered species in some regions, raising sustainability concerns.
- Not recommended during pregnancy. May interact with hepatically metabolized drugs. Patients with severe liver disease should use only under strict medical monitoring.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Arishta/Asava (fermented liquid), Syrup (modern adaptation)

**Standard Dosage:** 15-30ml with equal water for hepatic conditions

**Bioavailability:** Self-generated alcohol enhances extraction and absorption of both water-soluble and fat-soluble compounds; fermentation produces bioactive metabolites not present in raw herbs

**Optimal Timing:** After meals, twice daily

**Shelf Life:** Indefinite per classical texts; commercially labeled 5-10 years. Improves with age.

**Storage:** Room temperature, tightly sealed, away from direct sunlight. Do not refrigerate.

**Marker Compounds:** Rohitukine, [Lapachol](/glossary/compounds-g-l/#lapachol)

**Quality Parameters:** Alcohol content 5-12% v/v, pH 3.0-4.5, specific gravity 1.01-1.08, total solids 15-25%, marker: Rohitukine, Lapachol

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Equal quantity of water after meals

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: [Rohitaka](/herb/rohitaka/) (Tecomella undulata), [Dhataki](/herb/dhataki/); fermentation creates unique metabolite profile with enhanced therapeutic activity
