## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Chikitsa Sthana ([Rasayana](/reference/rasayana/) Adhyaya - two formulations described); Rasatarangini by Govind Das (16th century CE); Bhavaprakasha Nighantu; classified under Medhya Rasayana

## How does it work?

- Bacosides (from [Brahmi](/herb/brahmi/)) modulate cholinergic neurotransmission, enhancing synaptic plasticity and memory consolidation
- [Withanolides](/glossary/compounds-t-z/#withanolides) (from [Ashwagandha](/herb/ashwagandha/)) provide [neuroprotective](/reference/neuroprotective/) action through reduced oxidative markers in hippocampus
- [Guduchi](/herb/guduchi/) fractions upregulate IL-2 production, enhancing immune surveillance and reducing neuroinflammation
- Combined [antioxidant](/reference/antioxidant/) enzyme activity (SOD, catalase) increased by [Brahmi](/herb/brahmi/) and [Ashwagandha](/herb/ashwagandha/) extracts protects against age-related neuronal damage
- Anti-acetylcholinesterase activity preserves acetylcholine levels in brain, supporting cognitive function in aging
- Classical rasayana doctrine describes multi-herb Rasayanas as promoting Ojas (vital essence) and longevity through rejuvenation of all Dhatus

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Medhya (cognitive enhancement/memory): traditionally used for memory and cognition. Clinical studies have reported improvement in memory recall and MMSE scores.
- Vayasthapana (anti-aging/longevity): traditionally used for longevity and healthy aging. Studies have reported antioxidant and neuroprotective activity in relation to age-related neural damage.
- Smriti Vriddhi (memory improvement): traditionally used for memory improvement. Animal studies have reported memory restorative activity.
- Ojas Vardhana (vital energy): traditionally used to build vital energy. Laboratory studies have reported [immunomodulatory](/reference/immunomodulatory/) activity, including IL-2 upregulation, and [adaptogenic](/reference/adaptogenic/) effects.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Avaleha/Rasayana (semi-solid confection), Capsules (modern), Granules (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 12 g twice daily on empty stomach with milk, as given in the Ayurvedic Formulary of India, Part I, entry 3:20. The formulary's anupana: water, milk. The formulary attributes this formula to Astangahrdaya, Uttarasthana, Adhyaya 39; 15-19½.

**Optimal Timing:** Early morning on empty stomach and/or before bedtime, for long-term Rasayana therapy (minimum 3-6 months)

**Storage:** Store in wide-mouth glass or food-grade jars with airtight lids in a cool, dry place. Temperature not exceeding 25°C. Protect from moisture and light.

**Marker Compounds:** Bacoside A, Bacoside B, Piperine, Gallic acid, Ascorbic acid, Beta-asarone (from [Vacha](/herb/vacha/), within safe limits), Withanolide A

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm milk (classically prescribed)
