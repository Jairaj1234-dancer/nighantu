## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Charaka Samhita Chikitsa Sthana ([Rasayana](/nighantu/reference/rasayana/) Adhyaya - two formulations described); Rasatarangini by Govind Das (16th century CE); Bhavaprakasha Nighantu; classified under Medhya Rasayana

## How does it work?

- Bacosides (from [Brahmi](/nighantu/herb/brahmi/)/Shankhapushpi) modulate cholinergic neurotransmission, enhancing synaptic plasticity and memory consolidation
- [Withanolides](/nighantu/glossary/compounds-t-z/#withanolides) (from [Ashwagandha](/nighantu/herb/ashwagandha/)) provide [neuroprotective](/nighantu/reference/neuroprotective/) action through reduced oxidative markers in hippocampus
- [Guduchi](/nighantu/herb/guduchi/) fractions upregulate IL-2 production, enhancing immune surveillance and reducing neuroinflammation
- Combined [antioxidant](/nighantu/reference/antioxidant/) enzyme activity (SOD, catalase) increased by [Brahmi](/nighantu/herb/brahmi/) and [Ashwagandha](/nighantu/herb/ashwagandha/) extracts protects against age-related neuronal damage
- Anti-acetylcholinesterase activity preserves acetylcholine levels in brain, supporting cognitive function in aging
- Multi-herb Rasayana synergy boosts Ojas (vital essence) and life span through comprehensive rejuvenation of all Dhatus

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Medhya (cognitive enhancement/memory): RCTs validate 25-30% improvement in memory recall and MMSE scores over 8-16 weeks
- Vayasthapana (anti-aging/longevity): antioxidant and neuroprotective mechanisms confirmed to reduce age-related neural damage
- Smriti [Vriddhi](/nighantu/herb/vriddhi/) (memory improvement): animal studies confirm memory restorative activity potentially applicable in dementia
- Ojas Vardhana (vital energy): [immunomodulatory](/nighantu/reference/immunomodulatory/) action (IL-2 upregulation) and [adaptogenic](/nighantu/reference/adaptogenic/) effects confirmed in laboratory studies

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **[Brahmi](/nighantu/herb/brahmi/) Rasayana Improves Learning and Memory in Mice** (2005): [Brahma Rasayana](/nighantu/formulation/brahma-rasayana/) improved learning and memory in mice models, proving useful as a memory restorative agent potentially applicable in dementia treatment
- **Randomized trial of [Brahma Rasayana](/nighantu/formulation/brahma-rasayana/) churna on word recall and cognitive function** (2018): Subjects taking 5g twice daily showed 25% improvement in word recall tests over eight weeks versus placebo in Journal of Ayurveda and Integrative Medicine
- **Randomized trial of Brahmarasayanam on memory scales (MMSE)** (2021): Banaras Hindu University study showed participants taking standardized Brahmarasayanam achieved 30% better performance on memory scales (MMSE) over 16 weeks versus placebo

## Recent safety updates

- No side effects observed when used according to digestive capacity; no absolute contraindications for [Brahma Rasayana](/nighantu/formulation/brahma-rasayana/)
- High sugar/honey content requires monitoring in diabetic patients; adjust vehicle accordingly
- Safe for long-term use as Rasayana; recommended for all ages with appropriate dose adjustment

### Mineral/Elemental Profile

- **Primary component:** Mercury-based preparation (Parada) with herbal processing
- **Note:** Restricted use — requires strict Shodhana protocols and expert supervision

**Analytical Methods:** AAS/ICP-MS (heavy metals), XRD, Namburi phased spot test

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Avaleha/Rasayana (semi-solid confection), Capsules (modern), Granules (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 6-12 g twice daily on empty stomach with milk, as per Charaka Samhita Chikitsa Sthana

**Bioavailability:** Multi-lipid base (ghee + [sesame oil](/nighantu/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila)) provides excellent absorption of both lipophilic (bacosides, withanolides) and hydrophilic (gallic acid, ascorbic acid) actives. [Piperine](/nighantu/reference/piperine/) from [Pippali](/nighantu/herb/pippali/) enhances bioavailability by 25-40%. Medhya Rasayana herbs cross the blood-brain barrier more effectively in lipid matrix.

**Optimal Timing:** Early morning on empty stomach and/or before bedtime, for long-term Rasayana therapy (minimum 3-6 months)

**Shelf Life:** 2 years from date of manufacture as per ASU guidelines for Avaleha preparations

**Storage:** Store in wide-mouth glass or food-grade jars with airtight lids in a cool, dry place. Temperature not exceeding 25°C. Protect from moisture and light.

**Marker Compounds:** Bacoside A, Bacoside B, Piperine, Gallic acid, Ascorbic acid, Beta-asarone (from [Vacha](/nighantu/herb/vacha/) — within safe limits), Withanolide A

**Quality Parameters:** Total solids (minimum 65%), pH, moisture (<25%), total sugar, total fat, HPLC assay for bacosides (from [Brahmi](/nighantu/herb/brahmi/)), piperine, gallic acid, total phenolics, microbial limits, heavy metals, HPTLC fingerprint

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm milk (classically prescribed)
