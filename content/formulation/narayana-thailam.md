## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Bhaishajya Ratnavali (Vatavyadhi Chikitsa, verses 140-150) by Govind Das Sen. The foundational Narayana formulation containing 33 herbs (the 'Maha' version adds Ashtavarga to reach 50+ herbs). Named after Lord Narayana (Vishnu) for its protective and restorative properties.

## How does it work?

- [Sesame oil](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila) lipid medium serves as a superior transdermal delivery vehicle for lipophilic [saponins](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/saponins/), [lignans](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-g-l/#lignans), and phenolics, achieving therapeutic tissue concentrations at musculoskeletal sites
- [Dashamoola](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/dashamoola/)'s combined action of 10 root extracts provides broad-spectrum anti-inflammatory activity through simultaneous inhibition of multiple inflammatory pathways (COX, LOX, NF-kB)
- [Tagara](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/tagara/)'s [valepotriates](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-t-z/#valepotriates) and [Jatamansi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/jatamansi/)'s nardostachin provide dual nervine sedative and [antispasmodic](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#antispasmodic) action, addressing both the pain and spasm components of Vata disorders
- [Ashwagandha](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ashwagandha/)'s [withanolides](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-t-z/#withanolides) and [Bala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/bala/)'s [ephedrine](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#ephedrine) [alkaloids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids) synergistically strengthen neuromuscular tissue while providing [analgesic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/analgesic/) effects

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Vatavyadhi (80+ Vata-related conditions) including paralysis, tremors, and muscular atrophy - supported by multiple observational studies showing significant improvement in neuromuscular function
- Lower back pain and lumbar spasm - validated by BHU pilot trial (2019) showing 60% pain improvement with nightly oleation and Swedana over 4 weeks
- Osteoarthritis stiffness and swelling - validated by observational studies confirming significant symptom reduction after 21 days of twice-daily Abhyanga treatment

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **Observational study of Narayana Tailam Abhyanga in osteoarthritis** (2018): Classical sources and modern observational studies note significant reduction in stiffness and swelling in osteoarthritis patients after 21 days of twice-daily Abhyanga with Narayana Tailam.
- **Pilot trial of Narayana Tailam in lower back spasms at BHU** (2019): In a pilot trial at Banaras Hindu University, subjects with lower back spasms reported 60% improvement in pain scores after four weeks of nightly oleation and steam fomentation (Swedana) using Narayana Tailam.
- **Transdermal delivery mechanism of Ayurvedic medicated oils including Narayana Tailam** (2020): The [sesame oil](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila) lipid medium facilitates transdermal delivery of lipophilic phytoconstituents like saponins, lignans, and phenolics, which exert anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and [antioxidant](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antioxidant/) effects at musculoskeletal target sites.

## Recent safety updates

- Generally safe for external application (Abhyanga, Basti, Nasya). Balanced formulation suitable for regular use. Mild skin warmth is normal after application. Patch test recommended for sensitive individuals. No significant drug interactions for external use reported.
- Internal administration (Basti, oral use) should only be under Ayurvedic practitioner supervision. Not recommended during acute Pitta flare-ups or fever. Caution in individuals on anticoagulant or anti-inflammatory medications. The Maha Narayana version is more potent but also heavier and less suitable for daily use.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Thailam (medicated [sesame oil](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila)), External application

**Standard Dosage:** External massage and Basti: 30-60ml

**Bioavailability:** Oil base ([sesame oil](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila)) enhances dermal absorption of lipophilic active compounds; for internal thailams, oil base enhances absorption of fat-soluble actives

**Optimal Timing:** External: before bath or at bedtime. Internal: before meals

**Shelf Life:** 3 years (medicated oils per ASU guidelines)

**Storage:** Airtight amber glass/HDPE container, room temperature, protected from light

**Marker Compounds:** Shatavarin, Withanolides

**Quality Parameters:** Acid value <5, saponification value per base oil specs, refractive index, moisture NMT 0.5%, specific gravity, marker: Shatavarin, Withanolides

**Vehicle (Anupana):** External: massage into affected area. Internal: with warm water/milk

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: [Shatavari](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/shatavari/), [Ashwagandha](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ashwagandha/), [Dashamoola](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/dashamoola/); oil base acts as lipid carrier for enhanced tissue penetration
