## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sahasrayogam (Taila Prakarana); also referenced in Ashtanga Hridayam. A signature Kerala Ayurvedic formulation specifically designed for lower body Vata disorders.

## How does it work?

- [Catechins](/glossary/compounds-c-d/#catechins) and [tannins](/reference/tannins/) from Sahachara are proposed to inhibit prostaglandin synthesis
- Curcuminoids (from [turmeric](/herb/turmeric/) component) are proposed to act as antioxidants and inhibit NF-kB inflammatory pathways
- [Manjistha](/herb/manjistha/)'s [anthraquinones](/glossary/compounds-1-a/#anthraquinones) ([purpurin](/glossary/compounds-o-q/#purpurin)) provide blood-purifying action and mild analgesia, improving microcirculation in the affected lower extremities
- [Gokshura](/herb/gokshura/) and Shalaparni are traditionally used to support muscle function, and [diuretic](/reference/diuretic/) effects are attributed to them

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

*These summaries come from the source notes, which do not name the studies behind them.*

- Gridhrasi (sciatica): traditionally used for sciatic pain. Clinical studies have reported reduced sciatic pain and disability scores with Kati Basti application.
- Lower back pain and chronic lumbar conditions: traditionally used for these conditions.
- Peripheral vascular insufficiency and lower limb weakness due to Vata: traditionally used for these conditions.

## Recent safety updates

- Generally well-tolerated for external application. Mild skin warming is normal. Patch test recommended for sensitive skin. Not recommended for application over open wounds or acute inflammatory skin conditions.
- Internal use should be under Ayurvedic practitioner supervision. No significant drug interactions reported for external use. Caution advised in individuals with severe varicose veins - gentle application preferred over vigorous massage.

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** Thailam (medicated [sesame oil](/glossary/concepts-m-y/#tila-taila)), External application

**Standard Dosage:** 6 to 12 g, as given in the Ayurvedic Formulary of India, Part I, entry 8:59. External massage for Vata/neurological conditions. The formulary attributes this formula to Astangahrdaya, Cikitsasthana, Adhyaya 21; 66 - 67½.

**Optimal Timing:** External: before bath or at bedtime. Internal: before meals

**Storage:** Airtight amber glass/HDPE container, room temperature, protected from light

**Marker Compounds:** [Sesamin](/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sesamin), [Lupeol](/reference/lupeol/)

**Vehicle (Anupana):** External: massage into affected area. Internal: with warm water/milk

### Synergistic Combinations

- Key herbs: Sahachara (Strobilanthes), [Devadaru](/herb/devadaru/), [Eranda](/herb/eranda/); oil base acts as lipid carrier for enhanced tissue penetration
