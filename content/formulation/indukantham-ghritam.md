## Key Ingredients

- Dashamula (group of ten roots)
- [Trikatu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/trikatu/) (Shunthi, Maricha, [Pippali](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/pippali/))
- [Triphala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/triphala/) ([Haritaki](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/haritaki/), Vibhitaki, Amalaki)
- Saptaphala (group of seven fruits)
- Chitraka mula (Plumbago zeylanica)
- Shunthi ([Zingiber](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/zingiber/) officinale / [ginger](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ginger/))
- [Yavakshara](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/yavakshara/) (alkali of barley)
- Panchakola (five pungent herbs)
- [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) (clarified cow butter)

## Where is it described in the classical texts?

Sahasrayogam (ancient classical Ayurvedic text from Kerala tradition); mentioned in traditional Malayalam Ayurvedic compendiums

## How does it work?

- Dashamula provides anti-inflammatory and [analgesic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/analgesic/) action, reducing abdominal inflammation and pain
- [Trikatu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/trikatu/) stimulates Agni (digestive fire) through [piperine](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/piperine/)-mediated enhancement of digestive enzyme secretion
- Chitraka ([plumbagin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-o-q/#plumbagin)) acts as a potent appetizer and digestive stimulant, reducing fat accumulation and improving gut motility
- [Yavakshara](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/yavakshara/) provides antacid and [diuretic](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/diuretic/) properties, managing urinary and abdominal disorders
- [Immunomodulatory](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/immunomodulatory/) action of combined herbs supports immune function - demonstrated as adjuvant to chemotherapy

## Which traditional uses are supported by research?

- Gulma and Udara Roga (abdominal tumors/diseases): validated through gastroprotective and anti-inflammatory studies of ingredients
- Agnimandya (digestive weakness): [Trikatu](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/trikatu/) and Panchakola ingredients confirmed to enhance digestive enzyme activity
- Immunomodulation: demonstrated as adjuvant to cancer chemotherapy via immune stimulation in published research
- Amlapitta (acid peptic disorders): individual ingredient screening validates antacid and mucosal protective mechanisms

## What do recent clinical trials show?

- **Indukanta Ghritam - A Review** (2021): Comprehensive review established gastroprotective, [antioxidant](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/antioxidant/), and immunomodulatory activities of individual ingredients, proposing modes of action in acid peptic disorders
- **A polyherbal ayurvedic drug - Indukantha Ghritha as an adjuvant to cancer chemotherapy via immunomodulation** (2008): Indukantha Ghritha demonstrated potential as an adjuvant to cancer chemotherapy through immunomodulatory action, supporting immune function during cytotoxic treatment
- **Screening of activities of individual ingredients of Indukanta Ghritam for acid peptic disorders** (2021): Individual herb activities validated gastroprotective mechanisms including acid neutralization, mucosal protection, and digestive stimulation

## Recent safety updates

- Considerably safe for most individuals in recommended dosage under professional supervision; no adverse effects reported in clinical use
- Safety for pregnant women and breastfeeding women has not been specifically studied - effects are unknown; use only under practitioner guidance
- May cause mild digestive discomfort in high doses; avoid in individuals with severe gastric ulceration without supervision

## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee), Soft gelatin capsules (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 6-12 g twice daily on empty stomach, as per Sahasrayogam/Ashtanga Hridayam

**Bioavailability:** Ghee matrix enhances absorption of [Dashamoola](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/dashamoola/) [triterpenes](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-t-z/#triterpenes) and [Bala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/bala/) [alkaloids](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-1-a/#alkaloids). Lipid vehicle facilitates CNS penetration for Vata-pacifying action. Superior bioavailability compared to kashayam form for lipophilic compounds.

**Optimal Timing:** On empty stomach, morning and evening, for Gulma (abdominal tumors/masses), Udara roga, and Vata disorders

**Shelf Life:** 2 years from date of manufacture as per ASU guidelines for [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/)

**Storage:** Store in airtight glass or tin containers in a cool, dry place. Temperature not exceeding 25°C. Protect from light.

**Marker Compounds:** [Lupeol](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/lupeol/), [Marmelosin](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-l-o/#marmelosin), [Ephedrine](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#ephedrine) (from [Bala](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/bala/)), 6-[Gingerol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-d-g/#gingerol), Beta-[sitosterol](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-q-t/#sitosterol), [Cedrene](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/compounds-c-d/#cedrene) (from [Devadaru](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/devadaru/))

**Quality Parameters:** Acid value (<6), peroxide value (<10), saponification value, refractive index, moisture (<0.5%), rancidity test negative, HPTLC fingerprint for [Dashamoola](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/dashamoola/) and Bala markers, microbial limits, heavy metals

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water or warm milk
