## Dosage forms and preparation

**Dosage Forms:** [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/) (medicated ghee), Soft gelatin capsules (modern)

**Standard Dosage:** 6-12 g twice daily on empty stomach with warm water/milk, as per Ashtanga Hridayam/AFI

**Bioavailability:** Ghee base provides optimal vehicle for 30+ herbs with diverse solubility profiles. Lipid matrix enhances BBB penetration of Medhya ([nootropic](/ayurveda-atlas/glossary/pharmacology/#nootropic)) compounds. Multi-compound synergy provides broad-spectrum activity with individual compound bioavailability enhanced by ghee matrix.

**Optimal Timing:** On empty stomach, morning and evening. For psychiatric conditions: continuous use for 3-6 months minimum.

**Shelf Life:** 2 years from date of manufacture as per ASU guidelines for [Ghrita](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/ghrita/)

**Storage:** Store in airtight glass or tin containers in a cool, dry place. Temperature not exceeding 25°C. Protect from light.

**Marker Compounds:** Bacoside A (from [Brahmi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/brahmi/)), Alpha-santalol, Beta-asarone (from [Vacha](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/vacha/), within limits), [Piperine](/ayurveda-atlas/reference/piperine/), Gallic acid, Ursolic acid (from [Sariva](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/sariva/))

**Quality Parameters:** Acid value (<6), peroxide value (<10), saponification value, refractive index, moisture (<0.5%), rancidity test negative, bacoside A (from [Brahmi](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/brahmi/)), alpha-santalol (from [Chandana](/ayurveda-atlas/herb/chandana/)), HPTLC multi-marker fingerprint, microbial limits, heavy metals

**Vehicle (Anupana):** Warm water or warm milk
